Best times to snowshoe or cross country ski are January-February. Groomed trails when snow allows! Snow level was low on our visit. Best part was on the John Wayne Trail, very scenic bridges and icicles. Bathrooms are heated and have running water! Elevation is around 2200 feet. 4-5 miles of round trip trails.
