Jordan Peterson gives advice for very attractive men. It's pretty clear anthropologically that sexual choice
tends towards a Pareto distribution, especially for men. So most men have very little selection at their disposal and a small number of very attractive men have excess opportunities. The question is what should that small number of men do? Should they choose one woman and have a relationship or should they capitalize on their opportunities? Jordan Peterson argues they should be attractive to many women but only choose one and have a relationship with them. The problem with treating other people as casual sexual partners is that you also treat yourself that way simultaneously.
