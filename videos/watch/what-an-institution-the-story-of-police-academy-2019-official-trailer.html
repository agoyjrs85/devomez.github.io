Brought to you by Cult Screenings UK ltd, Producer Adam F. Goldberg and Executive Producer Paul Maslansky come this brand new feature length independent documentary on the legacy of Police Academy and its sequels.

The Police Academy movies were a mainstay in the comedy genre of the 1980s and early 1990s with the first film being released in 1984 and spawning 6 sequels.

Directed by Gary Smart, 'What An Institution! The Story of Police Academy' features never-before-seen video and photos as well as interviews with over 30 members of the cast and crew of Police Academy including, Michael Winslow, Marion Ramsey, Leslie Easterbrook, Lance Kinsey, Bobcat Goldthwait, G.W. Bailey, Tim Kazurinsky, Matt McCoy and many more...
