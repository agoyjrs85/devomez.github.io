(24 Jan 2019) Secretary of State Mike Pompeo called on members of the Organization of American States to recognize National Assembly President Juan Guaido as the interim president of Venezuela.

At a meeting of the OAS in Washington, Pompeo said "the time for debate is done. The regime of former President Nicolas Maduro is illegitimate."

Pompeo pledged $20 million in U.S. humanitarian assistance to Venezuela and called on Venezuelan security forces to protect Guaido's physical safety.

Following his remarks, some of the diplomats in the chamber applauded, while a protester dressed in pink was ejected after she held up a sign that read "OAS: Don't Support a Coup in Venezuela."

On Wednesday, U.S. President Donald Trump recognized Guaido as Venezuela's interim president, leading President Nicolas Maduro to demand that all U.S. diplomats leave the country in 72 hours.

Pompeo told the diplomats to stay because the U.S. doesn't recognize Maduro.
