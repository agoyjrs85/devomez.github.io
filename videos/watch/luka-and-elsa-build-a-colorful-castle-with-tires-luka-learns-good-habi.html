About WOA Luka Channel:
The channel mainly focuses on daily life of a clay character named Luka and his family including his parents, his little sister Elsa and little baby Jack. Together, they go through struggles everyday to learn many lessons about good behaviors.
All of our contents are designed with the purpose of children's education based on their ages and development. Not just only for children’s self education, parents can also learn the good way to teach their children. To a brightened young generation, we hope our contents can convey useful messages to the community.
With WOA LUKA team, our mission is to make learning a fun experience for kids aged 3 – 5 by creating 3D Stop motion animations. Children get to learn good habits, safety knowledge, letters, colors, numbers and more with our colorful characters! WOA LUKA team want a better future, to helps children to:
❤️ Learn letters, numbers, animal sounds, colors, and more with humility, creativity and curiosity
🧡 Love and take care of animals through communication
💛 Explore the world and learn about children surroundings with compassion and gratitude
💚 Respect and encourage to help others in rough times
💙 Recognize good behaviors and essential life skills: Problem Solving, Interpersonal Relationship, Empathy, Positive thinking, Unification
💜 Providing parents with an opportunity to practice teamwork, perseverance, self-control while teaching and playing with their children as they watch together.
