This video tutorial I will show you how to create a reflection in Photoshop CC. You can create a realistic looking reflection on objects on black or white glossy surfaces. This Photoshop effect is great for adding to your products.

Sometimes objects may be curved at the bottom like a bottle, cup, or can and may need a bit of editing. I will be using the Warp tool which can create a correct mirror reflection.
