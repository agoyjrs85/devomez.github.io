Greetings from Manju Mittal cookery house ! We really hope that you and your family are well and taking all precautions during the lockdown. Many of our subscribers requested for an easy Kadha recipe that will help boost their immunity and help them fight virus and diseases. In this video recipe by Manju Mittal, you will learn to make a very healthy Kadha that will boost our immunity and is great for your overall health. It can be had anytime of the year and is especially good for changing weather when we need our immunity to be strong. It can be easily prepared from the ingredients available at home. It can also be a simple substitute for your green tea. Do try this recipe and share it with your friends and family. Again, we will pray for your good health and hope we all overcome this soon.
