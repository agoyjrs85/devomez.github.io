Microsoft are joining the barrage of companies making ARM CPUs, leading to a lot of speculation about what it could be used for, primarily the Xbox next gen console follow up after the Xbox Series X. This comes to us thanks to new news on the Intel 11 CPUs, which includes specs across all the SKUs for the next gen processors from Intel.
