Microsoft are joining the barrage of companies making ARM CPUs, leading to a lot of speculation about what it could be used for, primarily the Xbox next gen console follow up after the Xbox Series X. This would be a big departure for console processors, and could be another ripple in a big change in overall PC gaming as well as console gaming.

We have some new info on RDNA 2 APUs, which could also lead to a big change in gaming. These powerful mobile graphics cards could make other low power GPUs a much less viable.

There's also an update on the Intel 11th gen CPUs, and Rocket Lake will not be the only processor in the 11th gen CPU lineup. This comes to us thanks to new news on the Intel 11 CPUs, which includes specs across all the SKUs for the next gen processors from Intel. But how will 11th gen stand up against AMD's Ryzen dominance?
