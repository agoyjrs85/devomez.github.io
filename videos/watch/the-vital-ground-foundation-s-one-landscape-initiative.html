Vital Ground is acting boldly to conserve North America’s richest and most intact wildlife communities. One Landscape is our push to protect key habitat and knit together a permanently connected landscape to ensure the long-term survival of grizzly bears and the many native species that share their range in the Northern Rockies and Inland Northwest.


You can join a growing coalition of visionary supporters who care about the long-term health of this region and all the creatures that call it home. You will lead the charge to protect and connect this place for future generations. Please support One Landscape and save vital habitat today!
