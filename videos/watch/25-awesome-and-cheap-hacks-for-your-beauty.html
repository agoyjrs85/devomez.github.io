Try our cool beauty tricks we share today: if your belly doesn’t ready for the summer, try contouring makeup technique; get rid of flyaways using a toothbrush and hairspray; make a makeup remover at home from an old highlighter.

Try our natural recipes of teeth whitening. We use baking soda and lemon juice activated carbon and water of even kiwi and cucumber! If you tried all the ways to get rid of bad breath and there is no result it’s time to see a doctor.
