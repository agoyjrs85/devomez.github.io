MUST KNOW BEAUTY TIPS

Try our cool beauty tricks we share today: if your belly doesn’t ready for the summer, try contouring makeup technique; get rid of flyaways using a toothbrush and hairspray; make a makeup remover at home from an old highlighter.
Having healthy teeth requires a lot of effort and is a vital part of everyday routine. Our helpful lifehacks will help you to prevent different problems.
Try our natural recipes of teeth whitening. We use baking soda and lemon juice activated carbon and water of even kiwi and cucumber! Here is a simple recipe: crush the strawberry and mix it with toothpaste and spread the mixture on your teeth using a toothbrush. Also, you can crush kiwis and cucumber and use a toothbrush to clean your teeth. Repeat for 7 days. One more cool hack on how to quickly whiten your teeth. Rub the inside of a banana peel on your teeth. Besides, this hack will save you a lot of money.
Bad breath might be a sign of health problems like gastrointestinal problems, liver problems, problems with lungs, throat infection and more. There are few ways to check if you have an unpleasant smell in your mouth. Check our video and find how to test your breath using a spoon or a glass. Moreover, we share reasons of unpleasant smell: the wrong toothbrush; you don’t brush your teeth after a meal; incorrect diet and more. If you tried all the ways to get rid of bad breath and there is no result it’s time to see a doctor.
Don’t forget to eat fresh crunchy vegetables like carrots, fruits that contain acids – oranges, pineapples, strawberries, nuts to feed the bone tissue. Always drink water as water balance is highly important and also it washes away food and strengthens the enamel.
