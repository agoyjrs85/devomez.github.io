Camila (Maja Salvador) puts a stop to the tense standoff between Vito (Geoff Eigenmann) and Fabio (Miko Raval) when she realizes something amiss. Despite this, he tirelessly offers his help in hopes of reaching out to his former lover that he long thought dead. With Camila's secret out, the foundations of Emma's family slowly start to crumble. Elsewhere, Luciano (Cris Villanueva) begins to plant the seeds of his new plot.

Upon seeing a letter from Emma, Manay Ichu storms to the Dela Torres’ mansion and points the finger at Luciano (Cris Villanueva). The alleged suspect, on the other hand, receives criticisms from his family following Manay Ichu’s accusations. Stumbling upon suspicious evidence against him, Luciano tries to find a way to prove his innocence. Meanwhile, Camila (Maja Salvador) plots the downfall of her enemies with the help of a beloved.
