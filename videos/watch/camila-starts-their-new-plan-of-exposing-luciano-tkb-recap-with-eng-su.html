Camila (Maja Salvador) puts a stop to the tense standoff between Vito (Geoff Eigenmann) and Fabio (Miko Raval) when she realizes something amiss. The alleged suspect, on the other hand, receives criticisms from his family following Manay Ichu’s accusations.
