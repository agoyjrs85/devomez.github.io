In this video Brad McCutchen Principal of the Canadian College of Osteopathy discusses how Osteopathic Manual Practitioners Treats Headaches.

Many people suffer from headaches for many reasons:

- Seasonal allergies
- De-hydration
- Muscle imbalance
- Alignment issues
- Injury

A Osteopathic Manual Practitioner will assess you thoroughly to see what the imbalance is in your body. Then the Osteopathic Manual Practitioner will treat you to help your body get back into balance.

This can take several treatments depending on the imbalance.

The osteopathic treatment is focused on aligning the body and increasing vitality to the area that was imbalanced. This will help elevate pressure, inflammation and tension in the area.
