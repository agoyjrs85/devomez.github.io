STANLEY & HIS FRIENDS SERIES:
Stanley stops by at Chef's to find out what's cooking. The Chef is making a lunch of varying temperature items. Stanley and the Chef talk about Hot, Cold, Warm and Cool.

Some friends have asked to show them to their kids so I am opening up the links.

These are amateur videos as I am still learning to record, edit and just work with them. Am open to feedback to help make better though.
