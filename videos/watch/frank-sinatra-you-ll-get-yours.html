Few would argue with Frank Sinatra’s pole position in popular music. From the dark days of World War II to the fall of the Berlin Wall, Sinatra’s voice has always been there as the soundtrack to our lives. That omission is rectified here, with the release of The Capitol Singles Collection - 80 tracks bookending a decade of Sinatra hits, covering the period 1953-1962.
