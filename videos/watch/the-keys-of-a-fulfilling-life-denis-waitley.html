David Laroche is interviewing Denis Waitley, an american best-selling author in high performance and psychology. Denis is very inspiring, things that he drew in his experience. He showed all the trigger and the struggles he had before becoming a famous and successful writer. Denis recommends that nobody has something to prove and to succeed the state of mind is the most important.
