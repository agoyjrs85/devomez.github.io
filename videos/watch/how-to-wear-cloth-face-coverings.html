We asked some of our Nest Partnership nurses to share some information on what kind of cloth face covering you should use and how to wear it properly.

Remember, cloth face coverings are just one tool in our toolkit for preventing the spread of COVID-19. You should also stay home as much as possible, wash your hands with soap and water often, and stay 6 feet away from others if you have to go out.

You can find more information about face coverings on our website here: https://www.springfieldmo.gov/5140/Masks-and-Face-Coverings
