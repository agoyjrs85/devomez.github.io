Sometimes the relationships in our lives may seem superficial because we tend to keep everything so private The connections we make with strangers can unveil what we hide on the inside and empower us to better understand ourselves. Riya Narayan, a 16yr old student will captivate you with her talk: "Strangers: Your expected therapist"!

SPEAKER BIO:
