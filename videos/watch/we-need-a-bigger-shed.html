Werner Farms, established in 1960, is a family farm operation based in rural Hanna, IN. The YouTube channel and social media pages are run by Keith Werner, the third generation of Werner Farms.

The farm has no-tilled since the early 1980s and continues to increase acres of cover crops year to year. Besides the farm operation, Werner Farms has sold Pioneer Seed since the 1980s.
