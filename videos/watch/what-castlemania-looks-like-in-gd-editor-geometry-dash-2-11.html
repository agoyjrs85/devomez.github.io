Tower Optimisation:
The background basically follows the player as it goes up and down, problem is I couldn't just make it simply follow using the follow trigger as it wouldn't look right, it would look like a floaty background, so I had to make it move in 10 blocks increment! For that I used collision boxes that would follow the player, and each 10 block they went through they'd collide with a box that would move the whole contraption up or down and at the same time, the background. Writing this down makes me realize I should probably make a video on that and it'd be easier to understand, maybe one day... But basically using this contraption you can essentially make infinite backgrounds vertically without using an infinite amount of obj!
