Louis Be Mindful collection is very colorful and uniquely beautiful. I chose the headband because it is a must have to compliment and to complete my daily outfit in any occasions whether I go to a party, grocery shopping and or even attending special events.

(Courtesy of Louis Vuitton official website)

This exclusive collection of Louis Vuitton accessories and textiles gives our printed silks a second life thanks to the House’s eco-design approach, know-how and sustainability goals. Each product in the Be Mindful collection is unique, created based on the model of upcycling (the art of transforming reclaimed materials and products into new objects with greater utility or aesthetic value). At Louis Vuitton, we pay close attention to the lifespan of our creations. That is why the House is committed to preserving natural resources which give our products their beauty and quality by offering them a new life. Inspired by the LV headband from past seasons, the Be Mindful headband is a new addition to the Accessories collection. This unique item is crafted from pure silk that has been creatively upcycled to give pieces a second life. Its vibrant print is complemented by a Monogram canvas pipping trim.
