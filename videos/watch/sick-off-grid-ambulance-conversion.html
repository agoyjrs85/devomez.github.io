I met CJ in a supermarket in Spain and it was great to see his van conversion. His converted a Sprinter Ambulance has everything you need and is a good example of a fully kitted out conversion. he has a toilet, a shower, hot water, a diesel heater and a wood burning stove plus a double bed, a TV and a fridge. (Not that he tends to use it all - but it can be nice to have).

In this video, CJ shows us how he converted the van and key elements of his design. Oh, he has a cute dog too!
