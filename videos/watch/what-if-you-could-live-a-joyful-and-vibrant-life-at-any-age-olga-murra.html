A trek in the Himalayas thirty years ago transformed 90-year-old Olga Murray’s life. In 1990, she founded the Nepal Youth Foundation, an organization that provides education, medical care and freedom to impoverished Nepali children—45,000 to date. Olga believes that giving is the new getting, at any age. This is her remarkable story.



More information on http://www.tedxvienna.at

You can live a joyful, fulfilling life at any age. In 1984, approaching 60 and retirement, lawyer Olga Murray discovered a country and a cause that transformed her own life and the lives of 45,000 poor children in Nepal.
