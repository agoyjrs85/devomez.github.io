With so many skaters being forced to withdraw, we discuss what it means for the rest of the figure skating season and debate how we can qualify skaters to the Olympic Games. Meanwhile, the holidays are approaching and like Victor Petrenko, we are very excited. We break it all down on another edition of 'This and That'.
