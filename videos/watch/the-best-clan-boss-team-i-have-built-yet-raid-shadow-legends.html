Unkillable teams have been one of the areas of Raid Shadow Legends that have been growing and growing in variety and capability over the past 12months. DeadWood Jedi (https://www.deadwoodjedi.com/ ) was one of the Pioneers of this movement working with his team to build a calculator that shows how speed works and how it can interact with the Clan Boss.

A number of Champions have been involved but ask anyone and they would agree the best way to build this style of team is to have TWO Maneaters as the compositions enable you to ignore affinity and stuns!

This team was the brainchild of TheDragonsBeard, Bobo, and Optilink and maybe the best one yet!


A 2:1 ration unkillable team with two Maneaters, Seeker, Ma'Shalled, and a DPS champion. (I am using Dracomorph)
