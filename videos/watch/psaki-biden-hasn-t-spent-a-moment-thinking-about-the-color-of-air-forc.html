White House Press Secretary Jen Psaki said Friday, "I can confirm for you here the president has not spent a moment thinking about the color scheme on Air Force One." She was referring to a 2018 order from former President Donald Trump to change the plane's iconic blue and white color scheme. She made the remarks during a press briefing at the White House on January 22, 2021, in Washington, D.C.
