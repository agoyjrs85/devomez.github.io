Join Megan for a chat with Toni Randall as she tells us all about herself, her design collection and her recent tragedy in the Oregon wildfires.

If you're interested in supporting Toni and her community, please find the link to her go fund me page here. http://www.whimsyandgrace.com/ money will be used to help her re-establish her business and her home. With what is left, Toni would like to help the neighbor who helped to save her life, as well as others through her church.
