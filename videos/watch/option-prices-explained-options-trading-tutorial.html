Option prices can be very confusing at first because there are so many expiration cycles and strike prices, all with different prices.

In this video, you're going to learn how to understand options pricing intuitively, laying a solid foundation for you as an options trader.

Be sure to watch the video all the way through, as there are many animated/visual examples that will help demonstrate the key concepts:

1) Intrinsic and Extrinsic Value
2) Calculating and Interpreting Intrinsic Value
3) Extrinsic Value (Time Value) Explained & How to Interpret It
4) Factors that Influence the Amount of Extrinsic Value in Options
5) Three Options Pricing LAWS!
