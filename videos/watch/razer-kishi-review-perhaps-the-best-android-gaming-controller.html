Mobile gaming is huge, and so here’s the Razer Kishi to solve all your problems with finicky touch controls on your smartphone.

To be honest, we never thought we would like it as much as we did. The Kishi does honestly, make for a much more enjoyable gaming experience on the go, as well as Steam Link to your favourite PC titles.

But of course, there’s pros and cons, and we actually compare it to Razer’s own Junglecat. But even so, the Kishi might just be the best Android Gaming Controller when it comes to mobile gaming.
