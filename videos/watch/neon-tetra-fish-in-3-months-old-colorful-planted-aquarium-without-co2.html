This time, I add some neon tetras to this Colorful Planted Aquarium without CO2 Injection. The neon tetra was quarantined for 17 days. During the quarantine, they showed white spot symptom. The white spot can be ich or any other parasite with the same symptom. I can only speculated, but the treatment works on 9 out of 10 fish.

There was also sudden bacteria bloom that make me think the filter in this tank is inadequate to make the water clear. In the end, I changed the filter with a canister filter. Before that, I found a simple DIY water polisher filter that easy to make. The filter is in the previous video here:
https://youtu.be/Tddi335mU24

Information regarding the aquarium set up can be found here:
https://youtu.be/yihk3aPfgC4

This aquarium is using The Walstad Method with some adjustment so consider to learn about walstad method directly at Diana Walstad's web here:
https://dianawalstad.com/
