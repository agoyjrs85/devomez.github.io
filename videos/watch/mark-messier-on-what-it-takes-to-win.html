It’s ‘94 Rangers week, and in an EXCLUSIVE interview for Line Movement, Mark Messier joins Nick Kypreos for a wide-ranging conversation on what it takes to win a Stanley Cup.

Messier is one of the greatest winners in the history of sports, and definitely the National Hockey League. His unparalleled leadership skills and unwavering dedication is well documented, and he currently sits third on the all-time NHL points leaderboard. Six Stanley Cup championships, two Hart Memorial Trophies as the league’s Most Valuable Player, and is the only NHL captain to raise the cup for two different franchises – the Edmonton Oilers and the New York Rangers.

The guys get into Messier’s iconic Game 6 “guarantee” in route to the Rangers’ first Stanley Cup championship in fifty-four years.
