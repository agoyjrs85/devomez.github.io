Jazz up your wardrobe with this fun crocheted tunic. This tunic design can fit all sizes if you really examine it. The tunic is crocheted from top to bottom going across the body. So if you need to cover more, you simply continue. Essentially, you could grab a shirt that fits you and lay the panel over the front of the shirt to get the sizing that works for you. Easy peasy! You'll want one in several colors. Here's the free pattern: http://thecrochetcrowd.com/drop-stitch-tunic-crochet-pattern/
