Are you curious about the Spousal Open Work Permit? This type of work permit is available to the spouse or common-law partner of either: a student who is studying full-time at a Canadian college or university or a worker who is working full-time in Canada in a skilled occupation for 6 months or longer (NOC level 0, A, or B).
