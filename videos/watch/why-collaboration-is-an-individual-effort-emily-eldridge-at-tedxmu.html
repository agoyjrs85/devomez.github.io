Understanding personalities, people, and their individual needs is essential to collaboration and good teamwork. Indeed, Emily shows us that there is an "I" in "Team," and that the individual has a lot of power in an over-connected world.

Emily Eldridge serves as the CEO of Pure, the co-founder of The Agency Post and an alumna of the University of Missouri. She collaborates with a talented team to make communication agencies better at what they do, and understands how important the individuals are in bringing together those valuable services.
