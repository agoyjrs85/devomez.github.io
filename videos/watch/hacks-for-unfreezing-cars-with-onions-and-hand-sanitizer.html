With winter storms showing no sign of letting up, Inside Edition is sharing some hacks that will help you get through it, using items you might already have around the house. For frozen car door locks, grab some hand sanitizer and pump a little into the lock and put some on the key. Don’t use hot water, that could freeze your lock more.
