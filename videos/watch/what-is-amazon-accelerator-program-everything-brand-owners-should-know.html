Amazon Accelerator program isn’t something new, but many Amazon private label sellers may not be aware of the existence of the program and its benefits.

The Amazon Accelerator Program is an opportunity to boost your private label business. It is designed to give participants unique exposure which otherwise may take a long time to achieve. It also opens up the opportunity to gain more sales. One of the biggest draw to Amazon’s Accelerator Program is the potential for preferred placement of products on Amazon’s website.

Brands who sign up for the program enjoy some of the benefits Amazon’s own products receive. This program has different benefits that could help in boosting the growth of one of your brands. One of the benefits is that participants gain access to built-in social media and email marketing support. They also receive additional tools and metrics to measure their performance within the program.

Amazon’s Accelerator program also offers Amazon Vine Reviews and premium product page content, which are expensive brand tools, at no cost. The program also allows brands to test new products as an Amazon private-label brand and earn customer feedback faster.
