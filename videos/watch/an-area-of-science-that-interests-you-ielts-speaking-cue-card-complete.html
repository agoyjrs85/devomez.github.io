This video lesson answers the recent IELTS Speaking Cue Card about An Area Of Science That Interests You. Watch the entire video and learn how to create a story talking about one branch of science in Part 2. Also,
you'll learn how to answer Part 3 questions talking about the importance of science to people and the world. Study how the speaker discussed his answers in a more organized way.

Here's the cue card:

Describe an area of science that interests you

You should say:
What it is
When you knew it
How you learned it
And explain why you are interested

What influence can international cooperation in science bring about?
What’s the influence of science on human life?
What can individuals do for scientific research?
What’s the best invention in the past one hundred years?
