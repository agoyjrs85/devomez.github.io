About Reese:
Reese Oliveira is 14 years old and in eighth grade. She is the oldest of five children and loves to SING! She was a member of One Voice Children’s Choir (2014 – 2018) and recently began singing with Rise Up Children’s Choir. Reese loves ballet and has been training with Barlow Arts Conservatory for almost four years. Through ballet, she has had the opportunity to dance in Nutcracker, Coppelia, and as Mother Duck in Duckling. She is currently preparing to perform in Swan Lake and 12 Dancing Princesses at the Covey Center in Provo, Utah.

Reese also loves to act and is very active in the theater and film community. She recently portrayed Young Beth in the 2018 Little Women movie (with Lea Thompson and Lucas Grabeel), and is preparing to perform as Rapunzel in “Into the Woods Junior” at Hale Center Theater Orem. Past performance credits include Young Cosette in Les Miserables (HCTO), child #1 for Joseph and the Amazing Technicolor Dreamcoat (HCTO), Gracie Shinn in The Music Man (Hale Centre Theatre), and more. She has also portrayed Kira in Xanadu Jr., Annie in Annie, and Young Rapunzel in Tangled. Reese can be found in a few commercials and training videos throughout the intermountain region. She is represented by Talent Management Group (801-263-6940) in Salt Lake City.
