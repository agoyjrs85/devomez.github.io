This cold process soap making tutorial was part of a ‘World Peas’ collaboration started by Bee from Sourcery soaps, the idea being that one soap maker would make some soap dough worlds as embeds and send them as a gift to another soap maker. That second soap maker would make a soap with the embeds and then make some of their own and pass them on to another soap maker.
