With all of this uncertainty surrounding the current state of the world, it’s easy to feel like everything is spinning out of control. You’re not alone in that feeling. But David didn’t give into feelings of despair and hopelessness. First Samuel Chapter 30 is the setting for a lesson on encouraging ourselves.
