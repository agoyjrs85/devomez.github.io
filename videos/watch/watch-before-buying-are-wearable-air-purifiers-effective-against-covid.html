Air Ionizers
- Do not use air filters
- send negative ions to air surrounding the person and increase weight of particles therefore causing them to drop to the surfaces/ ground
- they can also repel other particles which are charged the same
- pollutants / pathogens will not be in the air BUT will be along the ground / surfaces
- it may prevent the inhalation of the virus BUT can still infect you if you do not clean your surroundings because the virus/ bacteria may survive along surfaces
