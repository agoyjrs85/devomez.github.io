In frame 6, Kyren Wilson plays ‒to quote Neal Foulds "one of the worst shots of his career" 11:30. After the frame, at 12:34, an anecdotal incident occurs involving Ronnie's watch, which had bleeped in frame 6 when Kyren missed the easy pot.

Although the standard in this match is not the highest ‒as Stephen Hendry says: "Both players tonight are just really struggling to get that cue ball under control" ‒it is always interesting to watch Ronnie and Kyren facing off.
