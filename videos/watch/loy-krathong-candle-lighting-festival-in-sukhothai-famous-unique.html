This is one of the famous festival in Thailand.It is the time to release a lotus-shape rafts with flower ,candle and incense stick on to the water to pay respect of the Goddess of Water.It is celebrated with several days.One of the main activity is a beauty contest known as Noppamas Beauty Contest.The other is Lantern Contest.It's one of the festival in Thailand that people really enjoy.
