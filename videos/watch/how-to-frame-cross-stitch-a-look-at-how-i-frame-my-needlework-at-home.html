This is an at home framing tutorial using readily available supplies from your local craft store. This is a budget friendly alternative to expensive, but beautiful, custom framing. This tutorial uses the pin method to mount the cross stitch piece. Supply list below.

Supplies:
Frame
Foam board
Batting
Spray Adhesive
Ruler
Marker
Sequin pins
Double sided mounting tape
Rotary Cutter or Scissors
Ring Hangers
Picture hanging wire
Needle nose pliers
Drill or screwdriver
Kraft paper
Patience

Cross stitch piece is Stacy Nash "Gathering the Greens" stitched on 40 ct Confederate Gray from Weeks Dye Works with the called for floss colors

Disclaimer: I am not a professional framer, this is intended as guidance and information only, there are many other ways to frame needlework, this is just what works for me right now! Enjoy and ask any questions below!
