ABOUT THE LESSON 📚
Let's see how well you know some advanced vocabulary words. I have a variety of questions for you. I'll give you a moment to think of your response and then tell you the answer. I tried to find words that were difficult but also may be used in everyday conversation depending on the context. Learning these words will help you better express your thoughts, opinions, and ideas.
