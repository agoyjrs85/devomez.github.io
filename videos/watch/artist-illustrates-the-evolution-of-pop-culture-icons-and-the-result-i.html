Ever wondered how some of our beloved characters have evolved over time?

With each passing year, many things change, and characters of popular culture do, too, get their characteristics modified from time to time.

With that idea in mind, an artist from Los Angeles - Jeff Victor - has depicted exactly that - evolutions of the most iconic pop culture characters from their earliest concepts to how you may recognize them today. The result is truly pleasing and unique, putting all that creative work behind infamous characters into a new perspective.
