Food Allergy 101: Prevent Tree Nut Allergies | Tree Nut Allergy Symptom

For some nuts are a great alternative snack, but for those with tree nut allergies, they can be dangerous. Mild tree nut allergy symptom includes stomach ache, runny nose itchy eyes, hives, or tingling of lips and tongue.

To prevent a reaction to tree nut allergies, avoid products with tree nuts. Tree nuts can be found in unexpected places like cereals, certain coffee drinks and even BBQ sauce.

Federal law requires tree nuts to be listed on product labels.
