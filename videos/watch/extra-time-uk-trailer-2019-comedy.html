Extra Time is a hilarious and heart-felt comedy described as THE FULL MONTY for the football pitch.

Troy takes the unusual, and controversial, decision to team up with a nearby refugee support centre, to recruit the new arrivals to save the team and take the community on a journey of change. But for some, it’s more of a change than they’re willing to take.
