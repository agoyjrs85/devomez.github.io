Extra Time is a hilarious and heart-felt comedy described as THE FULL MONTY for the football pitch.

Former football star and town local, Troy Carrington, is coaxed into rescuing the local footy team who have been told they will have to merge with a rival club or fold. Troy takes the unusual, and controversial, decision to team up with a nearby refugee support centre, to recruit the new arrivals to save the team and take the community on a journey of change. But for some, it’s more of a change than they’re willing to take.
