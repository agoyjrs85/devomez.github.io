It's a tutorial combining 'How to clear cache in Chrome' and How to clear cookies in Chrome' because the two are normally done together to achieve best results.

How To Clear Cookies In and Cache In Chrome:

1. Launch Google Chrome
2. Go to Settings
3. Click Advanced Settings
4. Click on Clear browsing data
5. Make sure cookies and Cache are selected
6. Cick Clear Data
