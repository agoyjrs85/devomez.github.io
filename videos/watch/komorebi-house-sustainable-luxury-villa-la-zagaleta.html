The architecture of Komorebi House in La Zagaleta has been developed by not only taking into account topographic and aesthetic aspects, but also by considering the natural resources of the extraordinary environment on which it is built, so as to reduce energy consumption: solar panels, charging point for an electric car, home automation and radiant flooring to foster energy savings... Designed from the start to be integrated into the environment and enjoy it with all senses. Where the inside and outside merge to become one and where nature and rest blend in a majestic way. Where details and quality of the materials used are worth taking the time to admire because like with all dreams, it is hard to believe they are real.
