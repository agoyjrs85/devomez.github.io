The Soldiers and Rock Choir appeared together on stage at the Cliffs Pavilion in Southend to sing for the sell-out crowd. This clip is taken from the fantastic DVD 'The Soldiers Coming Home: The Live Tour'. Rock Choir also sang with the boys on the Beatles classic 'With A Little Help From My Friends' and Elton John's 'Don't Let The Sun Go Down On Me', all of which feature on the DVD. The crowd were amazing!

The tour was an incredible ride for the 300 Rock Choir members who took part, utterly emotional sometimes as you can see in this clip when The Soldiers star Ryan Idzi is seen fighting back the tears, and other times being the most unexpected, wonderful and life affirming Rock Choir™ experience.

The Rock Choir team would like to thank The Soldiers management for their permission to use this clip.
