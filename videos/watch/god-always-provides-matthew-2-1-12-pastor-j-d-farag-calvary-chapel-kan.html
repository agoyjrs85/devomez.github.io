About Pastor JD Farag and his wife Kellie

JD Farag was born in Beirut, Lebanon to an Egyptian father and Palestinian mother and immigrated to America in 1963. In 1988 he married his wife, Kellie, in Spokane, Washington and moved to Hawaii where they started their first Bible study in 2004. Being a strong supporter of Israel, he studies Bible prophecy pertaining to the Middle East as well as current prophetic events. In addition to his sermons he also does a weekly prophecy update. JD and Kellie currently live on the Windward side of Oahu with their three children Elias, Levi, and Sabia.
