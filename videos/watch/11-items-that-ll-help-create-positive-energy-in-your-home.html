This could be anything from a small elephant ornament to a pottery project. They are said to bring good luck, protection, wealth, and wisdom as this animal is considered to be a sign of prosperity, understanding, and loyalty.

Have you seen those rustic iron horseshoes hanging by certain entrances? Well. there's a reason for them.
