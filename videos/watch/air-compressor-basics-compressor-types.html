Transcript:
An air compressor is a device which takes input mechanical power typically from a motor and converts it to fluid air power. The compressed air can be stored for later use in compressed air tanks or it can be transferred via pipes to a point of use.

Compressors with a piston as the compressing element are called reciprocating compressors. Compressors can also be made with veins or impellers. These types of compressors are called rotary compressors.
