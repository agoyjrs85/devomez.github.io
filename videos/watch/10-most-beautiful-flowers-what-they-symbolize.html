10 Most Beautiful Flowers & What They Symbolize

We can find the numerous flowers around the world and almost all of them come with beautiful look, color and shapes.

But did you know that every flower symbolises something?

Here are top 10 most beautiful flowers and there symbolic meanings -


10. Dahlia -

This beautiful flower grow in variety of colors including rust, yellow, white, orange and purple. It symboize dignity and an elegance. It is also the symbol of a commitment and bond that lasts forever.

09. Blue Iris

Iris also grows in various colors including blue, purple, white and yellow, pink and orange, brown and red, and even black. It symbolize wisdom, purity, faith or passion.

08. Purple Daisy

Daisy flowers also grows in variety of colors and they can be found everywhere on Earth except Antarctica. It symbolizes innocence, loyal love and simpliciy. All these characteristics can be found in Mother's love so it is a symbol of mothers love!

07. Rose
As like many other flowers, roses are also available in various colors like pink, red, white, purple and blue. Their beauty has ability to attract everyone. They symbolize the TRUE LOVE, desire, passion, gratitude, joy of life, friendship, grace and success. That is why it is so popular among the lovers and become famous as a symbol of love.

06. Bird of Paradise

These flower has uniqe shape and beautiful color. Its sepals & purple petals resemble like a colorful flying bird, so it is named as bird of paradise. It symbolize faithfulness, Love and thoughtfulness.

05. Geranium

Geranium available in more 60 types and has variety of colors. Often, they used to make an essential oils. It symbolize gentility, happiness, determination & positive emotions.

04. Bleeding Heart

These pink, heart-shaped flowers had an unique hanging part on the bottom side that looks like a small drop. So, it is named as bleeding heart. It symbolize purity, innocence & true love.

03. Water Lily

Water lily flower symbolizes humility, fertility, sexuality, peace, purity, creation, pleasure and spiritual enlightenment.

02. Tulips

Tulips flower buds growsg almost perfectly symmetrical and most tulips sprout the single flower bud, but there are some tulip varieties that sprout the four on a single stem. They symbolize perfect love, royalty, forgiveness and worthiness.

01. Orchid

Orchid is a bilaterally symmetrical, colorful and one of the most ancient flower in the world. As per the various fossile evidences, it is assumed that orchids have existed on the planet around 100 million years. It symbolize love, luxury, beauty & strength.
