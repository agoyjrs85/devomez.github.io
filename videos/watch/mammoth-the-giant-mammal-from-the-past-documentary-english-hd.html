Mammoths were once some of the most versatile creatures to ever roam the planet. Curious about what made up the magnificent mammoths? Take a trip back in time with us as we explore the wonders of this gentle giant. Just imagine the mega-proportions; a pair of tusks up to 13 feet long, or a skull larger than any human! Discover with us where these animals lived, what they ate, why they went away, and if we might be able to bring them back. By studying ancient cave paintings, as well-preserved bodies and DNA, scientists have been able to piece together the puzzle of the mysterious mammal. Join us as delve into the extraordinary world of the mammoth.

Narrator: Larry G. Jones
