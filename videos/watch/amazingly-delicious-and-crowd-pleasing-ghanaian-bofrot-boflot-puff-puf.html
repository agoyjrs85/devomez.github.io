Hello Family,hope you are all well. I share with you my bofrot/boflot/puff puff recipe with you because I know you might want to try it during this lockdown. It’s very simple and easy to make. Comes together in 30 minutes. BOFROT in Ghana can be served with Millet porridge or enjoyed as a snack with any juice. I hope you enjoy and try this recipe.
Thank you for your endless support.


INGREDIENTS
5 Cups all purpose flour

1/2 teaspoon baking powder (just because I’m going to add yeast as well)

Table salt about 1/4 tablespoon

2 1/2 cups of warm water

1/2 tablespoon of active dry yeast

Half of whole nutmeg

Oil for frying
