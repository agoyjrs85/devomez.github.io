Sigma recently sent us over the New I Series lenses and they do not disappoint. The new I Series lenses consists of the 24/3.5, 35/2, 45/2.8, and the 65/2. The build quality was the first thing I noticed when I took the lenses out of the box. The full metal housing gave me an initial impression that these lenses were going to be good. Let's just say that initial impression was spot on. The image quality was great and to top it off the AF was reliable. I love small lenses like these that pack a punch. It is easy to want to spend more on a lens that opens up to 1.8 or even 1.4 but I found that an F2 is more than enough for 90% of my shoots.
