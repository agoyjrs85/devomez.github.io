OMNITREND is the proven software for the monitoring of operating conditions and diagnosis of rotating equipment and critical assets by allowing users to:
- monitor the trend of machine-specific characteristic; overall values and signal bands.
- analyze machine signals such as FFT, timewave forms, orbits
- carry out machine vibration and anti-friction bearing diagnosis.

This special webinar is designed to cover those questions most commonly asked by users on OMNITREND. Colin Pickett, Consultant Engineer, answered basic and advanced questions submitted by the users.
