Come behind the scenes of the ten-year production of my stop-motion animated sci-fi short, 'Fabricated'. In the beginning, I had an idea, along with hundreds of pounds of broken machines and scrap metal. What sort of world, and what sort of life would arise from the pieces left behind? This video documents the construction and animation of that world, as well some equally complex goof shots I made to relieve the heavy tedium of the whole process.

The completed film can be seen here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=19A0X7wLX9A
