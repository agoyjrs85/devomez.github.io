Founded by the renowned Musician Vidwan Delhi P. Sunder Rajan, Maarga translates to "The Path" in Sanskrit. True to its name, Maarga’s primary aim is to pave a path for preserving and promoting the traditional art forms of India. Maarga strives to propagate our Indian Music and Culture throughout the world.The organization aims to create opportunities for deserving youngsters to perform.



Maarga was launched under the auspices of “Delhi P. Sunder Rajan’s Academy of Indian Music” , which began in 2004.

Maarga has been in the limelight since 2008 when it started its activities in chennai with an Inter school Music competition in which more than 1000 students participated.

Maarga launched its Annual Music Festival during the famous December Music season in the year 2013, where many brilliant youngsters were given an opportunity to perform. Top ranking musicians also performed in this festival. The even was an instant success and since then this event has become very popular all over India and abroad.

In the last Music season in December 2018, the Festival was held from 26th to 31st December ,at Ramakrishna Mission School, T.Nagar Chennai. More than 300 artistes from different parts of the world performed in this grand Festival. MAny talented youngsters performed in this festival and in the evenings popular stars of Carnatic Music were featured. Maarga institued an award called ‘Sangeetha Margadarshi, which is given to a Veteran Musician/musicologist/Guru. This year it was awarded to Vidwan Shri PS Narayanaswami.



The festival drew a very large audience and was a huge success yet again !!
