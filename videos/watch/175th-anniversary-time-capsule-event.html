Clackamas County lowered a 50-year time capsule on the ground of the Red Soils campus in Oregon City in celebration of the county’s 175th anniversary. Items in the time capsule – to be opened in 2068 – were selected because of their uniqueness in representing Clackamas County, their representation of the county’s strategic plan (www.clackamas.us/performance), and their overall expected interest level for future residents.
