Learn how to light a scene and make it look like daytime when it's dark out and actually the middle of the night. I show you how to light a dark room in the easiest way possible, with only one light.

Getting the film look and be a cinematographer requires shooting at odd times. Sometimes a shoot runs late into the night, but you still need it to be sunny daylight in the scene, that's where lighting scenes like this come in handy.

It's easy to find videos about how to do day for night videos, but how about night for day? We make window light look natural and soft, when in fact it's an artificial lighting setup. Please share this video with your friends if it helped you in any way!
