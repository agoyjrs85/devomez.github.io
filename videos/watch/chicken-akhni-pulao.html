Ingredients
For Chicken Stock
Chicken 500 grams
Coriander seeds 1.5 tbsp
Cumin seeds 1 tsp
Fennel Seeds 1 tsp
Black pepper corns 1/2 tsp
Bay leaf 1 no
Cinnamon sticks 2 nos
Cloves 8 nos
Mace 1/2
Green Cardamom 2 nos
Black Cardamom 2 nos
Onion small size roughly chopped
Ginger 2 inch
Garlic cloves 5 nos
Salt 1 tsp
Water 4 cups

For Akhni Pulao
Onions - medium size 1 no thinly sliced
Ginger garlic paste 1.5 tsp
Green chillies 4-5 nos slit
Yoghurt 1 cup
Rice 2 cups soaked in water for 30 minutes
Ghee 3 tbsp

Method
For Chicken Stock
Take a vessel and put the chicken, coriander seeds, cumin seeds, fennel seeds, black pepper corns, bay leaf, cinnamon sticks, cloves, mace, green cardamom, black cardamom, onion roughly chopped, ginger, garlic cloves, salt and water. Mix it and keep on high heat for 5 minutes. Now keep on low heat for 15 minutes. For cups water should be reduced to three cups. Then remove the chicken pieces from the stock. Now strain the stock which is 3 cups and add 1 cup of water to the stock.

For Akhni Pulao
Take a vessel and heat the ghee. Now add the onions and fry till it turns light brown. Then add ginger garlic paste and fry for 2 minutes at medium heat. Now add the chicken pieces and fry for 2 minutes. Add green chillies and mix well. Now add yoghurt and fry till water absorbs from the yoghurt. Then add the 4 cups chicken stock and close the lid and bring it to a boil. Meanwhile check the salt. Now add the rice which is soaked in water for 30 minutes. Cook on high heat till the water is almost reduced. Now close the lid and keep it on low heat for 15 minutes until the rice is done.
Serve and Enjoy
