“A laser is a solution seeking a problem.” Theodore Maiman, developer of the first laser. Do you want to know how a laser is used in biomedical imaging? What is needed to get medically relevant information from biological tissue? How is it even possible to bring systems from optical laboratories to the patient’s bedside? The Road to YSA’21 webinar "Optical imaging in medicine: A cutting-edge selection by the SPIE BIOS chairs" with JENNIFER K. BARTON and WOLFGANG DREXLER gives insights into most relevant topics in the field of biomedical imaging research. Both are chairing the SPIE BIOS conference (part of the SPIE Photonics West, with more than 22.000 attendees).
