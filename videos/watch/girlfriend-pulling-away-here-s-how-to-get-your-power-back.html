Girlfriend pulling away. If she's pulling away I tell you the one thing you have to do when she pulls away. When a woman says I need space or when she says she needs space means the same thing. Some women ask for it and some don't. The #1 thing you have to do too is pulling away too. Especially if your girlfriend losing interest or when your girlfriend no responding. She needs space possibly because she feels that you are too overwhelming for her or overbearing which can happen.
Pulling away is the ultimate thing to do so you can develop in your own personal growth. You have to get in line with your emotions and if she's pulling away there may be some reason behind it. Why do women pull away? It's because she might not be in a good place in her life too. You have to really look into the reasons why she is pulling away and not try and convince her to come back to you. As if you will change and you will do whatever it takes to be with her. Attraction stems from being focused on you. When your girlfriend pulls away do not convince her to show actions if it was something that you needed to change to be in this relationship.

She will try and reconnect and this is when you can set the tone and really be driven on focusing on your own personal growth. When she sees that you are developing a new change in your life she is going to want in because its a new you. Come from a place of power, growth, and respect so the same challenges don't reappear in the relationship.

Taking the time away from her to show her you are of value and you don't need her to validate your existence will speak strongly to her wanting to connect again with her. It's like that saying. Distance makes the heart grow founder! The key is focusing on you and moving forward in your life and stop letting your emotions ride on their reactions.
