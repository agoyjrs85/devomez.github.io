📚 Your portfolio is your very own little corner of the internet. And in our competitive design industry, it’s more crucial than ever to make the most of this space.

You’ll hear from Kelly Stevens, a product designer with 8+ years of experience designing digital products and systems at scale. Kelly is currently a senior product designer at WeTransfer in Amsterdam, where she designs tools to move ideas. Previously she was part of the design teams at Facebook Messenger and Tripadvisor.
--------------------
In this webinar, Kelly will share her tips for crafting an effective portfolio, including:

👉 Start with your narrative
👉Design for the job you want
👉Save inspiration from designers you admire
👉Craft it well
👉Sweat the details
👉Update it often
👉Spread it everywhere
