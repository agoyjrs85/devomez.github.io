You're making a TRAUMA-DRIVEN DECISION when you *think* you're making a good choice but your past abuse and neglect are distorting your thinking. You *know* on some level you're doing it, but you make that self-destructive choice anyway -- to move in with someone you just met, or marry someone who treats you badly, or go the other way and choose to isolate yourself, or to storm out of a job you actually liked and needed -- because your past trauma got triggered and you felt this urgent need to make the feeling stop. In reality these decisions put you in danger of *more* trauma.

In this video I'll teach you how to tell when you *might* be doing it again, and how turn the situation around so this time, you can make solid choices that support your goals and your life.
