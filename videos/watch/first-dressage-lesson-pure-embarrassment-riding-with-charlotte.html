Goodness me, the day has finally come where I bit the bullet and underwent my first real flat work ‘dressage’ lesson! I think the best way to describe my ability in emoji format would be this...🥴...First of all, the only reason I am happy to share this is because I am so determined to transform, so please feel free to laugh if you wish...I mean, I didn't even know where 'X' was in the school and got confused by my left and right rein🤣🙈

My dressage coach is very reputable and has competed very successfully herself so as you can imagine, I was EXTREMELY nervous...can you image how I feel on a daily basis being at a yard filled with incredible riders🙈 anyhow, I started like a sack of potatoes or as my coach phrased it “riding like an old lady🤣”. Although we only stayed in walk and trot, I believe towards the end we had some significant improvement and that’s what counts 😁

I’m thoroughly excited for my up coming lessons, really really eager to learn and improve 💪🏻

I hope you enjoy😁
