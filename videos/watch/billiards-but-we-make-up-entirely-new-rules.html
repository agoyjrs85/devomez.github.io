This was pretty fun but this video couldn’t have been made without the videoshop app and obviously not sponsored. Here are the rules if you don’t know them.
1. The goal is to get every ball in the pockets in increasing number order
2. Play order is age order oldest to youngest except for the last two people who play rock paper scissors and the winner of the rock paper scissors picks where both people go in the play order
3. If you scratch you lose a point
4. If you hit the right ball in you gain one point
5. If you hit the right ball in and also scratch you do not gain or lose any points and both balls are taken out of the pockets they were hit into
6. If you hit the wrong ball in and also scratch you lose two points and only the que ball gets taken out of the pocket it was hit into
7. If you hit the wrong ball into a pocket you lose one point
8. The 8 ball is worth 2 points whether you gain or lose points
9. The 15 ball(the last ball)is worth 3 points whether you gain or lose points
10. When a player scratches the next player must place the ball wherever they want on the opposite side the table was scratched on
