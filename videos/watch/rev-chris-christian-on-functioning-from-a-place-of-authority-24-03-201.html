Authority is defined as willingness to function, to act, it is different from power. Power is the ability to function. The two are related because when you are in a place, you use power. Authority is stronger than power. Satan is not afraid of power. Situation fears authority. Authority cannot be exercised until there is a given place. If you remove place, there will be no authority. Relationship that doesn't have place does not have destiny. The moment God created Adam in His image and likeness, He brought him to a place of authority so that he can function for Him. Satan knows the power of authority, so he wants believers to be ignorant of it.

All intelligent and sensible people on earth don't trifle with authority because authority has power of life and death. Relationship with God can only prosper around a place. If people try to manifest authority without a place, it is called manipulation and it is an abomination in the realm of the spirit. It is not correct for you to give somebody a place and deny the person the authority of the place.
