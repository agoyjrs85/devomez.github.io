Discipline is the bridge between thought and accomplishment, the bridge between necessity and productivity. If there is one word that separates failure from success, it's Discipline. Failure is the result of neglecting the small daily disciplines over and over again. This neglect accumulated over a couple of years brings disaster. On the other hand success is the repetition of small daily disciplines over and over again on a consistent basis. Do not let neglect stack upon you so that you have Sorry scenario years from now, instead of celebrating your Success - Jim Rohn
