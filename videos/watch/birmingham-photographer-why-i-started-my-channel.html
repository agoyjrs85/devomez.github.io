I want to talk to y'all about why I am starting a YouTube channel. Video has always seemed kind of intimidating for me. I was very hesitant to do any type of video content like Tik-tok, Instagram Reels, and of course, YouTube.

To me it seemed like everyone was shifting towards podcasts, but my mind has since been changed, and I feel that video is making a big comeback. This video outlines my reasoning for this.

This channel will be about all things photography, but not just for the fellow photographer. I will be educating clients as well. This is a channel that I feel everyone can get education and use from. So I hope you will follow along in this journey with me, and hang with me on my other platforms as well!
