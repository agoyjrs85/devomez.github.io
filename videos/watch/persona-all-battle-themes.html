You might be wondering why I decided to separate the PS1 games from the rest of the series and Persona 5's spin-offs from itself:
This is because I believe the PS1 songs are stylistically very similar but different from the rest of the series, and making them "separate" from the rest would make more sense sonically. As for the Persona 5 situation, this is because I found that it'd be very odd to have the Persona Q2 songs play AFTER Persona 5 Royal and Strikers, and I believe that this order makes more sense, is more cohesive, and more pleasant to listen to.
Also, I know that the Featherman song is from P3D & P5D -- however, Royal was the first time the song itself has been featured in its full length, and as such, I thought that was enough to count for this video. Hope you don't mind that.
