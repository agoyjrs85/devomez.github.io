Join us this week on @ABOARD MERMAID MONSTER as we discuss what the cost of boat maintenance is. There are many costs that go into living aboard full-time but maintenance with a trawler is something that is pretty darn consistent and if you put it off you end up with astronomical expenses down the road. If you are new here, then welcome. We are the Palmer family and we have been living aboard our Nordhavn 55 trawler, Mermaid Monster for 2.5 year now with plans to circumnavigate the globe. With time being non renewable and our home being moveable, around the world we go as we roam to know. Enjoy.
