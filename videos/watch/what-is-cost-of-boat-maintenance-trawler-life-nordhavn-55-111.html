If you are new here, then welcome. We are the Palmer family and we have been living aboard our Nordhavn 55 trawler, Mermaid Monster for 2.5 year now with plans to circumnavigate the globe. With time being non renewable and our home being moveable, around the world we go as we roam to know. Enjoy.
