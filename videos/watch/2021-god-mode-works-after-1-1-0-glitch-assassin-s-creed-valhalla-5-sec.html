Contrary to popular wisdom God Mode, which makes Eivor invisible to enemy NPCs still works after update (patch) 1.1.0. Thus, you can kill any foe at any level by timing your exit after forfeiting the orlog (dice) game in Lunden, England or Fornburg, Norway. Its all about timing your fast travel as Eivor is getting up from the ground. If you are too early you cannot fast travel. If he or she is too late, you won't be invisible to foes. As she turns away from the game standing up is the prime time. The Fornburg orlog allows you to use God Mode in Norway only. The Lunden dice game allows you to enter God Mode in England only. Take off God Mode by using the atlas feature of the map to fast travel from England to Norway or vice versa.

Thanks to T7 PDM for pointing out this farming method to me.
