Coupe SUVs and crossover are the most controversial class of automobiles in the world. Drivers are scratching their heads: why in the world would car makers chop off a part of the car and sacrifice luggage space and head room? But designers keep insisting that the sloping roofline is stylish and adds sporty aggression to the otherwise boring SUV exterior. To decide who you will side with in this debate lets take a look at the best models currently on sale and review their technical specifications.

0:40 #1 2020 Porsche Cayenne Coupe

The 2020 Cayenne Coupe boasts a new exterior design with a sleek roofline, an aggressive front fascia, and adaptive rear spoiler. The model seats 5 in all modifications, and it is available in two trim levels with different performance characteristics.

1:45 #2 2020 Mercedes-Benz GLC Coupe

Since the conventional GLC model has gone through a facelift for the 2020 model year, the coupe version of the SUV was close to follow. The front fascia as usual gets more aggressive, and both front and rear bumpers and headlights are tweaked to lure in new buyers.

2:51 #3 2019 Audi Q8

2019 Audi Q8 gets a fully updated exterior design with a new octagonal grille, Matrix HD headlights and LED taillights with animations. Under its hood sits a a 3.0L turbocharged V6, which uses a mild hybrid technology and delivers 335 horses and 369 lb-ft of torque.

3:56 #4 2019 BMW X4 and BMW X4 M

The new 2019 BMW X4 represents the second generation of the compact crossover. It shares its platform with the X3 model and gets a number of exterior and structural revisions.

5:01 #5 2019 Mercedes GLE Coupe

Mercedes AMG GLE Coupe is scheduled to enter new generation 2020 and has already been spied testing in camo. Currently, the old generation 2019 model remains available, offered in the US as AMG GLE 43 and 63S Coupe versions.

6:05 #6 2019 BMW X6

2019 BMW X6 stays in the line-up barely changed for another year, standing on the same old bones of the second X6 generation introduced in 2015. The few 2019 additions include newly standard safety features, such as forward collision mitigation and blind-spot monitoring, plus Apple CarPlay.

7:11 #7 2019 Lamborghini Urus

2019 Lamborghini Urus is the fastest crossover model ever produced that is capable of sprinting to 62 mph in 3.2 seconds and speeding to 190 mph.

8:17 #8 2021 Tesla Model Y

Tesla’s domination in the EV segment could be explained by the well thought out strategy of expansion from expensive premium models and going down to the affordable segment. To solidify the success of the Model 3, a crossover riding on its platform is being released in spring 2020 under the name Model Y.

9:22 #9 2019 Range Rover SV Coupe

2019 Range Rover SV Coupe was to become one of the most expensive and luxurious models of the brand built to date, but unfortunately Land Rover chose to cancel production of all 999 units that were initially planned.
