Ever felt surface level while reading the Bible? Like you want to get into your Bible but you just don't know how. You read it then close it and that's it. Well... It's my hope that this video help you learn how to in-depth study your bible! In-depth bible studies are my favorite. I encourage y'all to dig deep into scripture and learn more about God & yourself!
