Ever felt surface level while reading the Bible? Like you want to get into your Bible but you just don't know how. You read it then close it and that's it. Or maybe you try and get something out of it but you are confused. Or... you just don't open your Bible at all because it's such a large book... Where to start?

Well... It's my hope that this video help you learn how to in-depth study your bible! In-depth bible studies are my favorite. I learn so much, grow so much, come closer to God, and put the Bible into action in my own life!! This type of bible study can also be called inductive bible study! I encourage y'all to dig deep into scripture and learn more about God & yourself!
