MedTap is a medicines information app developed by NHS clinicians. MedTap offers short videos from NHS staff on your medicines, in clear language, that you can access whenever you need.

•       How and when to take your pills
•       Side effects and what to do

MedTap is available to download via the Apple App Store or Google play for android
