This is a requested review on my Louis Vuitton Speedy 25 with the Monogram Strap. Thank you @lchen0729 for requesting this video on my Instagram. This is how I use the strap occasionally on my bag so please bear in mind that if you use the strap in the same way demonstrated in this video then you are doing so at your own risk. I cannot guarantee there'll be no damage with use over time. I personally don't use the strap on my Speedy 25 that often and usually I don't carry a lot when I do.

Sorry about the constant change of lighting throughout the video. The day I filmed was really windy, so literally one minute it was bright and sunny, and the next really dark and cloudy. Some of the close-up shots were a bit over-exposed due to this, but I hope you still enjoyed the video.

If you're wondering about the light patina despite a bag purchased many years ago - it's because I have not used this bag very much and it has been stored away for many years until recently when I have been taking it out a lot more. The vachetta has obviously patina'd over the years even when stored away, but on camera it looks a little paler than in real life. I am always careful not to hold the bag with dirty hands and not take it out when it's raining. I've always preferred hands-free bags so the Speedy has been neglected over the years, but I have fallen in love again...
