In this English lesson I'll model how to talk about yourself in English by talking about myself. I'll tell you 10 things about my life that you might not have known already. At the same time I'll give you model sentences that you can learn so that you can describe yourself in English!

I'll talk about birth order, family history, likes and dislikes. I know some of the things I may have mentioned before, but some will be new to you!

Also don't miss my hammer trick towards the end of the video!

I hope you enjoy this little English lesson on how to describe yourself in English and I wish you all the best as you continue to learn the English language.

Have a great day!
