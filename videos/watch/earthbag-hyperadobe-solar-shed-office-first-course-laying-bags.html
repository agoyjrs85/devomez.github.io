It's time to lay the first course in our hyperadobe solar shed office! First course went relatively smooth, but we're spilling the beans about what we did wrong and what we did right. It's always a bit challenging to work on projects off-grid, and this one is no different, but with the help of our four amazing kids, we're getting the job started!
