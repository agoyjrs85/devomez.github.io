The spotlight this week is on the cast of 'A Suitable Boy' . The show is an adaptation of Vikram Seth's novel and is directed by Mira Nair. NDTV's Rohit's Khilnani spoke to the show's cast about the theme of the show, its relevance in today's times, working with Mira Nair, and lots more.
