For the record, Hypermyopia is a word I made up to describe my extreme myopia. This video showcases the intense blurriness I experience in my vision without glasses.

In addition to myopia, I also have high factor astigmatism that causes light to be fuzzy in my vision. Currently there is no direct cure or treatment to completely cure this issue. My only advice is to take care of your eyes.

Don't read books or watch screens in the dark. Eat loads of Vitamin A and Bilberry and Lutein to strengthened the eyes. And lastly, don't stare are screens excessively like I did. Hope you enjoy the video!
