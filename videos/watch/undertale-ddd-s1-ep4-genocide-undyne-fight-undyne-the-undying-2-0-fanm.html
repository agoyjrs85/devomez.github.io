So, WHERE HAVE I BEEN???
Well, to tell the truth, I've been making other videos for some friends and relatives (birthday videos, photo collages - the lot). Plus, media projects ramp up and occupy my time. But enough of that!

Man, I didn't know Undyne's fight was gonna take this long. It might not seem like much (I mean, 7 minutes doesn't seem like much), but I was kinda stuck in a few areas. On top of that, I was actually conflicted multiple times on whether or not I should continue with Undyne, or scrap it altogether. I mean, this is a series that showcases Monsters - who didn't get a genocide run, having exclusive, genocide-run forms. Since Undyne was already done, at first I didn't really consider the idea to make it. BUUUT... With the Geno-Monster Kid video, I (accidentally) left Undyne's arc to be ambiguous in its ending. I didn't really imply that her fight was gonna end the same way it usually would. As such, many people started asking for the Undyne fight, understandably. AS SUCH, I made a whole video to patch up the open-ended story arc of Undyne. Another partial reason, was due to the fact that it DID, in fact, feel necessary to have one - for story's sake. So, I don't regret making this - but, let's just say, my enthusiasm was in different places. (Like the Undertale DDD Prequel! *ahem*)

If it's any consolation, I've learned how to do a few things, thanks to the creation of Undyne's fight. And with that, Asgore's fight is gonna have that extra kick to it! (Hopefully)

Anyways, hope you enjoy!

-TK35

Undertale DDD Info
Undyne the Undying (2.0)
HP: 24,000
ATK: 99
DEF: 99
Difficulty: ***

Alas, Undyne has transformed once again in one last ditch effort to strike you down. But she’s got a few more tricks up her sleeves this time!

Undyne utilises ‘Green SOUL Mode’ but instead of it shifting to whatever key you press, your arrows now rotate the shield, so long as you hold the key down. Attacks are much harder, plentiful and have newer designs as well as revamping old attacks. Thankfully, she does slightly less damage than before.

“Undyne didn’t really have much of a revamp. She was perfect the way she was for a genocide run and all that I could revamp really, was her attacks. I didn’t wanna over-complicate things. I did however revamp the Green SOUL mechanic to make things both unique and interesting. I actually decreased her damage to the player to make it hard but ‘fair’, given the amount of bullets she spams this time around (WOAH, I BALANCED A CHARACTER?!). Inspiration-wise? Youmu, ‘nuff said. Oh, and a bit of Meira here and there. Aside from that, I gave her the Kingdom Hearts BBS Shotlock system. Why? Next commentary I ‘might’ be able to explain it.”
