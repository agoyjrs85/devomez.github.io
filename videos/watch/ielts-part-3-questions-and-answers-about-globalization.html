The general topic of “globalization” can pop up on the IELTS exam in a variety of ways, usually on Speaking Part 3 and Writing Task 2.

It could be connected to other topics like commerce, industry, advertising, travel or culture.

Example Speaking Part 3 questions about globalization that are answered in this video are:

1. How has globalization changed your hometown or city?
2. What are the disadvantages of globalization?

In this video, I follow the simple, clear strategy as taught in the online course The 3 Keys IELTS Success System, which can be used to tackle every Speaking Part 3 question.

First, give your opinion, then support it with very specific reasons, examples and details, and, finally, provide a very short conclusion sentence.

Watch this video a couple times and pay attention to how the answers are organized.

Then, practice answering the questions on your own.
