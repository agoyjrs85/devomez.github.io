Formed in 2016 by High School desk neighbors turned life-long friends Brody Tolliver (singer/rhythm guitarist) and Shelton Wood (lead guitarist), In Good Condition captivated a devoted audience that only expanded with their debut EP: For Real! (2019). Their sound has been described as explosive and technical instrumentation alongside contemplative lyrics. The combination of these cathartic rhythms and pensive, flowing melodies makes In Good Condition an unforgettable Indie-Rock experience.
