The government sources have informed Times Now that the Central government has rebutted the claims made in an article in The Washington Post. An official statement for the same is also likely to come out soon. The government has claimed that the claims made in Washington Post's article are exaggerated and fallacious. This is in regards to the Urban Naxals and the evidence that is said to be planted. The report has been generated by a lab in the United States and the government has rejected the report. Pune's FSL report has denied the charges of tampering. The government agencies have cited the FSL report. Watch!
