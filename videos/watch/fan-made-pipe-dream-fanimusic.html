Hey all, I finally finished adding instruments and effects to the Pipe Dream model and It's essentially complete. When/if I improve the script, I'll be able to go back and fix some wonky animations, but as a prototype, I'm happy with it.
The design and name of Pipe Dream was created by Animusic LLC, I really enjoy their work, and it inspired me to become a 3D artist in the first place so I decided I should pay tribute to it! https://youtu.be/x-eq1jTm0ak
