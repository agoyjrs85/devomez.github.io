Interview with Jack Heasman and Ruth Betteridge (Peter Pan 2019/20) - Flying into Blackpool for Christmas 2019 is the swashbuckling adventure, Peter Pan. Our roving reporter Hayley Kay caught up with actors Jack Heasman (Peter Pan) and Ruth Betteridge (Wendy Darling) on all the behind the scenes gossip from this year's Panto!

Peter Pan Pantomime Trailer - Flying into Blackpool for Christmas 2019/20 is the swashbuckling adventure, Peter Pan.

Blackpool's Best Pantomime!

Starring Soap and West End Star Tom Lister as Captain Hook, Blackpool panto favourite from BBC Radio Lancashire Steve Royle as Smee, Jack Heasman as Peter Pan, Christina Meehan as Mrs Darling, Rachel Grundy as Tinkerbell, Natalie Hollingsworth as Tiger Lilly, Ruth Betteridge as Wendy, comedy acrobats The Nitwits as The Pirate Crew and the Barbara Jackson Theatre Arts dancers.

Meet Peter and the lost boys, Tiger Lily and Tinker Bell and get ready for a family show full of fun, fairy dust and pirates galore. Set sail for Neverland and take on Captain Hook and prepare for out-of-this-world flying effects, galleons of laughter and the hungriest of crocodiles.

A J M Barrie Classic tale adapted for stage by UK Productions Ltd.
