The Conference is a forum for presenting new advances and research results in the fields will provide an excellent international forum for sharing knowledge and results in theory, methodology and applications of Information Technology Convergence and Services.

Highlights of ITCSE 2020 include:

• 8th International Conference of Networks and Communications (NC 2020)

• 9th International Conference on Cryptography and Information Security(CRYPIS 2020)
• 9th International Conference on Digital Image Processing and Vision (ICDIPV 2020)
