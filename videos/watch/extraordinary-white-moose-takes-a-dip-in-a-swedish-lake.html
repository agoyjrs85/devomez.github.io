A rare white moose was spotted taking a dip in a pool in Sweden’s Varmland County.

Hans Nilsson told Storyful he captured the video between Charlottenberg and Gunarskog.

Nilsson describes himself as a “nature lover.” He told Sweden’s Sveriges Radio the experience was “as great as seeing a leopard in Africa.”

Nilsson along with multiple sources said white moose are rare. He told local reporters there were only 50 white moose in western Varmland, where the video was captured.
