For success in life it is important to be stubborn. Most valuable virtue to make it trough difficult situations, that every man faces on the road to success. Peter Mankoč is a Slovenian swimmer. He is one of the most successful short course European Championship swimmers in the history of the event. Mankoč is the former world record holder in the 100 meter individual medley (short course).
