"The Warmer Side of Cool" is the title track to Wang Chungs fifth studio album, fourth on Geffen Records. The song is the eighth track on the album. Wang Chung is Jack Hues (vocals) and Nick Feldman (bass).



"I came up with the opening riff in a dressing room while we were touring in '87 but the song evolved over a long time. I wrote two complete songs around the riff before I finally started liking what I heard. Peter tried to persuade me to drop the middle section but Nick and I didn't want a commercial song out of this. It needs to fly off into an exotic place at that point, that's what the song is about -- push things beyond their limits, be committed to creating something unique! Paulinho da Costa came in one afternoon to do all the percussion. He's a Brazilian and had all these different instruments and objects out of which he conjured the strangest sounds. We all just sat back and enjoyed the show." - Jack Hues on "The Warmer Side of Cool"
