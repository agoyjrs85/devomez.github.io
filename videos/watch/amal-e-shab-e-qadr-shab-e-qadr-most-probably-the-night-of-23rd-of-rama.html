Shab-e-Qadr (the night of Qadr) is a highly virtuous night. The prayers of this night is better than the prayers of one thousand nights. The Holy Quran was transferred on the holy heart of the Holy Prophet(sawaw) in this night. It is the night during which the events of the next year are determined and Angels descend on earth throughout the night and visit the Imam-al-Asr waz-Zaman Imam Mehdi(a.s.). It is recommended to stay awake and pray, recite the Holy Quran and ask for the betterment of the current life and the life hereafter.
