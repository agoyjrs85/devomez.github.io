One of the biggest challenges of being an entrepreneur or freelancer is how much to pay yourself. It's easy to shortchange your own paycheque in the interest of keeping money in the business -- or to overpay yourself and end up with a huge tax bill at the end of the year! In this video I share my resources, tools, tips and tricks to paying yourself when self-employed to keep both your business and your personal bank account happy.
