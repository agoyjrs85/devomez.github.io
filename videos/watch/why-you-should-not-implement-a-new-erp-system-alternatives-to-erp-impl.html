ERP implementations are risky initiatives and not for everyone. Many leading flagship cloud solutions are immature and many organizations are not ready for the technological capabilities that new ERP systems can provide. This is why it is important to consider saying "no" to a new ERP system and focusing on lower risk initiatives instead.

This video outlines the reasons why you should consider this option, along with some of the alternatives that may provide lower-cost, lower-risk, and higher ROI results for your organization. IT also provides some potential interim solutions as you prepare for an ERP implementation in the future.
