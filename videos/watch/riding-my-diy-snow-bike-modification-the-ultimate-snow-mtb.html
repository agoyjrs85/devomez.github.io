Riding my DIY snow bike modification - The ultimate snow MTB! I built a snow bike and went for a test ride in snow covered England. I go full speed, hit kicker ramp jumps and even some urban MTB freeride, would you ride this?

I woke up to a dreamland of snow which covered my hometown and most of England so the first thing I did was build a sick snow bike using an old snowboard, my Haibike dirt jump bike and some imagination before heading out to try riding my new ultimate snow MTB! I start the ride off at a local snow park that gets filled up whenever snow comes to Colchester which doesn't happen very often hence all the huge crowds! After getting the hang of the bike I move to a new location and try some more daring tricks, which of course end in disaster! After dusting myself down I head over to a perfect location to build up the MTB Hopper kicker ramp and try to send it, but will it work?? After a few crashes and a serious slow motion video we head further into town to hit a full speed run down one of the steepest hills in town before attempting a sick urban MTB freeride spot! I'm so happy with how this custom made snow mountain bike turned out and with endless snow forecast over the next week I'm hoping to get in a few more sick sessions with this thing! The bike is brakeless of course but that doesn't matter when soft snow is all around you and most of the time i just crash riding it anyway so who needs brakes, would you ride this machine?

I hope you enjoy the video!
