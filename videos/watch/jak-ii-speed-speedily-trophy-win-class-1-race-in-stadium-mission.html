Game: Jak II HD (PS3)
Trophy: Speed Speedily (gold)
Description: Win Class 1 Race in Stadium

Guide: There are two shortcuts per lap. The first is directly to the right of the starting line. The second is near the end of the lap, on the right after you come out of a tunnel. The first shortcut is a bit tough to make sometimes, but the second shortcut is hard to miss. Use both shortcuts every lap and you should be fine.
