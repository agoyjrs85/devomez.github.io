Following on from our You Need To Do This BEFORE Every Run video, we have put together the ultimate post-run recovery routine! Whether you are a beginner unsure of what to do after your run, or you’ve been looking for a quick and easy post-run routine, The Running Channel has got you covered.

In this video, Anna and Andy take you through the best post-run stretches and exercises to help you reduce your chance of injury and feel better the next time you head out for a run. This simple routine is perfect for after a run and designed to help maximise your recovery and improve your running performance.
