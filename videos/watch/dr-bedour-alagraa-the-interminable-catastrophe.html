Dr Bedour Alagraa is Assistant professor of Political and Social Thought in the Department of African and African diaspora studies at the University of Texas Austin. Her manuscript, The Interminable Catastrophe: Fatal Liberalisms, Plantation Logics, and Black Political Life in the Wake of Disaster charts a conceptual history of catastrophe as a political category/concept (rather than an event), via its inauguration in early modern natural science and empiricist debates, and subsequent crystallization as a concept on the plantation.
