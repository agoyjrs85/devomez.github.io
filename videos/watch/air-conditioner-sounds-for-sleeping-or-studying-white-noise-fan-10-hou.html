Now that summer is over and it's too cold to turn on the AC, here is a white noise air conditioner sound to help you sleep or study. You'll enjoy the soothing deep white noise without any cold air. By creating sound masking that blocks out distractions, the A/C helps create a relaxing environment that is ideal whether you want to sleep, study or focus.

Air conditioners have fans in them which help create the fan white noise that people love. The fan sounds play steadily over the course of 10 hours and we've made sure the AC compressor does not turn on and off so that the fan noise remains steady. It's like having a virtual white noise machine in your bedroom.
