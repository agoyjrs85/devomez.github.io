As Lou Marquez begins his series on lane play, he first enlists USBC research technician Dave Nestor to talk about the differences between wood and synthetic lane surfaces.

While all lanes might look alike to the untrained eye, bowlers know there are many subtle differences that can make one lane drastically different from the one next to it. Lou will get into these topics in the coming weeks, starting today with an overview of wood lane characteristics and synthetic lane characteristics.
