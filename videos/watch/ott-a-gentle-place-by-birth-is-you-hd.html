Ott is a British producer & musician. Influenced by the love of organic dub and pristine electronics, he combines bass-heavy rhythm, abstract soundscapes & timeless melodies into a huge, ever-shifting sonic universe, intended to convey a spirit of hope & positivity.
His 2002 album “Hallucinogen in Dub” which contained chilled dubbby mixes of Hallucinogen tracks, gained universal acclaim and was followed by “Blumenkraft” (2003), “Skylon” (2008) and "Mir" (2011), "Ott & The All-Seeing I - Live At Terminal West” [2013] and "Fairchildren" (2015).
The rest of his time has been spent performing a whirl of gigs, remixes [Shpongle, Bluetech, Entheogenic, Tripswitch] and collaborative projects with Chris Barker, Simon Posford and other collaborations with Brian Eno, The Orb, Youth & more.
Ott has performed worldwide at acclaimed festivals such as Wakarusa, Camp Bisco, Rainbow Serpent, Ozora & many more.
