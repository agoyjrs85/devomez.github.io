This vignette is taken from Paramahamsa Prajnananandaji's classes on the Yoga Sutras of Sage Patanjali. Here the Paramahamsa Yogi explains how ordinary people live with instinct and emotion, while a yogi lives with conscious choice. This vignette will help all seekers practice the art of being a yogi in the simple events of the day. Paramahamsa Prajnanananda is a God-realized master and the current Guru of an authentic and unbroken lineage of Kriya Yoga masters represented by Kriya Yoga International organizations. Paramahamsaji is a world renowned teacher of Spirituality and his discourses and classes on the Gita, Yoga Sutra of Sage Patanjali, and the Upanishads are a veritable spiritual treasure for all seekers.
