Jamie Hopkins, Director of Retirement Research at Carson, explains the place of home-ownership in retirement planning. The average American couple has two-thirds of their wealth invested in their home, and it can be a helpful asset at any point in retirement.

Whether you tap into home equity with a reverse mortgage, or sell and downsize, or use equity to help support long-term care costs, home-ownership can’t be overemphasized as a strategic asset in retirement.
