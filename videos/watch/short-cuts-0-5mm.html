North America's biggest festival of new Japanese film returns for its eighth year, serving up a thrilling cross section of Japan's diverse film cultures to New York audiences! Screening 28 features across 10 days, including co-presentations with the 13th New York Asian Film Festival, JAPAN CUTS premieres the best of recent action epics, genre oddities, touching dramas, warped comedies and cutting-edge arthouse cinema made in and around Japan.
