Many differences make the male and female brain different that developed throughout human history. It’s not bad, it’s a tale of two brains.

There are many differences between the female and male brain, including how neurons interact, the ability to multitask, the ability to think about nothing, and how to communicate. Both genders have strengths and weaknesses, and when working together can create miracles.
