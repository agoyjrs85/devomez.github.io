Rescue Two Abandoned Puppies That Were Fortunately Protected By The Bird || Great Shelter

I was on my way home from work and came across a deserted street. I left at noon so everyone should have gone home. I do not feel scared, but the feeling inside is very strange. I suddenly heard something strange and I decided to stop and see what was the problem? I went over here and saw a huge landslide. I walked down here but couldn't hear any more. Have I heard wrongly?

Oh my God! I saw animals in a small cave. I approached and saw 2 puppies and a bird. The 2 puppies are very young and have not opened their eyes. Why are the two dogs and the bird in this cave? I touched the bird and found it to be quite obedient. The land here only needs to be lightly touched, and the risk of landslides is very high. It's dangerous to have these dogs here. I'm going to get these two dogs out of this place and bring them back to take care of. The dogs are very pretty but their faces are covered with dirty sand. I was about to take the 2 puppies home right away but I thought of the other bird and decided to let the 2 dogs lie in the grass.

I turned around and let the bird fly, but it couldn't fly. I decided to grab the hay to enter the cave for the bird to lie there waiting for its mother. I hope the bird's mother will return to take care of her baby. Thank you birds for protecting these 2 puppies. Immediately after that I took 2 puppies home to take care of. Keeping a very young puppy like this is very difficult but I will try. I brought them home with 2 dogs. Fortunately they eat very well, hoping they will stay healthy.
