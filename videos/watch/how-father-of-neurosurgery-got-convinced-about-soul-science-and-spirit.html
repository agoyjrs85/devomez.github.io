In this video it is described that the concept of soul is not just based on faith, rather it has a scientific basis.

He conducted this experiment for more than 40 years to disprove the existence of the soul. However, in the end, he realized that Consciousness is not part of the brain rather it exists outside of the Human Brain and that is how he got converted from a materialist to a dualist.
