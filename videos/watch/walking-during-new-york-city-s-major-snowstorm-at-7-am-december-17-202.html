Tropical storm Gail has covered New York City with over nine inches of snow. Walking during New York City's major snowstorm at 7AM, I was able to capture different aspects of the city. The video starts at the intersection of 7th Avenue (Fashion Avenue) and West 38th Street. Most small businesses were open for customers while others remained closed. New York City and private business employees cleared streets and sidewalks frequently. Walking during New York City's major snowstorm at 7 AM felt much harsher than the previous night.
