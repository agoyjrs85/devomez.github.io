Tropical storm Gail has covered New York City with over nine inches of snow. Walking during New York City's major snowstorm at 7AM, I was able to capture different aspects of the city. New York City and private business employees cleared streets and sidewalks frequently. Walking during New York City's major snowstorm at 7 AM felt much harsher than the previous night.
