INFO:
I have been drumming for 7 weeks and playing open handed for 3 days. (I find open handed so much easier. Another great discovery in Drumeo courses!) I am playing the full track heel down. I can play heel up so I am trying heel down dynamics to save the strain on an old knee injury.

I omitted toms as the track sounded better to me without ( I was going to strike the floor tom and snare instead of the double snare shots but it did not sound as good so sorry toms you took a back seat). I will continue to work on tom sections to compliment the track.

I orchestrated and then practiced the parts for about a week.
