Brave - Merida has a daughter and they ride Angus while practicing archery! Merida is now the queen of DunBroch and she decides her own fate, raising her daughter to run free like in Touch The Sky!

Hi everybody^___^ How are you? Merida from the Disney movie Brave is the last of the Disney Princesses to get a family! So now I'll probably start on the other Disney girls and villains, haha :) Merida was the very first Pixar princess and I was torn between a baby and an older daughter, but decided that if Merida has a daughter, she would be very restricted in her movements and it would take a lot of time away from being Queen, so I thought it would be better if Merida has a daughter who is older, adopted from an orphanage. I imagine the willow of the wisps led Merida (and Angus) to the little girl, who had the same hair and wild spirit as herself. Seeing this as a sign, Merida took the little girl in and is now raising her as her own daughter, heir to DunBroch in Scotland^___^
I didnt include any bears, because I think both Elinor, Merida's mother, and Merida herself have gotten enough of bears for a while, haha. Instead, Merida's daughter carries a necklace with an eagle symbolizing her free spirit :) What do you think of this Happy Ever After for Disney Princess Merida?
