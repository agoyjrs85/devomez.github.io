A note from Damian:
Late last year, we dedicated a cover of the Zombie’s classic “This Will Be Our Year” to everyone who doesn’t share our politics or our world view. Now we’re releasing it with none of the politics attached, so we can all look forward together as the miserable year 2020 recedes in the rear view.
"This Will Be Our Year” is one of my favorite songs of all time, and it’s more timely than ever. After such a catastrophic year — so much that we rely on just crumbling beneath our feet — it feels good to add a drop to the world’s bucket of hope and optimism. Like the song says, this year was a long time coming, and I pray we look back on it as the time when things got better.
