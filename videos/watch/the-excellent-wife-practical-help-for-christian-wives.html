This video was the last ladies meeting I led at GCC in San Antonio. We covered aspects of what it means to be an excellent wife and many practical ways to apply biblical principles. I highly recommend the book “The Excellent Wife” by Martha Peace mainly because it’s packed with scripture. It was an absolute privilege to teach the women of GCC for so many years and to witness up close the transforming power of God as they diligently sought to be holy, humble, obedient women of the Word with the glory of God as their chief aim. Love and miss you all! ❤️
