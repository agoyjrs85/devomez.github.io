Enforce was a band from Baltimore that was formed by Nick Ellingson and Cliff Saunders back in 1987. The line up consisted of Lonnie Fletcher - vocals, Nick Ellingson - Guitar, Jody Lewis - Bass, Charles Parker - Keys, and Cliff Saunders - Drums. The band played the east coast and opened for acts like Dream Theater, Pat Travers, and headlined several A clubs. The band recorded 2 demo tapes and were included in the CD compilation "Metal Molten Monsters 1" created by Keith Menser of Seigen records. Their original music was compared to Queensryche, Dream Theater, and Fates Warning. The band finally disbanded in 1993 after Heavy Metal music diminished with rap and grunge taking the front.
