The Wehea Dayak is a sub-ethnic Dayak group from the Muara Wahau District in East Kutai Regency, which is characterised by rich and beautiful traditions. Through an international effort, the BOS Foundation helped to bring the Wehea Dayak under the spotlight at Kaltim Expo 2020 so that the rest of the world could discover this mesmerizing culture.
