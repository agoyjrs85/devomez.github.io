New Week, New Theme! Here's to New Beginnings!!! We're going to call this week "Unfamiliar" and the proposition behind these exercises is a simple one. Instead of understanding our subject matter as being "difficult to paint", we can acknowledge that it is, perhaps, something that we're not particularly familiar with. It could even be, like today, comprised of shapes that we are used to looking at, but just presented in a way that makes them seem challenging and new.

Throughout this week, we're going to try, emphasis on TRY, give the old a new coat of paint and attempt to see it with fresh eyes!
