Causes of frequent urination can be many including medical conditions. Very common causes are drinking lots of water, anxiety can cause frequent urination. There are so many medical conditions which can cause frequent urination are diabetes, now a days in any age group diabetes is seen, frequent urination can be first symptom of diabetes. Children can also be affected by frequent urination due to diabetes as well. There are so many nuerological disorders which may cause frequent urination, these conditions are especially seen in elderly paople. They can consult physician to get rid of frequent urination. If there is neurological cause in younger age group men or women, it might be due to the trauma and nerves affected due to not having bladder control. Frequent urination in men can be due to compression over urethra which causes stagnancy of urine in bladder causing frequent urination in elderly men. Urine infection also causes most frequent urination in young people, it can be bladder infection, kidney infection or urethral infection. Person might have stone in the kidney or bladder, stone which is skipped from kidney to urethra causes obscursion leads to frequent urination. Anxiety itself causes frequent urination, you just have to watch carefully your symptoms and visit a physician to know the exact cause and get treated.
