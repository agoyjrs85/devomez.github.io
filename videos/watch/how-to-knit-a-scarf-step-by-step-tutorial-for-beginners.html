Want to learn how to knit but literally don't know how? This is the video for you! In this tutorial we take you from total beginner to finished scarf.

In this video we go through:

2.09 - 6.27: How to hold your needles + casting on (AKA how to get your woolly stitches onto your needles)
6.28 - 15.57: How to do the basic knit stitch / garter stitch
17.17 - 20.00: How to cast off (AKA finish off your project)
20.03 - 22.15: Weave in your ends
22.32 - 23.10: BLOOPERS!

This tutorial works for all types of yarn and all sizes needles but should you want to make the same scarf as us, here are the links for our chunky yarn and big needles, perfect for beginner projects:
