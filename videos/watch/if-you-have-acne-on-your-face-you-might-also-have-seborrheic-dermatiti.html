In Lee Moon Won Clinic, we have been treating hair loss patients for a long period of time and it has been noticed that mostly all the patients with seborrheic dermatitis have acne.

That is why it is important for people who have acne to be aware that it is easy for them to have seborrheic dermatitis and, eventually, seborrheic hair loss.
