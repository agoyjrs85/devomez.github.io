Today we are learning about how to use and meaning of THERE IS and THERE ARE. (How to use There Is and There Are in English | by Socheat Thin)
THERE IS and THERE ARE are used to say that something exists.
Positive sentences
We use:
there is + singular
there are + plural.
— There is a book on the table.
— There are five books on the table.
— There is one car.
— There are 5 employees in the office today.
— There is a beautiful girl working on the street.
— There are many kids playing together.
— There are many cows at the rice field.
—

We also use
There is + uncountable noun
— There is ice cream in the fridge.
— There is gasoline in the battle.
— There is some sugar in the bag.

Negative form:
To form a negative, NOT is put after There is and There are
— There is not a phone on my desk.
— There is not a gas station on this road.
— There are not 10 elephants in the zoo.
— There are not many students coming to class today.
Question form:
— Is there a dog in the market? - No, there isn't.
— Are there any dogs in the national park? - Yes, there are.
— Is there a guard in the phone shop? - Yes, there is.
— Are there any tigers in the this forest? - No, there aren't.
— Is there any ice-cream in the fridge? - Yes, there is.
Question form with HOW MANY
How many + plural noun + are there (+ complement).
— How many dogs are there?
— How many students are there the class?
— How many countries are there in Asia?
