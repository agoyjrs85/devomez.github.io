IMPORTANT ADVISORY
ALL airport passengers MUST download and register an account in the TRAZE Contact Tracing App beginning 28 November 2020, prior to entering the airport.
In the raging battle against COVID-19, Cosmotech Philippines in cooperation with the Philippine Ports Authority (PPA) Launched Traze Contact Tracing App

TRAZE is a nationwide and unified contact tracing app that automates manual contact tracing by using QR code scanning such that the tracing process done in several days can now be achieved in just a few minutes.

An in-app notification will be sent to all who may have come in contact with an identified Covid-19 positive patient so that they can observe precautions and start self-isolation if symptoms occur to protect their families and loved ones.
