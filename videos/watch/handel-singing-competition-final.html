Treat yourself to an evening of pure delight, and see if you can spot a star of tomorrow. Our four finalists (Jerome Knox, Jessica Cale, Ruairi Bowen and Morgan Pearse) represent the very best young baroque singers who are required to present a varied all-Handel programme and are accompanied by the London Handel Orchestra, directed by Laurence Cummings. Past finalists of the international Handel Singing Competition have included Iestyn Davies and Lucy Crowe. In 2020 we had record number of applications, with 190 singers applying from 32 countries all over the world.

Please note that this is a live performance behind closed doors, with safe distancing between all the musicians, in strict compliance with current UK rules around lockdown.

For more details, please visit: https://www.london-handel-festival.com/about-us/handel-during-lockdown/
