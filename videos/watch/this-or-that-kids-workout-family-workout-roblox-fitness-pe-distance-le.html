Directions: Students have 10 seconds to choose which of the 2 options is their favorite, and they step to either side of the room or area to show their choice (divide area in half with cones, rope, etc). Then another timer appears for 30 seconds along with an exercise GIF depending on which choice they made. There are 10 slides of choices.
