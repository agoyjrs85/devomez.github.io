What do I love about aviation? Is it the beautiful views? Or the feeling of accomplishment after a challenging flight? Or the wonder of transiting large distances in minimal time? But, CitationMax saved the day as he offered to come in from NYC and fly me up to the show in his brand new Citation M2. It was a wonderfully enjoyable time and Max is quite an accomplished pilot especially considering he just started flying four years ago!
