I'm Fly Fishing A TOP 50 Tailwater in the Winter for the Most Beautiful Rainbow Trout! One of my favorite times to fly fish in Arkansas is during the winter months. As long as the generation schedule is favorable, the consistent flows and low crowds usually mean quality fishing can be had when many other places aren't worth visiting. The day started under 20f but warmed to just over 40f by noon. The bite started slow as expected but picked up mid afternoon. In the video, I explain the set up and flies used...and targeted the slower deep pools off faster flows. Started with an tandem nymph rig using an attractor nymph as my first fly, frenchie, GCHR and evil olive with a small midge or baeits as a dropper. All flies produced, the evil olive was probably the most productive.
