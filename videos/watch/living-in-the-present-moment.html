The present moment is a unique gift from God, yet so often, rather than live in the present, we pine for another time in our lives, focusing on future goals or better times from the past. In this video, Fr. Mike Schmitz challenges us to notice the “sacrament of the present moment” and see what graces God wants us to enjoy right now.
