BORIS Johnson today told parents schools are safe - but SAGE warns kids are seven times more likely to bring Covid home.

The Prime Minister said "the threat to young people is very, very small, the risk to staff is very small" in a debate on schools reopening amid a surge in coronavirus case

Speaking on the Andrew Marr Show the PM said: "I understand people's frustrations, I understand people's anxieties, but there is no doubt in my mind schools are safe."
