Bear hunting with hounds in the mountains of West Virginia during early bear season. This ones for the HOUNDS! Follow along as team member Hunter LeMasters travels into the mountains of West Virginia to meet up with his buddy Spike for the first two days of early bear season. Spike and the entire back-fork crew did what they do best and let their well trained hounds start screaming at first light. Nothing in the area was safe. With a quick start to day one, treeing the first bear shortly after 7am, how the rest of the weekend was going to unfold was set in stone right then and there. Action packed with five hunters (team member Hunter being one) getting to harvest their first bear! This video is not your typical hunting film. This is aimed more towards following these bear hunters around and shooting footage as they do what they do best and showing as much hound footage as possible. Bear hunting with hounds can bring everyone together for a great experience in the outdoors as this video shows. If you ever get the chance to tag along on a hunt like this we highly recommend giving it a go! For the entire week of Early Season, this crew and their packs of hounds treed 16 bear and harvested 8 of them. Other hunters tagging along during the week were able to harvest their first bear as well. These guys live for bear hunting. It's their way of life. We appreciate everyone who helped make this video possible and hope we can capture some more bear hunting footage in the future.
