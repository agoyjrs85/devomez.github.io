EVERYDAY LIFE FAILS

There are a lot of everyday awkward moments every girl understands. We are not just talking about bad hair days, makeup fails and awkward dates. Watch this video and find a lot of funny situations that happened to every girl at least once. Enjoy these compilations of fails. Sometimes you don’t have a mirror in your bag but need to check your makeup? And you see a car and use a car window as a mirror in the street. It’s such an embarrassing moment when a window suddenly opens. Another fail is when you’ve shaved your legs in the morning and the most annoying thing is that hair appear suddenly again in a most inappropriate moment, for example, when you are relaxing in the swimming pool. You may look stunning in jumpsuit but it’s the most confusing garment ever! Because you have to sit half-naked on the toilet.
Sometimes we are not ready to share a cake with friends - cut a small piece for your friend and eat the rest by yourself. How do you usually spend your weekend? You definitely want to relax and read a favorite book during the weekend but in reality, you clean your house, wash clothes and cook food. Don’t forget to relax during weekends!
Check out more awkward moments that may happen at the grocery store: someone standing in front of the item you need and you need to wait for a long time; you wanted to buy pasta but collected a lot of items and of course forgot to take a cart; sometimes it’s colder than in the Arctic when you stand near the supermarket refrigerator and need to choose the item you need as fast as possible.
