EVERYDAY LIFE FAILS

There are a lot of everyday awkward moments every girl understands. We are not just talking about bad hair days, makeup fails and awkward dates. Watch this video and find a lot of funny situations that happened to every girl at least once. Enjoy these compilations of fails. Sometimes you don’t have a mirror in your bag but need to check your makeup? And you see a car and use a car window as a mirror in the street. It’s such an embarrassing moment when a window suddenly opens. Another fail is when you’ve shaved your legs in the morning and the most annoying thing is that hair appear suddenly again in a most inappropriate moment, for example, when you are relaxing in the swimming pool.
