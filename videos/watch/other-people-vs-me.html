EVERYDAY LIFE FAILS

There are a lot of everyday awkward moments every girl understands. We are not just talking about bad hair days, makeup fails and awkward dates. It’s such an embarrassing moment when a window suddenly opens. Another fail is when you’ve shaved your legs in the morning and the most annoying thing is that hair appear suddenly again in a most inappropriate moment, for example, when you are relaxing in the swimming pool.
