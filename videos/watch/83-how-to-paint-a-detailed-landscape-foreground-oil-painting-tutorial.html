In this oil painting tutorial I'll show you how to paint a detailed landscape foreground. I will focus on how to paint detailed grass along a river, where you will learn the brush techniques used for painting grass and how to mix green oil colours for painting realistic grass.

I'm a professional landscape artist with over 20 years of experience in oil painting. My YouTube Channel is dedicated to helping people learn how to paint in oils. I really believe that everyone can learn how to paint!
