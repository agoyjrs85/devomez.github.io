In this video, I explain how your existence is intimately connected with the existence of God, which in turn proves the existence of God. Many Spiritual Seekers who haven't yet got a glimpse of their Divine Nature sometimes entertain the doubt about the existence of God. They start thinking, does God really exist, or is it just the figment of my own imagination? If you're one of them who are doubting the existence of God, then I would like to ask you a very simple question, you don't need to answer it right now, but just ponder over it, be with the question for sometime and it will reveal something which will change your life forever, of course in a positive way, it will undoubtedly prove to you the Existence of God, the existence of the Divine Reality. And that question is, have you ever experienced your own absence? I'll repeat it, have you ever, ever in your life experienced you own absence? And the answer is, no, no you have never experienced your own absence. Now those who think that they experience their absence in sleep, then to them I would say, no, you don't experience your absence, it's rather the absence of the body that you experience, it's the absence of the mind that you experience. The moment you leave this waking state in sleep, you loose all consciousness of your body, but you are still conscious in the dream that your mind creates, there in the dream, the mind creates another body with which you identify, you don't even miss this physical body of yours, all your cares and worries, stress and anxieties are left behind, the moment you fall asleep. Now what does it all show? It clearly shows that your existence is not dependent on this physical body that you refer to as "I", whenever you say "I" all you mean is this body, right! So the sleep experience proves that with the death of the body, you don't die, because you are not the body! Now the question must be arising in your mind, if I'm not the body, am I the mind, then? No, you are also not the mind, because even this mind disappears in deep dreamless sleep, but you continue to exist. Some of you may be thinking, how do I know if I exist in deep sleep? If you really think that you don't exist in deep sleep then how are you able to say that I slept like a log, I slept like baby, when you say that all you mean is that you had deep dreamless sleep, you experience the bliss of deep sleep, right! After having a sumptuous meal, you say that the dinner was fantastic, now this fantastic dinner thing came out as a result of the sumptuous meal that you had, you were present there to have it, similarly, when you say, I had a deep dreamless sleep, what it means is that you were present there to experience it, which in turn means that you exist in deep sleep, without the body, without the mind, and this clearly proves that you are not the body, you are not the mind, you are the Consciousness which expresses through this body and mind, you are the Consciousness which moves through the three states of waking, dreaming and deep sleep, not getting affected by them at all.
