We, The Voyagers, are glad to present to you our final addition to our debit album, Lolak. 'Oh Maarie' is an original rendition written and composed by us. The arrangement is an wonderful combination of Vasai folklore and 80's Bollywood vibe!

We all have been heavily influenced by old-school Bollywood some way or other. Often by the style, the aura, the retro-feel. The classic and heavily cliched love stories that we have all enjoyed. This song is our take on a similar classic and dramatic love-story.

The video focuses on two individuals, Jaani and Maarie, and the exhilarating nostalgic story that follows.

Hope you like it!
