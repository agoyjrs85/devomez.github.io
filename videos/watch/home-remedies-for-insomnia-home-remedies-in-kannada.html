Insomnia is a common conditions which can be caused due to physical or/and medical conditions, unhealthy sleep habits, stress and depression, other factors.

Home remedy for insomnia (ನಿದ್ರಾಹೀನತೆ )
remedies for good sleep
FOr good night sleep along with this remedy, it is important practice discipline(good habits), balanced diet, exercise, and positive thinking
around 20 to 25 doddapatre/ sambar balli leaves
1/2 tbsp honey
1/2 cup water
grind the leaves to a fine paste adding enough water
Strain the juice to a cup, add honey. mix it well.
Drink this juice before going to sleep in the night
Remedy 2:
1 cup water
around 20 mint leaves
soak the mint leaves in the water for minimum 1/2 an hour.
Drink this water before going to bed in the night
Remedy 3:
1 tsp jeera/cumin seeds
1/4 tsp ghee
1 banana cut into small pieces
Heat the ghee in a ladle, fry the jeera powder until you get a nice aroma. turn off the heat and add this to the banana pieces. mix it well. Eat this before going to bed in the night
