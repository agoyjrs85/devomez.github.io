The first full length trailer for Netflix "We Can Be Heroes" has landed!

ABOUT WE CAN BE HEROES: When alien invaders kidnap Earth's superheroes, their kids are whisked away to a government safe house. But whip-smart tween Missy Moreno (Yaya Gosselin) will stop at nothing to rescue her superhero dad, Marcus Moreno (Pedro Pascal). Missy teams up with the rest of the superkids to escape their mysterious government babysitter, Ms. Granada (Priyanka Chopra Jonas). If they're going to save their parents, they'll have to work together by using their individual powers — from elasticity to time control to predicting the future — and form an out-of-this-world team.

"We Can Be Heroes" Stars Boyd Holbrook, Christian Slater, Chris McDonald, and Adriana Barraza and is directed by Robert Rodriguez.
