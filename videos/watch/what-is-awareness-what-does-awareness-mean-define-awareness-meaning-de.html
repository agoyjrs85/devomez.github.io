What is AWARENESS and what does it mean? Understanding Awareness (meaning and definition) is very important for all spiritual truth seekers in this modern age. Awareness is one's very capacity for experiencing. It is fundamental and primary to all life and all of existence. Awareness is light, the light of consciousness that illuminates the mind. It is the foundation of the spirit, the soul, the true Self. Becoming more aware of the inner workings of the mind, the thinking process, the senses, the perception is part of the spiritual awakening process. As one grows in Self awareness through such spiritual practices as meditation, contemplation, mindfulness, study, prayer, surrender, conscious breathing, devotion, letting go, and others, the boundaries of the mind and one's consciousness will expand. Raising awareness will increase one's capacity to learn, understand, concentrate, and contemplate the higher nature of Reality. One perceives, focuses, and narrows one's view of reality through limiting beliefs and programs. Deep knowledge is healing and transformative. We exist within an Infinite Field of Consciousness. This field of Universal Awareness (God, Source, the Supreme) is everything, literally. We are all connected, we are all manifestations of the One. Developing Awareness of self, of others, of life, of awareness itself, is necessary to take full advantage of this most precious human lifetime. One may increase one's awareness and raise one's level of consciousness through many Religious, or Spiritual practices. The Eastern traditions of Buddhism, Zen, Hinduism, Yoga, Sikhism, Taoism, Jainism, and others all have foundational and fundamental spiritual teachings and practices. The Western traditions also have many contemplative branches. Contemplation and meditation can be found within Christianity, Islam (Sufism), and Judaism (Kabballah, Zohar). Modern New Age spiritual seekers are no longer limited in their quest for awakening to the Religion of their physical ancestors. From the dimension of spirit all of Humanity is one, and so all traditions of the past are ancestral to us all. The greatest Enlightened Beings of all time all wanted us to make our way home, they all pointed to the same Truth. Pristine Awareness is the source of the highest wisdom, knowledge, insight, intuition, and realization. It is the True Self, the Higher Self, it is Being and Knowing beyond the thinking mind. The Source of "I Am" and "I know that I Am." The benefits of awareness are truly great for oneself and the World. It is healing, curative, a stress reducer, helps to decrease negative thoughts, helps to increase happiness, self worth, contentment, love, empathy, compassion, and peace of mind. Transcend the limitations of the ego thinking mind. Raise your level of consciousness and Raise the World.
