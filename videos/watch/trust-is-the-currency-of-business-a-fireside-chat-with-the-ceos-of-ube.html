For any leader at any company, trust is central to success. Trust with employees. Trust with stakeholders. And, most importantly, trust with customers. Yet, it’s becoming harder for businesses to maintain that trust. Uber Technologies CEO Dara Khosrowshahi joins Salesforce Co-CEO Keith Block for a conversation about this new reality — that trust is the currency of business. From innovation to product launch to customer engagement, every business decision is an opportunity to reinforce trust or lose it forever. From their different perspectives, Dara & Keith will look at building a culture of trust from the ground up, to maintaining trust as the #1 value as a company grows. They’ll share advice and look ahead at the challenges facing the next generation of executives
