Our essential oils guide is an easy to use, completely searchable platform chock full

of customizable features and tools. This all-in-one reference guide for essential oils

is the only one you will ever need.

This guide is non-biased and not associated with any company or distributor of

essential oils. A great fit for anyone no matter the goals or brand-loyalty. The

customizable features allow for all users to make the app their own!

What makes our app different?

OUR BEST FEATURES

- Can be used as a Sales Tool to keep track of leads and contacts

- Inventory library, My Oils, allows users to keep track of every oil they own

- Sharing your oils with friends? Keep track of what you shared & with whom

- Create and save your own essential oil recipes and blends

It’s Essential app is perfect for any level of essential oils user. There is useful

information and tools for everyone. From the EO newbie who just wants to know

the basics and how to use essential oils, to the veteran user using essential oils daily

for years, to the sales consultant who wants to keep track of leads and share

valuable EO information on the go.

New to using essential oils? Learn the basic information you will need to get started.

Find uses and detailed descriptions of the most popular and common essential oils

such as fan-favorite, Frankincense oil, and the must-haves Lavender, Peppermint,

and Lemon. Learn how and when to use them.

You will find the following information most useful as you get started:

- Oil Basics guide full of information and frequently asked questions

- List of 80 essential oils in the Oil Index

- Common Uses section to help you find the essential oil to fit your need

Features for advanced users and oil sales consultants:

Staying organized is important for oil sales consultants so we have made a power

sections to help keep everything in top order. Using the advanced options in the My

Oils section allows users to keep important information in one place for quick and

easy access.

- My Oils shows your private inventory and allows you to see how many of

each item you have in stock

- As an add-on to this feature, we have added a Sharing Tool. The

Sharing Tool allows you to keep record of what oils and in exact

amounts that you shared and gave to a friend.

- My Blends allows for the creation of your own essential oil recipes as well as

aromatherapy recipes. Be inventive!

- Use the Notes section to jot down thoughts or reminders quickly and easily

- The Contact section stores and keeps all contacts organized by category and

alphabetically by name
