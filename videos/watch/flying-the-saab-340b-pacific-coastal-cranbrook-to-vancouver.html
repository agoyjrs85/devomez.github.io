Back in August 2020, I flew from Cranbrook to Vancouver with British Columbia's airline - Pacific Coastal!

Although I've flown the WestJet Link Saabs twice now, I haven't flown on a proper Pacific Coastal Saab before - see what I thought of it on this super scenic flight within British Columbia!
