Build at Meyer and Neptun Shipyards in Germany, AIDAnova is the first of a new generation of LNG Powered Cruise Ships for Carnival Cruises - the Helios Class. The vessel is 337m Long and can house 6.600 passengers.

We filmed the entire construction process with our advanced MK V4 timelapse cameras, shooting 40mp images every single minute.
