This is new level fish trap using plastic pipe & a piece of wood. They make totally new system to catch country fish from very beautiful canal.

They used about 10-12 feet plastic pipe & about 1 feet wood piece. They make trapping tools first then put some fish food in the plastic hole.

When fish come in the plastic pipe to eat food they just pulled the wood to trap the fish under plastic and then collect the fish from pipe.
