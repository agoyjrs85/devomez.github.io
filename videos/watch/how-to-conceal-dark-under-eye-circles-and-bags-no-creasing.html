As some of you already know, my biggest beauty issue is dark under eye circles and bags. They drive me nuts!!!! If I don't put a concealer on I always get questions about my health and fatigue. It took me a very long time to find a way to conceal my under eye area and not get the product to crease.

-I use organic green tea bag in the morning to reduce the puffiness under my eye. I put them in the fridge for 20-30 min.

-I have recently added jade roller to my skin care routine and I find it to be very effective in reducing puffiness under my eyes and all around my face.
