Learn to use "the future will form" In the second half of this video, you will practice on your own. This will help you build your speaking and fluency skills. Each conversation is illustrated for better understanding. Have fun learning and speaking English now.

Language in use:
What will you do today? I will go shopping.
What will you do today? I will go to the beach.
What will you do today? I will ride my bicycle.
What will you do today? I will work.
What will you do today? I will go to the park.
What will you do today? I will practice the piano.
What will you do today? I will listen to the radio.
What will you do today? I will go to the pool.
