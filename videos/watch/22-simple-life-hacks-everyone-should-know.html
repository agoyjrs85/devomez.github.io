HOW TO SOLVE ALL OF YOUR PROBLEMS WITH THESE AMAZING TRICKS



One item can have a ton of uses and today I give you some ideas on how to solve all of your problems using these incredible hacks.

You can also use a swirly hair tie as a key chain for your keys to avoid losing them or dropping them.

If you don’t have laundry detergent, you can grate a soap bar and add it to your laundry.

Take a plastic bottle and cut it diagonally to create a container that will collect all the dust.

To iron a shirt quickly, simply boil some water and then place your shirt on the counter to iron it.
