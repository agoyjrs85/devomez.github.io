The two typical measures of downside risk focus on only the "bad" dispersion: Semi-deviation squares returns below the MEAN return, while downside deviation squares returns below a TARGET return (aka, minimum acceptable return, MAR). The Sortino Ratio divides by the downside deviation. [Here is my excel http://trtl.bz/1109-yt-sortino-frm-t1-12-xs]
