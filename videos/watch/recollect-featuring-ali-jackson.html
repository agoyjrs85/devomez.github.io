In RECOLLECT, Blue Engine Records takes some of the world's greatest jazz musicians on record shopping sprees, digging up forgotten favorites and certified classics in the process. In this episode, drummer and Jazz at Lincoln Center Orchestra member ALI JACKSON talks Miles, Duke, and Dizzy. Plus, he has a chance encounter with someone who has a high school connection to Wynton Marsalis...
