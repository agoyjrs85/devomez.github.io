2020 Tata Nexon BS6 launched in India! 2020 Tata Nexon Facelift BS6 is sub 4-metre compact SUV from Tata Motors. 2020 Nexon BS6 Facelift is the first major facelift for Tata Nexon since it was launched. Instead of slapping the BS6 engine in the Nexon, Tata gave the Nexon a major facelift with additional features. Competition is tight in the segment thanks to Hyundai Venue, Ford Ecosport BS6, Maruti Suzuki Vitara Brezza, Mahindra XUV300 BS6. Even Kia QYI/ Sonet, Renault HBC/Kinger & Nissan are coming in the segment. So, Facelift Nexon 2020 BS6 is need of an hour! Tata motors design team did an awesome job. New Nexon 2020 looks like a whole new car! Gone are the cute little looks and comes in meaner, stronger, Ranger Rover like looks! 2020 Tata Nexon exterior gets all new redesigned front bumper. Tri-Arrow design theme is common on the Nexon. Look at the headlamps LED DRLs, front air dam, side cladding, LED tail lamp, everywhere that tr-arrow design is significant. (Reminds me of Lamborghini Design language!) 2020 Nexon Facelift BS6 gets all new design 16" diamond cut alloys which looks superb. Rear end is somewhat similar but looks upmarket now. New Nexon BS6 2020 interior also gets some tweaks like new flatbottom steering wheel, all digital instrument cluster, floating touchscreen infotainment system with Apple CarPlay & Android Auto. Seats gets new 3D design fabric upholstery like New Tata Altroz. Rear seats gets armrest and cupholders, rear AC vents, but no power outlet. Top model Tata Nexon gets sunroof also. Featuring in the video is 2020 Tata Nexon BS6 XZ+ second top model. Watch the video for more information about the 2020 Nexon XZ Plus BS6 Facelift foliage green colour.
