Once upon a time Seiko was the king of the affordable entry level watch world. However, over recent years we’ve seen a steady increase in their price, leaving many watch fans, first time buyers, and those seeking affordable and reliable timepieces to look elsewhere. One company that has remained consistent in offering the best value, reliability, and accuracy is Casio.

But the best thing is you can get all of this from as little as $10 to $50.
