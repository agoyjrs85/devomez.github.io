Religious leaders in the North West, in partnership with other role players in society are calling upon the public to intensify the fight against Gender-Based Violence. They emphasised that it is everybody's responsibility to take a stand against the scourge. The call was made at a two-day Gender-Based Violence and Femicide Dialogue, in Dinokana village in Lehurutshe near Zeerust in the North West. The dialogue was also part of commemorating 16 Days of No Violence Against Women and Children.
