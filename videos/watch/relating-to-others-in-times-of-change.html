Relating to Others in Times of Change

Worried about Nothing Podcast 42 - conversation with Danielle, Katya and Kim, 31st January 2021

~ Worried About Nothing & Nothing to Worry About ~

Weekly conversations (and individual podcasts) with Danielle, Katya and Kim (and friends) about topics ranging from spirituality, to meditation, to fears and worries, and other subjects in between.

Today we spoke about relating to others in times of change. How in the crazy topsy-turvy times we are living in, relating to those around us can sometimes feel more like a battlefield than a connection of love. How it can be easy to either get bogged down in others' fears and beliefs, or dismiss them out of hand. 

We talked about how the most important thing is to relate from a place of truth and compassion, and zoom out to look at the bigger picture, remembering that we are all one, rather than focussing on the minutiae of the micro-details. How it is important not to get stuck in rigid thinking or blind unfounded beliefs. And that we will feel what is happening in the collective consciousness, and to remember that this is happening. We can love others without wanting to engage with them. We can love others without agreeing with them. That the most important thing is to keep turning towards the Light.
