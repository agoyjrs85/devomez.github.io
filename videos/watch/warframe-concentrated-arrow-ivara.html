This augment is actually terrifyingly powerful - assuming you can aim with a bow.

NOTES:

The augment's explosion range scales with power range. Unsure if the crit bonus scales with power strength. Explosion radius does not seem to scale with Firestorm. Explosion does not deal self damage :)))

Lenz stat stick build: You can swap the elemental mods for dual stat viral mods, if you want to focus on Viral + Slash procs.

Arcane Acceleration and Rage both work on Artemis Bow, but I personally recommend Acceleration as it makes life slightly more forgiving.
