In this Film Courage video Masterclass, film producer Mark Heidelberger talks about script development and film production, along with stories about initial growing pains of his 20 + year career. He shares tips with the class on how to "just get the film made," urging students not to to be so precious about how it is made and quelling expectations for one's first few independent films.
