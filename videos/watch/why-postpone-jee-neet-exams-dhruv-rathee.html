Despite lakhs of students and their parents requesting to postpone the JEE and NEET Exams, the Indian government and Supreme Court refused to do that and they are scheduled to take place in September 13 (NEET) and September 1st - September 6th (JEE). In this video I explain why it is a bad decision considering the COVID-19 situation, the transportation problems and the ongoing floods in Bihar and Assam.
