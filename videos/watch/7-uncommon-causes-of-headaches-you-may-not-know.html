According to the World Health Organization, 50 to 75% of the adults in the world suffer from headaches. Did you know that?

However, most people don't pay much attention to this issue since headaches can range from moderate to intense, sharp, or overwhelming, and their focus might be in different parts of the head.

But did you know that headaches can happen because of a disease or even be a secondary symptom?

0:00 7 Uncommon Causes Of Headaches

0:54 Effect of tyramine
Tyramine, which is found in bananas, bacon, avocados, and aromatic cheeses is a toxin that can cause migraines.

1:13 Benzyl alcohol
Found in perfumes and air fresheners, combined with synthetic compounds, benzyl alcohol can cause headaches.

1:29 Medication overdose
Headaches can be either chronic or periodic. And they might also be followed by vomiting, accelerated heartbeats, anxiety, and nervousness. This state can lead to the irresponsible use of analgesics.

2:05 Encephalitis
In cases of encephalitis, inflammatory processes are developed in the brain. People think that this disease is caused by tick bites, but that's not always the case.

2:33 Glaucoma
Do you have headaches near your eyeballs and temples and suffer from photophobia? Also, do you usually have symptoms like nausea, fever, and double vision? This might be glaucoma!

2:59 Intracranial hypertension
This rare disease might also be called "pseudotumor cerebri." It is a strong intracranial pressure that comes accompanied by headaches and a decrease in vision.

3:26 Meningitis
With basically any infectious disease, people suffer from headaches in varying degrees of intensity.
