It's a mystery that has intrigued Americans for centuries: What happened to the lost colonists of North Carolina's Roanoke Island? Theories about the disappearance have ranged from an mass alien abductions, to annihilating disease and violent rampage by local Native American tribes. But now, a clue has been found!
