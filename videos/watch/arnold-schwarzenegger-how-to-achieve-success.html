Hollywood actor and former California governor, Arnold Schwarzenegger, in an exclusive interview at the Hindustan Times Leadership Summit 2014, in Delhi, offers the key ingredients to becoming successful in life.

The 67-year-old also talks about how weightlifting has taught him one of life's crucial lessons, that to succeed you have to keep working hard while being prepared for failure.

Schwarzenegger also speaks about why it is important to ignore the naysayers and believe in yourself as you pursue your goal single-mindedly.
