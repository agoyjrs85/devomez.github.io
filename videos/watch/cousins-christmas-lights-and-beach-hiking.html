NickyTV is the engine that is going to drive our future endeavors for Nicholas and the special needs community in general. We are going to blink our eyes and Nick is going to be 21 years old, and his support team will pretty much cease to exist anywhere near the levels of today. It's up to Shannon and me to keep that from happening. Additionally, when he ages out of the public school system, I will retire from teaching and seek to create employment and entrepreneurial opportunities for Nicholas and others with special needs. If you are interested, take a look at the Rising Tides Car Wash and John's Crazy Socks. We are going to start several companies that resemble, and improve upon, these two amazing organizations. At this point, we have plans for a sleepwear company, an event DJ company, and a pizza store. These kinds of initiatives will take a tremendous amount of planning, commitment, and the biggest factor of all, money. If you have the means to do so, please consider making a donation.
