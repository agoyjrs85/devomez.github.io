I'm Patrick, my wife Leslie and I have been enjoying living the #vanlife! We designed and built our van / trailer camper and this video is a detailed look at how we maximized solar power collection for minimum cost in our off-grid solar camp trailer. I show how you can use Inkscape, a free vector graphics tool, to plan the physical layout of solar panels. I also hit the pros and cons of different panel array configurations.
