Dr. Brannon shares about the distinctives of the Biblical Studies major at Belhaven University. At the heart of the Biblical Studies degree is the conviction that the Bible is God’s Word and that it is the final authority for faith (what we believe about God) and life (how we are called to live). At Belhaven learn from faculty who teach all curriculum through the lens of a Christian worldview, who care for their students’ growth and success, who have a heart for God's Word, and who teach small classes that allow for intimate interaction. Learn more about the Biblical Studies degree at https://www.belhaven.edu/academics/Bible.
