The Dark Souls franchise is something special. Dark Souls III is teeming with details and I hope I've shown you at least a few things that you didn't know before - even you veterans out there! As usual, the idea is to break the footage down into small chunks that are roughly chronological, so that by the end it's like a complete playthrough rather than a patchwork of unconnected facts.

All of the music is from the game itself and I got all of the footage from the PS4 version (except where footage credits have been given). As for the discoveries, I've tried my best to give credit where I can, and ultimately this video would have been impossible without such a great community behind it. Thank you for watching and for being a part of that community!

"Only 183 this time?"
Many of the discoveries from the DS1 video carry over to DS3, but I've not included many of those because I expect viewer overlap. I was also a bit harsher with my quality threshold this time around.
