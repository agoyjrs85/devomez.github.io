SiddhYogi Guruji Vishalbhai Pandya is explaining through an example that no two human mind think in same way even if they are twin brothers or sisters. But, for animals it is almost he same irrespective of their locations / continents. They have fixed set of lifestyle which they will follow since start of the day till end of the day. Because, they live within certain upper & lower fixed bandwidth intuitive processes set for them by nature.

Another important concept Guruji shared with us is, we have to learn and must adopt practice of staying with ourselves without any external support or objects as that is the one thing which will be useful for us as we move forward in our life. That does not mean stop all your social connectivity but, have some practice with own self to to get adjusted and accommodated with self through the practice of meditation.
