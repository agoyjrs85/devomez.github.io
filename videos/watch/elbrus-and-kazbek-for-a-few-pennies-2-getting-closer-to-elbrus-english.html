In August 2018 we went to the south of Russia and the north of Georgia to climb two 5000-meter summits: Elbrus (5642m, the highest of Europe) and Kazbek (5033m). Additionally, we visited friends in Kakhetia and of course we made new friends along the road.
As usual, we tried to spend not more money than necessary.

In this episode, we do the last preparations before going up Elbrus. We do an acclimatization climb to mount Cheget, from which we already can see Elbrus. We arrange the registration of our stay in Russia, repack our things and check the prices of the cable car which goes from Azau to a height over 3000 m.
