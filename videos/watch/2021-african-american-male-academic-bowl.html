The African American Male Academic Bowl (AAMAB) is an academic tournament that invites teams to compete in a virtual format. Each team will consist of three (3) students, 6th through 8th grade, and one (1) team coach. This academic tournament is designed as a single elimination competition that will quiz teams within a designated time frame. The winning teams will receive special awards.
