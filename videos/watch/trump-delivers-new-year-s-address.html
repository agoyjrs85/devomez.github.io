US President Donald Trump delivered a New Year’s message to America, praising the nation for showing “incredible grit, strength tenacity and resolve” throughout 2020.

“In this season of joy, Americans have so much to be grateful for,” he said.

“We’re all blessed to live in the greatest country ever to exist on the face of the earth and we have made it greater than ever before.

“As long as we remain loyal to our nation, devoted to our citizens and faithful to Almighty God, we know the best is yet to come.”
