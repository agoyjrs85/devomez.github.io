I’m very excited to be back with Jack Joseph Puig at his studio “Puigchild Studios!”- As usual, Jack is looking incredibly Dapper.

Jack’s listening environment is pretty fantastic, it’s a semi-hexagonal design that reminds me of the photo of 10CC with the Helios Console.- It’s a marvellous sight.

On the left of the console, there are some SSL Channels that Jack said are excellent on guitar or any percussive element that needs a lot of detail.
