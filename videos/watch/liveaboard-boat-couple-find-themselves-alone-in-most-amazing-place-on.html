Sometimes, living this liveaboard lifestyle feels like we truly live inside a fantasy. Things just come together, weather, people, and luck, to create the conditions for something really special. That is what happened here, as wind and weather kept us north a little longer than most cruisers, when the weather broke we managed to cruise to a part of paradise that we enjoyed all to ourselves. We hope in some small way, that we have captured and shared the euphoria of our days in this Exuma Bahamas location.

We are a travel couple, looking for a non-mainstream life of living aboard a boat and cruising in the Caribbean/Bahamas in Winter, and the Great Lakes in Summer. Working when we can to fill the cruising kitty. We have sold everything we own, and we now own a cruising yacht. We have chosen to do this voyaging in a powerboat/motor vessel, instead of the popular sailing route.

Our mission is to help inspire people to live their dreams earlier in life, instead of waiting until retirement. So often retirement dreams are dashed by health issues or other unforeseen later life circumstances. If you have a dream, then dream, plan, and do it now. We hope to make you smile, and spark the urning in your heart for fulfilling your dreams.
