How To Adjust a Commercial Door Closer:

We installed a commercial door closer for our office in a previous video and in this video we wanted to show two different ways to make adjustments on the closer after installation. The push of our door was a little heavy so I had to make it a bit lighter to ~5 lbs. Our close speed was too fast, something like 1.5 - 2 seconds, and per ADA needed to be a 5 second close so we tackled that adjustment also.
