In this flashback, Zaytoven spoke about producing "So Icy," featuring Gucci Mane and Jeezy, which was the producer's first big hit song. Zaytoven explained that he was working with Gucci Mane every day when they got a call about Jeezy wanting to do the song with Jeezy. When asked about the beef between the two rappers, Zaytoven says he doesn't know what happened, but he says it could've been over the ownership of the song.
