In this flashback, Zaytoven spoke about producing "So Icy," featuring Gucci Mane and Jeezy, which was the producer's first big hit song. When asked about the beef between the two rappers, Zaytoven says he doesn't know what happened, but he says it could've been over the ownership of the song.
