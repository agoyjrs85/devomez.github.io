Dr. Alexander Cummins - author, historian, poet and practicing magician - shares about the incredible records of a magician-scryer duo who used forceful methods and stripped down Protestant ritual to work with spirits during the spring of 1567. These records are found in one of the latest releases from Scarlet Imprint, “An Excellent Booke of the Arte of Magicke: The Magical Works of Humphrey Gilbert and John Davis.” This incredible tome was transcribed from British Library Additional manuscript 36674, edited and introduced by Phil Legard, with supplementary essays by Dr. Cummins, and includes a foreword by Dan Harms.
