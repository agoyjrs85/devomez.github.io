Basilica Sunday Mass Livestream
33rd Sunday in Ordinary Time
November 15, 2020

Join us live from the Basilica of the National Shrine of the Immaculate Conception on Sunday, November 15th at 12 Noon as we celebrate the holy Sacrifice of the Mass on the Thirty-Third Sunday in Ordinary Time. Our Catholic Sunday Mass livestream features sacred liturgical music by the Choir of the Basilica of the National Shrine of the Immaculate Conception.

The Mass is the greatest act of Evangelism the Church can offer the world. Our Sunday Catholic Mass livestream continues today due to God’s grace and the generosity of our viewers. If you would like to support the mission and ministry of our D.C. Basilica during the coronavirus pandemic, visit: https://www.nationalshrine.org/mass

The Basilica of the National Shrine of the Immaculate Conception in Washington D.C., is a Catholic church, a National Shrine dedicated to the Blessed Mother, patroness of our nation. It is a prayerful house of worship, pilgrimage, evangelization, and reconciliation. Mary’s Shrine invites people from across the country and beyond to share in Christ’s ever-living Presence.

Thank you for prayerfully watching the Mass at the Basilica and participating in our D.C. Shrine’s ministry!
