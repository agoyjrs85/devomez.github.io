Peterborough Police along with the assistance of the OPP’s Underwater Recovery Unit are currently on scene on Armour Rd at the hydro dam where they have discovered the body of the missing Trent University student Brett LeBlanc in the Otonabee River. Brett was reported missing Friday but had not been seen since last Tuesday. The community had come together and had been searching the area with no success, the OPP helicopter had also assisted city police for several days as well. The investigation by the Peterborough Police Service has concluded and this is now a Coroner’s investigation and no further information will be provided.
