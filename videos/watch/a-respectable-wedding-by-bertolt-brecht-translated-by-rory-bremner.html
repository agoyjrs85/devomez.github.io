A Respectable Wedding by Bertolt Brecht, translated by Rory Bremner
10-12 July 2017 at 7pm, 53two, 8 Albion Street, Manchester M1 5NZ.
What’s the best wedding you’ve ever been to? Did you spend a happy day sipping champagne & basking in the glow of a couple’s love? Were their friends & family all smiling and laughing, eager to get along?

Root & Branch invite you to a very different sort of wedding. One at which no one really gets on – even the bride & groom – and where the furniture is collapsing at the same time as the social niceties.

Will the father-of-the-bride ever shut up? Will the groom’s friend stop making dodgy jokes? And just what is the sister up to in the kitchen with the handsome young man?

In a vibrant translation by comedian Rory Bremner, A Respectable Wedding will make you laugh out loud & cringe with embarrassment. Just like your average wedding.....
