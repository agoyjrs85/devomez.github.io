This video shows a brief description of the differences between a hand knotted area rug and machine tufted rug. Machine tufted rugs have become more prevalent recently. Hand knotted rugs are usually of better quality, clean up very well and will last a life time if properly cared for. Tufted rugs are cheaper to purchase but can be fraught with many cleaning challenges.
