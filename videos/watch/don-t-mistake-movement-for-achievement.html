"Don't mistake movement for achievement"- Jim Rohn

We are all busier than ever before and the irony is that at the end of the day we see less results. We are now facing the activity trap - doing a bunch of activities that constitute less outcome. So how do we improve productivity and finish more tasks. First of all, we must realise that the majority of time we spend is on distractions and not on tasks that produce results.

This inspirational shot is a wake up call to pay attention to our daily tasks and habits and to take notice of the majors from the minor tasks.
