Professor Matthias Turina’s team of physicians at the Department of Surgery and Transplantation specializes in surgery for colorectal cancer, and works closely with specialists from other UHZ clinics to offer customized, superior patient care.
With a high caseload, our surgeons have considerable routine, also when it comes to rare procedures and tumors. The team offers all modern surgical techniques. Since 2016, it also has conducted robot-assisted procedures using the latest da Vinci system.
