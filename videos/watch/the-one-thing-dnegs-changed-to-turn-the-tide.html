We are now at the halfway point in the epic heads-up matchup between Daniel Negreanu and Doug Polk, and Doug is currently holding a nearly $800k lead. However, this stark number belies the competitiveness of this match, largely due to variance.

Although early on it appeared that Daniel was outmatched and would be run over, after making certain adjustments, he seems to now be holding his own - and the most obvious adjustment Daniel made was an increase in bet sizings and aggression.

In this video we will be exploring a hand played before Daniel made this adjustment, as an example of a scenario where Daniel's play was 100% solver approved, but may nonetheless have missed out on value.
