Detailed the VW Touran Van which was extremely dirty inside & outside. It was used for outdoors, the body and the tyres were full of mud. We mainly used the Halfords products, but the same products can be bought from cheaper places. For this van we will be completing a part 2 with premium products, carpet cleaner, different brushes and polishing to show the difference. Stay tuned!!
