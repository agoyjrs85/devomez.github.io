Are you urinating frequently? Determine the cause with an AMITA Health urologist or urogynecologist today: https://www.amitahealth.org/services/urology

On average, people urinate 5–8 times per day, according to AMITA Health nurse practitioner Nicole Rodriguez, NP. If you are experiencing more frequent urination than this average, it might be caused by diuretics or medications you are currently taking. It might also be a symptom of a more serious condition such as diabetes, pelvic organ prolapse, urinary tract infection (UTIs) or overactive bladder. A urologist and urogynecologist can help you make the correct diagnosis.
