In this video we will discuss why smart people tend to have fewer friends.
Recent studies have determined that smart people tend to have fewer friends than those less intelligent. Before anyone sniffs I want to clarify that this obviously does not mean that all intelligent people have only a few friends, as it does not mean that all the less smart ones are surrounded by many friends, but if we were to analyze a large sample of the population we can confirm that these results are quite accurate.
