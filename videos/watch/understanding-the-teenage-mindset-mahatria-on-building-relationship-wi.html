Mahatria is asked about the responsibility of a father when the son turns eighteen. Mahatria replies by giving a beautiful understanding of the teenage mindset and explains how to build friendly relationship with them.
In this phase a teenager has a new toy called intelligence. So, they think that they know everything. And very importantly, they think the parents don't know anything! Hence their refuge becomes their peer group, which eventually shapes their attitudes and values.
Their peer group is based on compatibility of values and mutual acceptance. Their friendship depends on who can provide that compatibility?
As a parent you can become that friend. How? Mahatria explains in detail in PART 2 of this video.
