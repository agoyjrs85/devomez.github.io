Let us take you on a tour of this 1940's Art Deco Armadale Apartment designed by Stuart Hall. Lucy Glade-Wright presents her apartment which has been her home for the last 10 years. Recently moving into new digs which meant that she needed to put this fine old dame on the market. In order to get this Art Deco pad ready for sale she took on an apartment makeover to get her looking her best.

Not only were the interiors of the building given an update but the exterior has recently undergone a major facelift. Together with the small owner’s corporate Lucy was involved in the transformation of the building’s exceptional exterior.

"We wanted to restore the building to its former glory and recently completed major paint work to update its colour scheme to be more in line with its original colour and we also commissioned a Palm Springs inspired landscaped garden to frame the building and complement its Art Deco detailing." Lucy Glade-Wright
