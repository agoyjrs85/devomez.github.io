I DID IT AGAIN! 💪
29th of September 2020 i woke up and i decided i am able to break my own world record. In my previous attempt of doing stalder presses as many reps as possible i managed to do 342 consecutively. This time i was able to beat that number by 60! I still can't believe it.
This record was also officially approved by @Guinness World Records ❤️. I am sooo incredibly happy i don't know what to say. This has always been my biggest dream and i finally was able to achieve it.
I hope you like it as much as i do 🥰

Here are the numbers:
57 minutes non stop L-seat straddle press to handstand
402 reps!
