The main weapon of the Demon Slayers besides their katana is their breathing techniques. Each of the Demon Slayers has mastered a specific technique like Tanjiro and Giyu's breath of the water, or Zenitsu's breath of thunder. So with the Demon Slayer movie coming, let's break down all of the different Breathing styles of the Demon Slayers, and while we are at it let's see what all the demons different Blood art styles as well.
