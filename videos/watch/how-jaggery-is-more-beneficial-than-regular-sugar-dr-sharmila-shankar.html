So that is the main difference between jaggery and sugar. Traditionally jaggery is made to boil upto a certain temperature and it is further boiled to make it like a paste consistency so that the minerals and the vitamin content stay intact, whereas the sugars, once it reaches a certain boiling point, charcoal is added to it especially the bone charcoal to added to it to make it look white and make it a crystal. So that removes the entre mineral, vitamin content in the sugar and it gets very quickly absorbed in the blood. That is the main difference between the jaggery and the sugar, whereas the jiggery has long sucrose chains and it talks longer time to get absorbed by the body and it helps in keeping the energy level quite stable when compared to the sugar, so that is the main difference between consuming jaggery as a sweetener on a daily basis an consuming sugar.
