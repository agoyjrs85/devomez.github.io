Aerial footage shows the molten lava from the Kilauea volcano appear to form a giant smiley face as it pumps out lava for its journey to the sea. The 6.5 mile-long lava flow has been dribbling down the south flank of Kilauea since May, according to the U.S. Geological Survey Hawaiian Volcano Observatory. It reached the ocean earlier this week.
