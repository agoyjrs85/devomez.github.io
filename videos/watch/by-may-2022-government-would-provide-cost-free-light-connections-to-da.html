Bharatiya Janata Party President Amit Shah claimed that Modi led government would provide more than 7 lakh LPG connections to families of dalits in Karnataka. Speaking at a public rally, he also assured that the Centre has decided that before May 2022 the dalits families would be provided free of cost light connections under the Saubhagya Yojana.
