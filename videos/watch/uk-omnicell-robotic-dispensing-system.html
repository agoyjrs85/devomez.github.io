Omnicell robotic dispensing systems can transform your retail or hospital pharmacy to ensure new levels of speed and efficiency are reached.

Omnicell Robotic Dispensing Solutions ensure the ongoing, seamless loading, storage and safe dispensing of medication packs. Medication is sorted, identified, automatically loaded and then picked by the robot for dispensing in ward boxes reducing the risk of picking errors. This system offers many functions and offers bespoke automation for any hospital pharmacy.
