This one-day digital summit brings together some of South Africa’s best minds, leaders in government and external experts to showcase what innovative governance can do to improve the lives of residents, and to ensure the best sharing of DA innovation among DA-led governments across the country.
 
Special guests include Federal Leader of the Democratic Alliance, John Steenhuisen; Premier of the Western Cape, Alan Winde; Executive Mayor of Stellenbosch, Gesie van Deventer; Mayoral Committee Member for Water & Waste in the City of Cape Town, Xanthea Limberg; Minister of Finance & Economic Opportunities in the Western Cape, David Maynier; and Chairperson of the Democratic Alliance Federal Council, Helen Zille.
