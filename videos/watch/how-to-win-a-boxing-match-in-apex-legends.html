This is the ultimate melee guide where I show you the analysis and give you tips on how to win more boxing/melee matches in apex legends. This is an extra special boxing ring located in the centre of Pathfinders Town Takeover that stops people in the ring using their weapons, throwables or abilities leaving your only option a good old fashion melee/fist fight.

Hopefully this video will help you get more wins in the game by improving your melee skills and allow you to take advantage of the new Pathfinder Town Takeover on Olympus.
