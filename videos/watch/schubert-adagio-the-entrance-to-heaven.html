A very brief excerpt from the film "L'amour de la vie" (1969) The Love of Life, With Artur Rubinstein, Eliahu Inbal, Paul Kletzki.

Here, Rubinstein speaks of his love for the first half of the Adagio in Schubert's Quintet in C, D.956. "It is the Entrance to Heaven. Death, Resigned and Happy."
