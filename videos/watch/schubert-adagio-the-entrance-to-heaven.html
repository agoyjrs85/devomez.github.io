A very brief excerpt from the film "L'amour de la vie" (1969) The Love of Life, With Artur Rubinstein, Eliahu Inbal, Paul Kletzki.



The movie is a must have for any piano or Artur Rubinstein fan, as it takes you through his life, from Poland to the US, and on tour in Paris, Turkey and Israel. Here, Rubinstein speaks of his love for the first half of the Adagio in Schubert's Quintet in C, D.956. "It is the Entrance to Heaven. Death, Resigned and Happy."
