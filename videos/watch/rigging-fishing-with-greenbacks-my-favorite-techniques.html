Rigging and fishing with greenbacks, here are my two favorite techniques when using live pilchards for bait. They are also an effective way to catch many prized game fish such as snook, redfish and trout. In this demonstration I show you how to rig greenbacks in both the nose and tail. You also get to see some exciting footage and how to catch snook using the greenbacks. Pilchards have been recognized as a best bait by many fisherman and there is good reason, they are hardy, and fish love them . Cast netting greenbacks tends to be the preferred technique but you can also sabiki them which tends to keep them alive longer by not removing their scales.
