The webinar will present how Moving Particles Simulation can reduce development effort and simulation time of complex CFD models involving free surface flows and interaction between fluids and moving boundaries / bodies.
Moving Particles Simulation is a mesh-less CFD methodology that allows easy simulation of gear boxes, planetary drives, engines, aquaplaning phenomena, interaction of vehicles with water, mixers and separators, free surface flows.
