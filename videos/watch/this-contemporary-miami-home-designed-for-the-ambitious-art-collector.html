Built this year by artist and developer Fernando Guardazzi, 1641 S Bayshore Drive is a contemporary home situated in Miami’s historic Coconut Grove community. The six-bedroom estate represents the arrival of modern architecture in one of Miami’s oldest neighborhoods, where until recently traditional Mediterranean homes with vaulted ceilings and terracotta roofs were the standard.
