This is a 45 minute vinyasa flow class dedicated to cultivating joy and ease in your practice. It's a slow, heat building practice that includes some challenging OPTIONS. Class begins with a brief seated meditation, followed by a gentle warm up for the upper body and lunge salutes. The next two sequences include some playful variations on familiar poses, including humble flamingo from pyramid and a twisting swan from chair. A level 2 class that builds strength, flexibility and challenges your balance in equal parts. Most importantly, class emphasises finding enjoyment in your own practice, liberating your body with your movement and breath.
