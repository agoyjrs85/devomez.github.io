Odd Squad Family is an independent hip hop group from Phoenix, Arizona. Made up of 3 very unique individuals, Nubs, Snowman and A-Factor are turning heads everywhere they go, and not just because of their appearance. Their content is an inspiring blend of positive vibes and classic hip hop influences intertwined with a fresh, modern delivery.

Odd Squad Family had been a force hard at work in the Phoenix hip hop scene in 2016, and with the release of their 1088 Mixtape it was clear the fam wasn’t playing around. Working up a buzz and catching the attention of hip hop producer, AKT Aktion, the Squad signed a production based deal that offered them the resources they needed to excel as independent musicians. Not too long after that, OSF dropped their first single with AKT Aktion, New Ish, in January of 2017. The explosive reaction to the video on Facebook put it on the fast track to being a viral sensation, and the Squad's Facebook page gained over 20,000 fans in the first week. Since the release of “New Ish” the Squad has put out a few singles leading up to the release of their first studio album, Welcome to the Family which was released on October 3rd, 2017. Welcome to the Family is available on all major streaming platforms including Spotify, Apple Music, and Google Play just to name a few. Check it out today!
