John Bergman is a renowned chiropractor based out of Huntington Beach, CA. My daughter Deborah has severe cerebral palsy and one of the results from her condition is extreme muscle tightness and misalignment of her bones. My family and I have traveled here to California for a two month treatment which I wholeheartedly believe will make a huge positive impact on her health and life. I will be doing a video journal every step along the way documenting her progress. I hope the videos can help other families with loved ones experiencing similar conditions.
