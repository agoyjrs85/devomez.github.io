If as a species, every combustion engine car simply switches to being an electric car, then that would be 'sub-optimal'. We are interested in fewer, smaller, lighter & more shareable vehicles, but when cars are required, we want to inspire people's next purchase to be a used or new pure electric car. This round-up of electric vehicles for Europe, the UK & North America is designed to put every EV in one place to help you choose your next car.
