Children are a wonderful thing, and giving birth is one of the best things that can happen in life. But some of the mothers from the past centuries, had the "luck" to have tens of children.

Each time when you hear about a mother that gave birth to 7,8 or 9 children, you think that this is enormous and that happened only in the past times.
But you won't believe that out there are women who gave birth to 33, 35, 39, 41, 62 or even 69 children. You will think that this is SF or something, but is real.

Mary Johnas - 33 Children

Elizabeth Mott - 42 Children
Maddalena Granata - 52 children
Barbara Stratzmann - 53 children
Mrs Kirillov - 57 Children
