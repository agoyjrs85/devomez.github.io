Tyler Green has been running since he was in second grade. He's on the Nike trail running team and had a lot of big running plans for 2020, including a race across the world in China. Of course, the races were canceled because of the pandemic, so Tyler (along with most other runners) had to find something else to keep him challenged and motivated.

He achieved the Fastest Known Time (FKT) of the Timberline trail in 2018, and this summer got another FKT, this time of the Loowit Trail around Mount St. Helens. On Monday, August 24, Tyler is attempting his third FKT - the Wonderland Trail around Mount Rainier.

Follow Tyler on Instagram - he'll have a link to track him for the 16-18 hours he's running!
