PVC (polyvinyl chloride) panels are high quality plastic sheets for interior decoration. They can be applied in the same way both in the decoration of commercial premises (offices, exhibition halls, shops) and in the rooms.

The imitation wood PVC panels are assembled with assembly glue or universal adhesive guns on any smooth surface, have a very light structure, are distinguished by their reduced weight and thickness (0.4 mm) and are flexible. Thanks to its flexibility, the panel during the installation can hide the existing irregularities on the wall surface. The TRONCHI DI PINO CHIARO pvc panel has a finish similar to regular silver fir trunks.






Wall covering panels

Material: PVC

Laying: assembly glue (Pattex millechiodi, Polymax) the laying is easy and fast, the product approaches without the need for joints and grouting

Suitable for: inside

Sheet size: 48 x 96 cm

Thickness: 0.4 mm

3D: from 2 to 10 mm

Application tools: Cutter and / or scissors, ruler, assembly glue

Ideal use: Covering molds, covering sanitary walls, upgrading workplaces, restyling of bars, offices and commercial premises, setting up theaters and various sets, furnishing event stands, and fairs with great visual impact.

Redevelop walls with old damaged wallpaper, shower cover, kitchen wall.
