Lt. General Henry Tumukunde did not campaign at some of his scheduled venues in Kikuube district after the Police blocked him.

The presidential candidate was supposed to address voters in Kikuube Town Council, Kaigo, Kabwoya Trading centre among other places but was not allowed to make stopovers to distribute posters.

In Hoima, Tumukunde’s convoy was stopped and after walking some distance, he finally addressed a rally saying that as president, he would consider increasing oil loyalties for oil-producing districts in Bunyoro Sub-Region
