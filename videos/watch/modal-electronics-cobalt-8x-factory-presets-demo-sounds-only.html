Modal Electronics COBALT 8X - Factory Presets Demo (sounds only)
Recorded without any additional FX, directly to Tascam DR-05x (24bit wav).

My first contact with this 8 polyphonic extended virtual analog synthesizer from Modal Electronics.

Modal Electronics MODAL 8X specifications:
- 8 voice polyphonic
- Supports MPE-compatible MIDI controllers
- 64 high-resolution virtual-analogue oscillators
- 8 Oscillators per voice
- 4-Pole Morphable Ladder Filter with Resonance
- Three dedicated envelope generators for AMP, MOD and FILTER
- Three audio-rate LFO’s with tempo sync
- Eight assignable modulation slots and four additional fixed modulation routings
- Polyphonic Real-time sequencer with 512 notes and four recordable/editable parameter animations
- Polyphonic Step Sequencer with 64 steps, 8 notes per step, 4 lanes of Parameter-Lock style animation
- Programmable arpeggiator of 32 steps
- Three independent and user-configurable stereo FX engines
- 500 patch memories, all fully editable and ships with 300 factory programs
- FATAR 61-key Keyboard with velocity and channel aftertouch
- 29 endless encoders, 24 buttons, 4-axis joystick
