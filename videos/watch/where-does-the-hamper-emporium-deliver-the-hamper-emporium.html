The team from The Hamper Emporium are here to answer your frequently asked questions!

In this episode, we cover where we deliver our range of gift hampers.

Orders placed before 1pm on a weekday are dispatched on the same day.

We are proud to deliver our full range of hampers Australia-wide, with FREE delivery to all areas.

This includes residential addresses, businesses, and hospitals.

Express delivery is also available from $14.95.

Every hamper that leaves our warehouse comes past me for a final check.

The hampers are then handed over to our delivery partners at Australia Post and Star Track.
