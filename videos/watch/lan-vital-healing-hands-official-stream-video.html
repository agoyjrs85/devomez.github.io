You’re listening to my new single, “Healing Hands” 🙌🏾 This single means a lot to me. I wrote it for all the people in my life who have had experiences involving toxic relationships & domestic abuse, and struggled with reconnecting with themselves. This is to remind you that the experiences you endured do not define your worth, or your future. And coming out about it only proves how truly strong you are.
