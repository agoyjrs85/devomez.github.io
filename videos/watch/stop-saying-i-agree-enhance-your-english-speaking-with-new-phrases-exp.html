How long will you speak basic English? Wondering how can you take your English to the next level and speak English fluently and confidently? Well I can answer that - Most beginners in English keep using basic English vocabulary and stay away from learning advanced words and phrases which are imperative to speak better English. This English lesson will help you replace basic English phrase ‘I agree with you’ with new English expressions to perfectly match different situations in which you have to agree or say yes.
