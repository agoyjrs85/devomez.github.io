How to know if a guy likes you? Are you interested in someone and perhaps keep missing the signs he is interested too? If you’re wondering how to interpret his subtle clues and signals, but you just aren’t able to decode his behavior, you're not alone. Many people find it quite challenging to figure out if someone is actually into them. In fact, research has shown your own personal biases and desires can shape the way you interpret the words, actions, and romantic intent of others.

So, how can you tell if your crush likes you? To be honest, learning how to know if someone is attracted to you is somewhat of an art. But, it's quite simple actually. You just need to pay close attention to the many facets of their behavior, instead of overestimating or underestimating their true intent. Consider your feelings when you're around this person, their reactions and the signs of interest before making up your mind that there is a possibility that this person is interested in you.

There are certain psychological signs when someone likes you, and they are usually quite evident. You just need to learn what they are, so you can recognize them.
