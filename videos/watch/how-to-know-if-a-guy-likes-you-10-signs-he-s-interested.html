How to know if a guy likes you? Are you interested in someone and perhaps keep missing the signs he is interested too? Many people find it quite challenging to figure out if someone is actually into them. In fact, research has shown your own personal biases and desires can shape the way you interpret the words, actions, and romantic intent of others.

So, how can you tell if your crush likes you? To be honest, learning how to know if someone is attracted to you is somewhat of an art. But, it's quite simple actually. You just need to pay close attention to the many facets of their behavior, instead of overestimating or underestimating their true intent.
