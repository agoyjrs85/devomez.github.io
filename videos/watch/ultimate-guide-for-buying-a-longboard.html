/ How old are you? / 18 years old.

/ How do I get a Crownboards? / You can easily get one by emailing info@crownboards.com or messaging them on Facebook.com/crownboards.

/ What editing program do you use? / Adobe Premiere Pro CS6.

/ What camera do you use? / A Canon 60D with 18-85mm lens and a Canon Legria HF G10.
