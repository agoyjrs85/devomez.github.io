Battles and competition is a natural part of the graffiti game. In the FRIENDLY BATTLE video series we decided to feature two graffiti artist in each episode. You will be the judge, but don't take it to seriously it's just for fun! ! In this episode of Friendly Battle we feature graffiti writers Coconut and Top Notch.
