Definition of yank according to Wiktionary:
yank can be a noun or a verb

As a noun yank can mean:
1. A sudden, vigorous pull (sometimes defined as mass times jerk, or rate of change of force).
2. A masturbation session.
3. A Yankee.
4. A Yankee, a Northerner: someone from the Northern United States, especially from New England.
5. An American: someone from the United States.

As a verb yank can mean:
1. To pull (something) with a quick, strong action.
2. To remove from distribution.
