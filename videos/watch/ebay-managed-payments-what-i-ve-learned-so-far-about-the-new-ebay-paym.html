Are you an eBay seller? If you are then there's a good chance you have heard about the new eBay managed payments processing system. It's a change that work from home sellers needed to adapt to otherwise listing on eBay would no longer be possible. So reluctantly we all enrolled, crossed our fingers and waited for the switch over to eBay managed payments. My first day was Thursday August 6, 2020 and I filmed this video on Monday August 17, 2020 with only 10 days experience with selling on eBay using managed payments but I wanted to update everyone on my own personal experience with it so far and to note the most important aspects of this new eBay program for anyone who is about ready to make the switch and is unsure of what to expect from eBay managed payments.
