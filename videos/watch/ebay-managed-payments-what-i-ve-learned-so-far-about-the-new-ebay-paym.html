Are you an eBay seller? If you are then there's a good chance you have heard about the new eBay managed payments processing system. It's a change that work from home sellers needed to adapt to otherwise listing on eBay would no longer be possible. So reluctantly we all enrolled, crossed our fingers and waited for the switch over to eBay managed payments. So let's chat about how it is going for this work from home mom...
