It's super common for little mistakes to pop up once a film or TV show is released. Some eagle-eyed fans can spot these inconsistencies from a mile away, while others have a hard time finding one, let alone two. Despite being one of the best Disney Channel Original Movies of all time, "Descendants 3" has dozens of little mistakes that viewers quickly picked up on. This isn't to say that the movie's production team didn't do a great job at planning out the set and everything that came with it. But, we couldn't help but notice that some scenes weren't consistent from frame to frame.

It's perfectly normal for little errors to pop up when filming an action-packed film like "Descendants 3." Most of the team is focused on costumes and choreography, amongst many other things.
