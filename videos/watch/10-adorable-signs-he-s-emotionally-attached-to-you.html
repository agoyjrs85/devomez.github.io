In this video, you will learn the top 10 signs that your man is emotionally attached to you. These signs are based on years of psychological research on adult attachment theory. So, if you’re seeing these things in your relationship, you can be certain that he has formed a strong attachment with you and sees you as his other half. What is attachment theory? Attachment theory explains our biological need to attach to or bond with others. This human need to be in a close relationship is embedded in our genes. In other words, we have been wired to single out a few specific people in our lives and make them precious to us. Evolution has bred us to be dependent on a significant other for survival. So, from the moment we are born until the moment we die, we have a need to be attached to others. And of course, our primary attachment relationship is the one we form with our parents or caregivers because they provide for our needs, they love us, and keep us safe.

But as we grow up, we become less dependent on our parents to meet these needs and as adults, we are able to form new primary attachment relationships when we partner up and get into a romantic relationship.

Of course we don’t get attached to everyone we date…. But when you meet a special person that you really like and start developing a relationship, the same physiological systems that attach babies to their parents is hard at work attaching and bonding you to your new partner.
In an ideal situation, your partner feels the same way and is also becoming securely emotionally attached to you. And when that happens, you are in the beginning of a long term loving partnership. So, how do you know if your man is emotionally attached to you? Well, if you are seeing these 10 signs, you can be sure that he’s attached.
