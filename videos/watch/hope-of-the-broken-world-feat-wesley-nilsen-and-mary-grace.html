In the beginning, when there was nothing but void, God surveyed the darkness and disorder and with four little words, “let there be light,” He began. Like an Artist, Potter, Healer, Maker and Creator, He began to bring beauty from the uninhabitable chaos. And He still does that today. Sin is a destroyer and it welcomes darkness. And we do that as humans. But, God.

Once again, when the dark was so dark, at the crux of time, God broke the longest 400-year night of sin. LET THERE BE JESUS ...and in the little town of Bethlehem, The Creator, Maker, Wonderful, Merciful, Savior, Author and Finisher, was born to us. He didn’t just create... He stepped into the story of our humanity. God in the most approachable way possible. Emmanuel. “God with us.” WITH us to make all things right, from beginning to end, from Cradle to Cross.

And He’s with us now. He’s alive and present. Still today, now, if we will welcome HIM to step into our personal world, He will enter and His Spirit will birth light into the darkness of our hearts and order into the chaotic uninhabitable that is uniquely ours. He will bring beauty, make all things work together, and make and all things right. He is the Hope of the Broken World.
