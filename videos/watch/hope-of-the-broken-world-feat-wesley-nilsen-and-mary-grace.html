And we do that as humans. But, God.

He stepped into the story of our humanity. God in the most approachable way possible. Emmanuel. “God with us.” WITH us to make all things right, from beginning to end, from Cradle to Cross.

Still today, now, if we will welcome HIM to step into our personal world, He will enter and His Spirit will birth light into the darkness of our hearts and order into the chaotic uninhabitable that is uniquely ours. He will bring beauty, make all things work together, and make and all things right. He is the Hope of the Broken World.
