What's up everybody! Just dropping a good 'ole dance rehearsal video with my friends Mikey and Sam. I probably gave up on finishing this piece a few times. Went at it, hated it… tried again, felt poopy still… and ultimately just found a window of energy where I emotionally aligned with the song again. Above all else it took moving with friends to verify why I felt like dancing to the song in the first place. Ah, the good ‘ole creative process. I’ll be filming this new one for Building Block 360 this week so you can learn it pretty soon at http://360.buildingblock.dance

Anyway, we have some new content coming soon in the next few weeks. Hope you're all doing well!

- Keone (& Mari)
