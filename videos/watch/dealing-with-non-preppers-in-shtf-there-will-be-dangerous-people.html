Most of the population is not prepared for any kind of long term SHTF scenario. Some are not even prepared for a basic emergency...

When dealing with non preppers in SHTF, you first need to understand that most people you interact with will not be prepared for any kind of an emergency. Once you can accept that, you can then proceed to understand their likely behavior during a disaster. You can use that knowledge to help you strategize your reaction to those possible behaviors and increase your chances of survival!
