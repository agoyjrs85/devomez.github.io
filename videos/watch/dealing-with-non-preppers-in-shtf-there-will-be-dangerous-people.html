Most of the population is not prepared for any kind of long term SHTF scenario. Some are not even prepared for a basic emergency...

Once you can accept that, you can then proceed to understand their likely behavior during a disaster. You can use that knowledge to help you strategize your reaction to those possible behaviors and increase your chances of survival!
