The ULS 3D+ and ULS Multi Tip Shooting Heads are nothing short of revolutionary. These fly lines offer a solution to target large predatory fish with light fishing tackle, much in the same way we fish for salmon and sea trout with heavier tackle. Based on our 3D+ density concept and available in 12-14-16-18 gram weights, these 5,7-6,2m long heads bring a totally new depth control into sink line fishing with lighter fly rods. They are designed to turn over all kinds of large and bulky streamers and nymphs as well as dries with ease.
