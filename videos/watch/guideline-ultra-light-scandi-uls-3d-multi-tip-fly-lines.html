The ULS 3D+ and ULS Multi Tip Shooting Heads are nothing short of revolutionary. Based on our 3D+ density concept and available in 12-14-16-18 gram weights, these 5,7-6,2m long heads bring a totally new depth control into sink line fishing with lighter fly rods. They are designed to turn over all kinds of large and bulky streamers and nymphs as well as dries with ease.
