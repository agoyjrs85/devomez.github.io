Let us jump into what myriad means?
----------------------------------------------------------
Too numerous to be counted
(chiefly in classical history) a unit of ten thousand
Having countless or very many elements or aspects
The cardinal number that is the product of ten and one thousand
A large indefinite number

Knowing similar meaning words make our speech varied. let's take a look at equivalent words of myriad.
----------------------------------------------------------
A great quantity
Multitudinous
A lot
Various
Untold
