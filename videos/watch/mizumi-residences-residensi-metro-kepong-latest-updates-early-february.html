Mizumi Residences consists of three blocks of 42 storeys high condominium set over 6 acres of leasehold land overlooking Kepong’s 220-acre Metropolitan Lake. These residential-only towers are located within a stone’s throw from Jalan Kepong, which is linked to MRR2. Tower 1 and Tower 2 each accommodates 460 units while Tower 3 houses 592 units – ranging from 3+1 bedrooms and 3 bedroom apartments. All units come with built-ups of between 901 sq ft to 1,027 sq ft. The average per sq ft price of units are between RM430 to RM490.

Residensi Metro Kepong, A project by Wawasan Metro Bina Sdn. Bhd. This project lies on a 6-acre area of leasehold land with a view of Kepong’s 220-acre Metropolitan Lake and is made up of three blocks of 35-storey high building complex with an afforable price ranging from RM188,000 - RM198,000 (800 sqft with balcony and yard, 3 rooms, 2 bathrooms, and 1 car park).

These towers of residence are set a short distance from Jalan Kepong, which is connected to MRR2, DUKE and Jalan Kuching. Located next to The Henge, Kepongmas, Fortune Perdana and distance away to the Kiara Bay (a 73-acre masterplan development poised to transform one of KL’s earliest townships into an awe-inspiring eco living destination-brought to you by UEM Sunrise, the makers of Mont’Kiara), this project set target completion by 2023 alongside with the upcoming MRT2 in just 700 metre away.

Developer : Wawasan Metro Bina Sdn. Bhd.

Construction Progress of 06/02/2021.
