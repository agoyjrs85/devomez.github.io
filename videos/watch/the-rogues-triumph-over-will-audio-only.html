The Rogues rose to prominence via Detroits
gritty, underground music scene. Detroit label,
Force Majeure Records, was quick to sign the
band. Soon after, infamous German Punk label,
Rock-O-Rama, released The Rogues debut, Zero
Street to an international audience. This gave the
band instant worldwide acclaim and credibility. Oi!
Detroit contains 7 songs from the bands
remarkable debut release. Representing a sliver
of time in the second generation of Punk, The
Rogues are a shining example of American Oi!
