Intel has acquired Cnvrg & SigOpt - Two innovative AI startups

Let’s look at these two startups

1. Cnvrg

Cnvrg calls itself the ‘Operating system for machine learning & AI.'

It is an end-to-end AI management platform

They enable teams to build, track, monitor & scale ML models

Cnvrg runs ML models on containerized infrastructure & NVIDIA machines

Cnvrg’s dashboards report metrics like server and compute utilization, memory utilization, and capacity

With data caching Cnvrg can process large datasets faster by storing them in the same compute cluster

The Workspaces feature allows data science teams to collaborate around ML notebooks

Cnvrg supports Jupyter, RStudio, or VS Code

2. SigOpt

SigOpt focuses on just one part of the ML lifecycle - ML model optimization

It doesn’t handle creating, deploying, or scaling models

However, it does have some advanced optimization capabilities

It works with any science data platform
TensorFlow, PyTorch, R, AWS SageMaker, Keras, MATLAB, scikit-learn, mxnet

Optimization with SigOpt begins by running experiments on ML models

Experiments improve a specific metric in an ML model

They are run in YAML files that are easy to store and reuse by multiple teams

After initial experiments, you move to the heart of SigOpt - model optimization

You can optimize specific metrics for your model

Optimization options include multimetric, multitask, and conditional parameters

SigOpt allows you to optimize for more than one metric, which is very powerful

Intel is out to build the best AI and ML management platform

Cnvrg & SigOpt will be a central part of this strategy

Intel wants to power tomorrow’s AI infrastructure with their own GPUs

NVIDIA is their biggest competitor in this space that also offers GPUs & AI services
