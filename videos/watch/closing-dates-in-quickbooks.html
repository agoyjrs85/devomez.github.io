Closing Date Passwords in QuickBooks - Your books should be closed on a consistent basis. Once your books are properly closed you need to set a closing date password in QuickBooks to ensure that you don't inadvertently change transactions in a closed period. In this video, Eric talks about what closing your books means, how to set a closing date password, and how this affects transactions in a closed period.
