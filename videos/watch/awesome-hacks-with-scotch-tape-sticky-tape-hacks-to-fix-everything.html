Maybe you can get some ideas for your next craft or improve something at home. We’ll begin with cute colorful house slippers. We have so many more hacks, so keep watching. We’ll show you how to use tape to get rid of the lint and hair covering your clothes. Easy-peasy! Then, you’ll learn how to open a jar lid using some duct tape. Did you know you can apply makeup using tape? That’s right, friends. We’ll teach you how to apply eyeliner, bronzer, and even how to contour your nose using your new sticky little friend!
