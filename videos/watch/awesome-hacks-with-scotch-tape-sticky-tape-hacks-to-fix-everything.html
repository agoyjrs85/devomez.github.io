Sometimes all you need is a little tape to make things right. Oh yeah, when things get rough, tape can be an unlikely savior, and we’ll show you some examples in this video. And who knows?! Maybe you can get some ideas for your next craft or improve something at home. We’ll begin with cute colorful house slippers. Just trace a tennis shoe on cardboard and then cover the cutout with tape. Add a strap and you’re done! And here’s a tip: give a pair to your guests when they visit, so your floors stay clean.

We have so many more hacks, so keep watching. We’ll show you how to use tape to get rid of the lint and hair covering your clothes. Easy-peasy! Then, you’ll learn how to open a jar lid using some duct tape. Did you know you can apply makeup using tape? That’s right, friends. We’ll teach you how to apply eyeliner, bronzer, and even how to contour your nose using your new sticky little friend!
