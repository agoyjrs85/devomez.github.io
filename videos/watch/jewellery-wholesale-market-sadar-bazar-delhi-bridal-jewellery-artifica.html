Sadar Bazar is the largest wholesale market in Delhi. while it is a mixed market that serves a variety of things, it is most well-known for its massive supply of household items like utensils, buckets, mugs, cutlery, crockery items. There are a number of smaller market within the market like Timber Market, for plywood and Swadeshi Market for artifical jewellery, gift items, toys, etc.
