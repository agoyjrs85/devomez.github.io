Learn English With Photos is a series of twenty ten-minute videos suitable for learners of English at intermediate level or above.

Each video is divided into three parts. The first part consists of a photo essay based on a visit to a tourist attraction or some other place of interest. In the second part, key vocabulary is presented and explained. Finally, there are a series of questions for the viewer to answer relating to the topic of the lesson.
