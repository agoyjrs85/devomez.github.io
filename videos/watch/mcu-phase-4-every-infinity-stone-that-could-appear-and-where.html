Where will the stones appear in phase 4?
Subscribe to CBR: http://bit.ly/Subscribe-to-CBR

There are few things out there as great as the MCU’s Infinity Saga. The long journey to see Thanos assemble all of the infinity stones while the Avengers assembled and then disassembled turned out to be more than worth it. The Infinity War and Endgame two-punch definitely left big shoes for Phase 4 of the Marvel Cinematic Universe to live up too. How can they possibly top something as epic as the infinity stones and the infinity gauntlet? Well, what if I told you that they don’t have to?

There’s plenty of evidence supporting the idea that the infinity stones will be back in Marvel’s phase 4. We already know that the new chapter of everyone’s favorite franchise will feature alternate universes, reality-altering sitcom hijinks, ressurected characters and time police.

We’ll also get more information on the Celestial beings that used to wield the infinity stones as Marvel continues to explore its past. Maybe we’ll also find out what a certain cosmic superhero was up to during the events of the first two Avengers movies and how that relates to the most powerful minerals in the multiverse.

Phase 4 will also introduce us to fun, new ways to tell stories that aren’t connected to the MCU. It’s only natural that the infinity stones could make an appearance in a series that doesn’t have to be connected to the larger story.

So let’s dive in and discuss how the Infinity Stones will reappear in MCU’s Phase 4!
