With the advancement in technology and space exploration, more and more evidence is being gathered from orbiting probes around Mars and the Rovers upon its surface. This once occupied planet looks to have been devastated by some form of cataclysm. Its surface is littered with ancient machinery, stone terraces and statues. Many strange photographs have been obtained showing regions of possible water, ruined buildings, plant like formations and much more. Researcher and author Mike Bara explores the red planet data.
