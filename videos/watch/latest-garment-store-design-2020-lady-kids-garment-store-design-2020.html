Yeh Garment Store 12'x11' (15x12') ka hai, yeh store ko Hamne Full renovate kiya hai , civil work to finishing work .
Garment shop k video me aap before picture dekh sakte hai, garment store me hamne wall tiles, Floor tiles kiya hai.
store ka height 11.5 Feet hai to hamne 2 layer me isko banaya hai floor to false ceiling 8' Clear height rakha hai , after then isme wooden ka heavy structure kar k ceiling banaya hai 6 " beam k sath. uske uper side Store Room me 3' ka. jahan garment shop k Stock rakhne k lie provision hai.
uski ceiling ko niche side se laminate & Charcoal panel se finish kiya hai,
Ceiling me 30 Watt cob light , 20 watt cob & 2 Speaker v lagaya hai , jisse ceiling ka outlook bahut hi accha aa rha hai.
Garment Shop ke 3 wall me hamne open Display Rack banaya hai. jiska depth 14 inch finish hai, usme 10 MM glass ko F-Bracket se hold kiya hai jisse design sleek & Space mile.
iske sath hi L-shape ka display counter kiya hai, counter k top me 12 MM solid corian use hai & Display counter k front me 8 MM groove k sath 8 MM profile led aluminum profile use kiya hai , jisse counter or v attractive lage.

Main Door me 12 MM Toughened Glass use kiya hai 2 side partition & 36" ka Door jo both side open-able hai.
Garment ke outside me Acp (aluminium Composite panel) use kiya hai jo ki Alu decor 3MM ka hai, usme 8 inch ka 3d Led Letter shop ke brand name ko dikhaya hai.
Garment Store Name- "New Ashoka Novelty Store" Kolkata
