Pikmin 4 is starting to reach game development kryptid status. We know that somewhere out there, some form of this game does, or at least has existed, but past that nothing! So much time has passed that my mind has gone crazy with possibilities for what the game could potentially be, the avenues where the series could go, so let's take a moment to talk about all the cool things Pikmin 4 could potentially be!

Thank you all for your patience in waiting for this video. Between the move and various other personal life shenanigans things have been a little crazy, but I think the end product was worth the wait. Hope you enjoy!
