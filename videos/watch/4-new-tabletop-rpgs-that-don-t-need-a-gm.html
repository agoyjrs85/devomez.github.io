Assembling a group of people to play an RPG with you can be difficult as it is! Let alone trying to convince someone (or even yourself) to be the GM... But that's okay, where we're going we don't need a GM. Here's wheels with 4 recommendations for games that do away with games masters altogether.

This time we're focusing on games that are brand new or have just been re-released in the last year but if you've got a favourite GMless RPG let us know in the comments!
