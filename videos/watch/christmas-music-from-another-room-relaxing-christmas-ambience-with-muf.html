Enjoy Christmas music from another room in this relaxing Christmas ambience scene. The scene is a snowy winter tableau with falling snow outside a beautifully decorated house with Christmas lights. This ambience is perfect for improved focus for work, reading, or studying, or to help with relaxation, meditation, and sleeping. It can also be used to set a relaxing holiday mood in your home. This relaxation video is 8 hours long to allow for uninterrupted studying or a full night of sleep.
