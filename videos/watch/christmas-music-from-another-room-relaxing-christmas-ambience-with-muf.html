Enjoy Christmas music from another room in this relaxing Christmas ambience scene. The scene is a snowy winter tableau with falling snow outside a beautifully decorated house with Christmas lights. Through the windows, you can catch a glimpse of the people in the house enjoying a festive and peaceful evening during the holiday season. The muffled Christmas music is a soothing and gentle mix of instrumental smooth jazz and piano Christmas songs. In the background, a faint crackling fire can be heard. This cozy scene is meant to evoke a feeling of home, safety, and nostalgia.

This ambience is perfect for improved focus for work, reading, or studying, or to help with relaxation, meditation, and sleeping. It can also be used to set a relaxing holiday mood in your home. This relaxation video is 8 hours long to allow for uninterrupted studying or a full night of sleep.
