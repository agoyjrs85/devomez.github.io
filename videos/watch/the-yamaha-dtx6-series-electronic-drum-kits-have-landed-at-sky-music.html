The new Yamaha DTX6 Series of Electronic Drum Kits have arrived at Sky Music!

Introducing the all new DTX6 Series from Yamaha. Available in three new configurations: DTX6K-X, DTX6K2-X, and DTX6K3-X

All kits feature the new DTX-PRO module, with its design and operation influenced by Yamaha’s popular EAD10 for easy use and intuitive navigation.

The DTX-PRO contains a host of newly recorded modern and vintage
drum and cymbal sounds that were recorded by top drummers in renowned studios around the world including Real World Studios in England.

All three kits feature a 13” 3-zone ride cymbal and an 8” 3-zone TCS snare.
The DTX6K2-X and DTX6K3-X include 13” vertical motion hi-hats with included Yamaha acoustic hi-hat stand to offer the feel and response of an acoustic kit.

The DTX6K2-X and DTX6K3-X also include the brand new KP90 kick pad with a multi-layer, large diameter playing surface – perfect for double pedal players.

KIT MODIFIERS allow you to take control of Ambience, Compression and Effects in real-time to shape your drum sound. You can even add up to 1000 of your own samples directly from USB flash drive.

Connect to your computer or smart device for audio recording and playback or use the free Rec’n’Share app available for Apple and Android to share your playing and video of your performance directly to social media
