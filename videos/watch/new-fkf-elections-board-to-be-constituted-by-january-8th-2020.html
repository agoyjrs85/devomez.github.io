FKF will go back to the drawing board to plan the federation elections that will now be held next year. President Nick Mwendwa has announced that a new FKF elections board will be constituted and given to members for public participation. A new elections code has been sent to members today and the feedback expected back on January 8th. Fkf will then call for a special general meeting to ratify the code before a new elections date is announced.
