For the millions of people worldwide who suffer when their own immune system turns against them, today’s treatment options—such as systemic steroids and immunosuppressants—are limited or inefficient. Led by co-founder Aleks Radovic-Moreno, Alivio is pioneering a new approach for treating autoimmune and inflammatory diseases based on the concept of targeted immunoregulation. Check out Aleks’s 2017 TEDMED Hive Talk to learn more about Alivio’s novel hydrogel technology and how it can serve as an alternative treatment option for autoimmune and inflammatory diseases.
