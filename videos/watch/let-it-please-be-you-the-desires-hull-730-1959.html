LET IT PLEASE BE YOU, The Desires, Hull #730 1959
Flipside: HEY LENA - Here’s another really great song from my music collection. The Desires met as high School kids at the 11th Street Youth Center in Harlem, NY. Group members were Robert "Bootsie" White (lead), Charles Hurston (1st tenor), Jim Whittier (tenor) formerly from The Jivetones on Apt, George "Smithy" Smith (baritone) and Charles Powell (bass). They originally called themselves The Students until “I’M SO YOUNG” was getting airplay by the original Students group.

In a move not done by most vocal groups, The Desires worked with a vocal coach, Eddie Jones. Jones was formerly of The Demens on Teenage Records & The Emersons on Newport Records. Jones was a singer/songwriter/producer and was The Cadillacs’ musical director for many years.

By 1959 The Desires were professional enough to have attracted The Bobbettes’ manager Jim Dailey’s attention. They had written a few of their own songs and Dailey took their demos to Hull Records.

Their 1st release for Hull “LET IT PLEASE BE YOU” got local radio airplay which enabled them to travel a bit and appear at decent venues with other acts. It is a classic doowop song and was covered by The Re’Vells (Roman Press #201) 1962, Rick & The Masters (Cameo # 247) 1963, The Carolons (Mellomood # 1003) 1964, and Patty & The Street-Tones (Clifton #49) 1980.

Although they recorded at least 8 other songs, their only other release on Hull was “RENDEZVOUS WITH YOU/SET ME FREE (MY DARLING)” (Hull #733) 1960. With the sudden death of Hull Records’ owner, bad management decisions and the record not having much success, the group soon disbanded.

Enjoy this classic New York style ballad, "LET IT PLEASE BE YOU" by The Desires with Robert “Bootsie” White singing lead. This is truly what a doowop song is all about! It doesn’t get much better than this!!
