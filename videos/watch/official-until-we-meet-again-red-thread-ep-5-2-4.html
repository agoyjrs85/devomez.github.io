Synopsis

In the age of thirty years before that may not be long Korn and Intouch, a university student in Bangkok.
Met at first, beginning with compassion Causing Intouch to step into the life of Kon, who is known as the son of an influential person, despite being chased, even if he is ignored at the end, the heart must also allow the young boy to be cheerful as the sun
But In the days when same-sex love was not accepted When the break reaches their father's ears Causing both of them to secretly meet InTouch tries to fight, but for Korn, who has endured such awkwardness for a long time, is disheartened. Turned into a tragedy When Korn shot himself to the point of Intouch who regrets and loses consciousness, suicide followed along. When the story should end But something is bound to both of them.
