Seoul will allow the U.S. military to start vaccinating South Korean soldiers and civilians working with the U.S. Army.
They can choose to get the shot, but must be told about the possible side effects... and how they're going to be treated in case of an adverse reaction.
USFK should also give the defense ministry a list of the Koreans who got the shots so their condition can be monitored.
Near the front of the line for the shots are 40 KATUSA soldiers at Camp Humphreys in Pyeongtaek.
Ten USFK-affiliated individuals tested positive for COVID-19 after arriving in South Korea over the past two weeks,... raising that total to 4-hundred-80.
