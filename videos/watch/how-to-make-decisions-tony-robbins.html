How To Make Decisions - Tony Robbins

(Motivation Monday 2019)

Thanks for joining Happy Facts Lifestyle for more Practical Inspiration from the man, Tony Robbins. Life constantly forces us to make very big and often very painful decisions.

Which career should I pursue? Should I break up — or get married?! Where should I live? Big decisions like these can be agonizingly difficult.

So, how do you decide what goals to set in your life? Well, that's precisely what Tony Robbins covers here, to stop you from asking the question "How do I know if I am making the RIGHT decision?".

Let's learn from Tony Robbins in this motivational speech he gives on making decisions, and specifically, the RIGHT decisions.
