About Me:
My name is Archana Sarat, and I've been an author since 2005. My debut novel, Birds of Prey, is a psychological crime thriller, traditionally published by the publishing house, Readomania. My second book is a collection of flash fiction stories, Tit for Tat, and can be found as a Kindle ebook. My third and most recent book is a collection of short stories that can be enjoyed by anyone from 8-88 years of age, 'Tales from The History of Mathematics'.
