So what can go wrong at closing? Especially on closing day on your new dream home home? Turns out, quite a bit. From possible issues with the final walkthrough to mortgage interest rate changes (if you didn't rate lock) to buyers simply getting ahead of themselves by going out and charging up credit cards for new furniture, appliances, etc...

Throughout the closing on a house process, you trusted real estate advisor will guide you with a closing on a house checklist and help keep you on tract.
