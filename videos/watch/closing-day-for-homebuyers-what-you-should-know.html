Both seasoned homebuyers and first time home buyers want the closing process to go smoothly. So what can go wrong at closing? Especially on closing day on your new dream home home? Turns out, quite a bit. From possible issues with the final walkthrough to mortgage interest rate changes (if you didn't rate lock) to buyers simply getting ahead of themselves by going out and charging up credit cards for new furniture, appliances, etc...

Throughout the closing on a house process, you trusted real estate advisor will guide you with a closing on a house checklist and help keep you on tract. Your local loan officer will share with you the common reasons why buyers fail to close on a home. All of which can be avoided with the simple steps in this video.

By better understanding a real estate closing and how closing on a house for buyers works, you'll be well prepared for success to avoid financial and emotional injuries.
