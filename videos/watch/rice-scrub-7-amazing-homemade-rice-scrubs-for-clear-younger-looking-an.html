Rice Scrub: 7 Amazing Homemade Rice Scrubs for Clear, Younger-looking, and Flawless Skin

Rice is an essential food in most countries. This staple food is consumed by more than half of the world’s population as it is readily available and versatile. It is the major source of daily calorie intake for many people. Apart from being great nourishment for your body, it is also great for your face and skin.

Rice can be ground into a fine powder and can be used for face powder, to exfoliate the skin, as a facial toner, and to treat acne. Rice scrub is a superb exfoliant. Rice powder has been used in the past for beauty and cosmetic purposes because of the many beneficial properties that rice carries. It has a soft texture and can help brighten up your skin. Rice can help absorb excess sebum and reduce sunburn. Rice also has anti-aging and anti-inflammatory properties.

Rice contains ferulic acid, a powerful antioxidant that is protective against harmful UV rays and the resultant damages. It can reverse free radical damage and ameliorate damages caused by oxidative stress.

Rice also contains inositol, a potent antioxidant that can help reverse damages caused by free radicals. These damages include wrinkles, skin sagging, and other aging-related skin conditions.

Rice flour also contains allantoin and para-aminobenzoic acid that is beneficial to the skin. Allantoin, which has anti-inflammatory properties, smoothens the skin, and boost skin regeneration and repair. Para-aminobenzoic acid can help your skin look younger and protect you from sunburn.

Scrubbing is an essential part of your skincare routine. And we have listed 7 amazing homemade rice scrubs that are simple to make, inexpensive, and very effective. The ingredients required can be easily gotten in your kitchen.
