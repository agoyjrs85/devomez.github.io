▶ ATEX/IECEx Directives | Equipment and protective systems intended for use in potentially explosive atmospheres must comply with a range of regulations, standards and directives before they can be placed into the market.

As a manufacturer, you are responsible for the conformity assessment and for obtaining the certification required in the country your equipment will be used in.
