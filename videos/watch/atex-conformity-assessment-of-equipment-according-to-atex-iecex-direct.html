▶ ATEX/IECEx Directives | Equipment and protective systems intended for use in potentially explosive atmospheres must comply with a range of regulations, standards and directives before they can be placed into the market.

As a manufacturer, you are responsible for the conformity assessment and for obtaining the certification required in the country your equipment will be used in. With our Notified Body status for the ATEX directive and testing laboratory approved by the IEC, we are your ideal partners for the certification of equipment used in potentially explosive areas.
