welcome to what being a growing youtuber living abroad is like!

In vlogmas days 18-19 I show you what being a growing youtuber living abroad in Canada is like. We go snowboarding, I share with y'all some iconic products I'm using at the moment, we reply to comments and get emotional together, go grocery shopping AND we plan for next week/Christmas!

Anyway I hope you enjoy seeing what being a growing youtuber living abroad is like!
