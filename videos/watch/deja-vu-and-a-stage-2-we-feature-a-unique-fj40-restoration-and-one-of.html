Red FJ40s are popular lately so we feature another, but unique, PRLC Body Off Resto-mod of a 1973 FJ40. Then, a look at a rare (for us) stage 2 restoration of a 1982 FJ40. Also in this episode, as part of the new STOP IT segment, we highlight what NOT to do when repairing sheet metal.

The red FJ40 in this episode sports a Proffitt's Resurrection Land Cruisers full Stage 3 body-off restoration. We replaced the original F engine with a 3FE and Automatic from an 88-90 FJ62. Suspension is an Old Man Emu 2.5" lift with our shackle reversal. We added power steering, and extended the wheelbase 3.5" Inside is our full custom family roll cage, Vintage Air heat and AC, and OEM seats covered with black leather.
