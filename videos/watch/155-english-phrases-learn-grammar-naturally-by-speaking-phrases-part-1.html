In today’s video, you will learn basic English grammar naturally by practising speaking 155 English Phrases. This will be part 1.

If you don't understand everything, don't worry. Try to shadow and copy the sound patterns as much as possible by listening and speaking each phrase. Then go back and try to understand the meaning if you want to later.

You'll naturally start to learn grammar through this method. This is how most native speakers learn grammar naturally by listening to a lot of phrases then speaking it. Comprehension will eventually follow.
