* What is a Lycan Shepherd?

- Answer: It is a new general purpose working breed in the early stages of development under the guidance of the 'Lycan Shepherd Project'. These puppies are from a F2 breeding in that process. The breeds currently being used in its development are Blue Bay Shepherd, working line German Shepherd and Malinois. The breed is still in the early foundation stage of development. The breed is intended to have the larger brain to body size ratio, superior problem solving ability, intelligence and long leg structure of a wolf with the working ability of a working line German Shepherd.
