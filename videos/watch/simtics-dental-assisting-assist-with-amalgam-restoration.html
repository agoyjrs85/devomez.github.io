Dental Assisting: Assist with Amalgam Restoration

We are excited to bring to you our brand new suite of Dental Assisting education modules with web-based skills simulations. A first for dental education!

This video is a tiny sneak peek into our Dental Assisting: Assist with Amalgam Restoration module. But wait there's more! - each module doesn't only have a video, but also has a simulation where you can learn and practice the procedure interactively, step by step, then test your skills. On top of that you'll get an illustrated text description of the skill or procedure to read, a 3D model of relevant anatomy or equipment to explore, and a multi-choice quiz.
