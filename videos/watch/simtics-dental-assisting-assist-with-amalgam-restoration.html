We are excited to bring to you our brand new suite of Dental Assisting education modules with web-based skills simulations. A first for dental education!

This video is a tiny sneak peek into our Dental Assisting: Assist with Amalgam Restoration module. But wait there's more! - each module doesn't only have a video, but also has a simulation where you can learn and practice the procedure interactively, step by step, then test your skills.
