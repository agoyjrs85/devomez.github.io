At age 18, Jay Shetty spent his time listening to CEOs, high-level marketers, and famous influencers. When a friend asked him to come listen to a monk speak, he reluctantly agreed to go just to please his friend. What he assumed would be a boring evening turned into the single most pivotal moment of his life so far.

As the monk spoke, Shetty was in awe. Although the monk had left behind the very things he and his friends had been chasing, he was happier than anyone Shetty had ever encountered. Watch this video to learn how one meeting changed the whole direction of Jay Shetty’s life forever.
