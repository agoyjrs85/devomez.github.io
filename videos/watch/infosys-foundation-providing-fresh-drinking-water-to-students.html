Infosys Foundation partnered with Akshaya Patra to solve the issue of acute water scarcity in Rajasthan. Through this partnership, Foundation will sponsor the providing of hygienic and safe packaged water to the schools in Rajasthan and, thus, ensure that children stay healthy and get an opportunity to study in a more caring environment.
