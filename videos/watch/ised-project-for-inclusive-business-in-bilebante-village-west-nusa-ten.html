#ISEDProject #InclusiveBusiness #SustainableTourism #BillebanteVillage #ProjectDrivers


Meet the #ISEDProject #ProjectDrivers from the #BillebanteVillage that are resilient to bring changes into their community. The partnership between the local community, private and public sector put forward #InclusiveBusiness model enabling all stakeholders to work collaboratively in an economical ecosystem as suppliers, distributors, retailers or consumers aiming for inclusive and sustainable employment. Some activities done include skills development in tourism, such as wellness, culinary, homestay, marketing & promotion, financial literacy and well as digital literacy.
