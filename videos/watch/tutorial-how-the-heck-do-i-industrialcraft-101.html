A tutorial series that I'm doing on various tech-mods to teach people the basics of using the mods, starting with Industrialcraft. I explain the different machines you can get with the mod, the different methods of power generation, storage, and transformation up/down between various voltages, the resources you'll encounter and where to find them, some of the other tools you can get with the mod, the various types of cabling, the concept of "per tick" vs. "per packet" (this confuses a lot of people!), and finally show a simple setup that can fit in the room of a house that allows for lots of machines, easy upgrading, and aesthetics without getting super complicated with it!
