The 12 POURS OF CHRISTMAS - For the 11th Pour of Christmas Masse Art Studio - 2 Dutch Pours beginners guide EPIC Highland Colors

For the 11th Pour of Christmas Masse Art Studio - 2 Dutch Pours. One with very wintery colors and there other with very highland colors. We loved how both of these turned out, after one epic fail!! We took the inspiration from the pipers, to blow, hence the Dutch Pour. We can't thank you enough for watching and enjoying this series like we have. Wishing you all a Happy Holidays.

We are so excited to bring you the 12 Pours of Christmas, our very first series of pours that will consist of a premier a day for the next 12 days. We have taken inspiration from the song the 12 Days of Christmas and have poured 12 special episodes for you that will start on 14th December and end on Christmas Day. Please join us and watch how this plays out. We are sure to have a lot of fun and silliness along the way.
