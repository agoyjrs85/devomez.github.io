This video provides an introduction into the goals of mechanical ventilation, as well as categorizing the indications for mechanical ventilation into 3 major categories.

At the end of this video the learner should be able to:
1) Describe the goals of mechanical ventilation
2) Understand the major indications for mechanical ventilation
3) Describe the primary ABG disturbance for Type I and Type II respiratory failure
