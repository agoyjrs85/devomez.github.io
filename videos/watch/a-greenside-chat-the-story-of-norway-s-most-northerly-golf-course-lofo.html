It always amazes me when I get to these curious and fascinating parts of the world where great golf courses have been built in fun environments, and you kind of pinch yourself wondering, how did we get here?  I think you will be absolutely intrigued by this week's Greenside Chat with Frode Hov, owner of Norway's spectacular Lofoten Links found 95 miles above the Arctic Circle in the Land of the Midnight Sun.

In the coming weeks I shall interview Shelley Duncan, Director of Golf at Otago Golf Club, the oldest club in the Southern Hemisphere on New Zealand's South Island; and Michael Murphy, General Manger at Waterville Golf Links, an outstanding test of golf located on the Ring of Kerry in Southwest Ireland and where a life-sized statue pays tribute to Payne Stewart.

Please enjoy Frode's story.
