Ashley Graham sits down with Paloma Elsesser. Paloma ia a model and style icon that got her big break when she was discovered by renowned makeup artist Pat McGrath on Instagram. You can catch her on runways and working with brands like Glossier, Nike and Fenty Beauty. In this episode, we talk fashion, mental health, confidence, and so much more.
