Famous Failures Before Success is a video about failure to successes. We all can learn a great deal from these famous failures to success stories. Tony Robbins explains how failure is your friend and can drive you to greater success. He uses examples of Lee Iacocca's Chrysler story and Tom Watson, the IBM success story. I'm sure this will give you the inspiration and motivation that you need to overcome failure. Remember, there is no such thing as failure.
