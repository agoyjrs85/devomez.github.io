Ross Hauser, MD and Brian Hutcheson, DC discuss blurry vision, ocular pressure, and brain fog which are common symptoms due to increased intracranial pressure, or intracranial hypertension, from cervical instability. Dr. Hauser explains how cervical instability affects the "main vein that drains the brain!" In our office, these cases demonstrate why it can be crucial to help someone restore their cervical curve in order to finally relieve symptoms that may have been plaguing them for years.
