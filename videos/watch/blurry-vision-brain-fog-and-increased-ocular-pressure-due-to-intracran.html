Ross Hauser, MD and Brian Hutcheson, DC discuss blurry vision, ocular pressure, and brain fog which are common symptoms due to increased intracranial pressure, or intracranial hypertension, from cervical instability. Dr. Hauser explains how cervical instability affects the "main vein that drains the brain!"
