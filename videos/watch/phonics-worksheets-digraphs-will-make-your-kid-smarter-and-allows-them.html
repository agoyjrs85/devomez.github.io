The very best time for kids to begin discovering to read is at a young age - even prior to they enter pre-school. They are naturally captivated by the printed texts they see and are eager to learn more about the noises made by those letters. You will likely discover that your young child likes to look at books and thoroughly takes pleasure in being read to.
