We always knew he was a trooper, and even now he’s still holding on with everything he has left to see everyone one last time.

Riding down in his chariot, going for a dip in the dam and settling in to watch all the doggies run past after the lure. Whilst we’ve been made fully aware of his time left, he’s making it very difficult for us to know when enough is enough. Being Charlie right up to the end, doing it his way and not missing out on a thing.

At lunch time, Luke’s Nanna’s funeral service was streamed onto our mobile and Zara the Labrador didn’t leave Luke’s side. She also brought him a big smile with saving his life in the pool later in the day.

Lots of fun in the pool today, it’s always a favourite with Wednesday’s pack. Seeing their joyous jumps and happy swimming was just what we needed today. Zac’s confident leaps in with a grin and brave face, Minnie’s cute jumps after her Uncle Luke, Annabelle and Zara’s belly flops, Kona’s kangaroo hops, Luna flying, Bandit pouncing on the big ball and even catching Blue the Samoyed jump in (he’s very elusive but Aunty Sam had the camera ready today!).
