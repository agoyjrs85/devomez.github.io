Professor in the Electronics and Telecommunications Department, Politecnico di Torino, where Daniele Trinchero founded and directs the iXem Labs. He carries on research and collaborates on projects involving industrial wireless communications, sensor networks and internet, and solutions for the reduction of digital divide. He spent three years in the Middle East where he directed projects related to sustainable development. His current focus is on high-tech platforms aimed at the protection of water resources and mountain environments as well as on the adoption of natural practices in agriculture. He is a member of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers.
