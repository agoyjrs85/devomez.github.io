Portfolio manager Michael Pento says the pre-COVID financial system was not normal. He runs down why the vaccines are not a cure-all and the risks for the future.

However, for the next six months, Michael is bearish because he sees a short term rise in real interest rates. he is bullish on gold long term because he sees a slow down in economic growth again mid-2021.

Michael addresses supposed gold and silver manipulation. He says Wall Street does not have an incentive to merely suppress the price. Instead, he believes prices are manipulated up as well as down.
