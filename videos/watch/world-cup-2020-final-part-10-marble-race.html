Welcome to the Marble Race World Cup for 2020!

There are 200 participating countries, including a few territories.

The last 25 countries to pass will be up for elimination.

In the beginning of each part, the 25 nominated countries from the previous part will race against each other for a chance to return to the game, however only the top 5 will be able to do so.

Last year there was a voting that would decide which countries would return, however some issues came from doing this, so this year luck will decide which countries come back.

Countries may return an unlimited amount of times.
