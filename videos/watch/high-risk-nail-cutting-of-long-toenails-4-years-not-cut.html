After watching Toe Bro videos on YouTube and seeing all the different foot problems people suffer with, a young girl began asking her parents, uncles and aunts and even close friends how their feet were. Once a month she visits her great aunt (90+ years old!!!) who lives 45mins away and on her last visit she asked to check her great aunt’s feet and…these were her nails!!!!!

This patient has very poor blood supply to her feet. Her skin was very tight, fragile and it has made her nails very brittle. One little cut could cause serious problems for her due to the possibility of not healing properly. This made her treatment considered high risk and lots of care was needed.

Thanks to her kind niece, this tough woman was able to finally have her feet looked at!!!

These feet could belong to someone so close to you but you would never know unless you ask or look. They could be suffering from long, thick and hard nails that they are unable to care for.

Bye, bye long brittle nails.
