0:36 #1 Range Rover Sentinel

Rather than lose money and let custom workshops modify their SUVs, Land Rover chose to jump into the armoring business and released a factory-made version of the Range Rover Autobiography with protective equipment.

1:46 #2 Karlmann King

The most expensive SUV on today’s market reigns over the competition with an imposing name King. The designers team from Europe was inspired by the Lockheed Martin Nighthawk stealth fighter and transitioned the angular design onto the body.

2:56 #3 Sand Cat

Among Plasan’s best-selling vehicles is the Sandcat which was thoroughly updated in 2018 and serves as a versatile platform for reconnaissance, border patrol, police and even military operations.

4:06 #4 INKAS® Mercedes-Benz G63 Bulletproof Limo

INKAS® Mercedes-Benz G63 Bulletproof Limo is built on the extended platform of the 2018 Mercedes Bens G63 AMG, and it offers the highest armor level by international standards - BR7.

5:07 #5 Rezvani Tank

You are probably familiar with Rezvani for their miniature supercars with astounding power-to-weight ratio, but they are actively trying to expand the portfolio by offering an extreme utility vehicle with an intimidating name TANK.

6:18 #6 Becker Automotive Design Cadillac Escalade ESV

Since the Escalade is among the favorite platforms of American armorers, when it comes to building discreet vehicles with luxury interiors, the world famous Becker Automotive Design works with this platform as well.

7:28 #7 Alpha Titan

Alpha Titan is the ultimate version of the special edition Mercedes-Benz G500 4x4 Squared that now accommodates 6 passengers and offers the highest level of ballistic protection.

8:32 #8 Toyo Ferret

Toyo Ferret is a 6-wheeled hybrid between a tank and an ATV, based on the Ferret Scout reconnaissance vehicle that was heavily revised by Action Vehicles Engineering and Toyo Tires.

9:40 #9 Inkas Armored Sprinter Van

Inkas Armored Sprinter Van offers full interior and exterior customization with BR6-level bulletproofing and armoring.
