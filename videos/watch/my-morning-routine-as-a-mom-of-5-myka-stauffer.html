My Morning Routine As A Mom Of 5! This is how my mornings go with 5 kids during the week. Our youngest, Onyx, is almost 5 months old and gets a lot of my attention in the morning before he takes his first nap and I can then start my morning routine. My makeup and hair routine is pretty simple and is dependent on how the morning is going thus far. Some days I just don't have time to do anything crazy and thats ok too :) While I'm getting ready my husband will help get the other 4 kids fed and going for the day before I help pick out their outfits. I like to eat breakfast a little later in the morning before homeschool starts to take a minute to my myself while Onyx sleeps and also review my plan and figure out what I want to accomplish for the day.
