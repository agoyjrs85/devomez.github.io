India’s dashing wicketkeeper-batsman Rishabh Pant has been rewarded for his tremendous effort with the bat in recent times. The diminutive cricketer was on Monday awarded the ICC Player of the Month award by the International Cricket Council.
Pant was awarded for his performances in the two Tests against Australia where he scored a 97 at Sydney and an unbeaten 89 at Brisbane that led India to a historic series win against arch-rivals Australia.
