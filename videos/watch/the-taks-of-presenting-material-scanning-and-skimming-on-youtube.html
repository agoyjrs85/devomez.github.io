Skimming is a technique of Reading quickly and only in passing to get an overall and general impression.

Definition of scanning is a speed reading technique to get information without reading the rest of the reading. Usually scanning is used to look up phone numbers, look up words in a dictionary, look for entries in an index, look for statistics, look for television shows and search for travel listings.
