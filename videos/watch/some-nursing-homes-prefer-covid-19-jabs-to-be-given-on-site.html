At least one nursing home has said it prefers residents not to travel elsewhere to get their COVID-19 jabs. It said that is to cut down on the risk of contracting the virus outside the nursing home. While they are still waiting for the Health Ministry to roll out plans, homes said not all may want to get vaccinated.
