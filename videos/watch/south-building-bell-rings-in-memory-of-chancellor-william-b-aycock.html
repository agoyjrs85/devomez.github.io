In memory of Chancellor William B. Aycock, the University tolled the South Building bell at the start of his memorial service on June 25, 2015.

It is a University tradition to toll the South Building bell on special occasions in the life of the University. It rang for most recently for Chancellor Carol L. Folt’s installation in 2013 and for the memorial service of President William Friday in 2012.
