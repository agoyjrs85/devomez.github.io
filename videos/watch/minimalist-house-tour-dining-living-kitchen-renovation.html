Hi everyone, I hope you've all had a great week:) Today I'm sharing our new minimalist house tour and the before and after getting to this point. It was messy that's for sure!

We tried to shop independent wherever possible and found some beautiful home decor brands along the way. And I was chuffed to find some really affordable yet stylish pieces on the High Street too. Hopefully proving you don't have to spend a fortune doing up your home, it's just a case of searching for the right pieces.

We reused, re-upholstered and even got handy with the spray paint doing up this house. Saving the pennies where possible and splurging on those items that I just couldn't resist! It's been exhausting but exhilarating, and I feel so lucky to finally have a house look just as we imagined:)
