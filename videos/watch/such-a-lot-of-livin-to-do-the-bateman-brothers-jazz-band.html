The Bateman Brothers Jazz Band pay tribute to Louis Armstrong and his All-Stars with this not often played version of Such A Lot of Livin' To Do. Performed in July 2011 at The Concorde Club, Eastleigh. It's short and sweet but an excellent example of how the band lulls it's audience with a nice gentle vocal before kicking off with a rousing second chorus - one of the band's trademarks!
