A recent survey showed 1 in 4 people with diabetes are rationing or skipping doses of insulin because of skyrocketing prices. Lorena Wright, a UW Medicine endocrinologist and diabetes specialist, describes what she's seen in her practice. She adds that some of her patients drive to Canada to be able to afford to purchase the insulin they need. Speak with your doctor to find out what resources and alternatives are available.
