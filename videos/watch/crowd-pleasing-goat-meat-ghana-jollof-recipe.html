Jollof rice is a popular dish among most West African countries. You will find this great dish at almost every Ghanaian social gathering be it wedding, outdooring, birthday party or funeral. I hope you have fun trying this recipe.

5 medium size tomatoes
2 large onions
6 cloves garlic
220 grams tomato paste
2 thumb sized ginger
2 star anise
1/2 teaspoon aniseed
1 teaspoon curry powder
3 bay leaves
1 branch fresh rosemary
1 Maggie shrimp tablet
1 cup cooking oil
6 cups rice
6 cups water
salt (according to your taste)
