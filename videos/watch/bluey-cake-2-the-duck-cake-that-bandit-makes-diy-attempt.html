Here is an attempt to make the duck cake as seen in Part 2 of Season 2 of Bluey. The results are... entertaining.

In the preview for the second part of Season 2 of Bluey there is a few snippets of Bandit attempting to make a duck cake. Those few seconds inspired this attempt at the cake and the end result is part funny and part nightmarish.

It should have been a warning sign seeing popcorn and chips being used on a cake. Anyway, it was fun to have a go and you can watch this video to learn some important lessons on making this ridiculous duck cake if for some reason you want to try it.
