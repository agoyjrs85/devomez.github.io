Coronavirus vaccines and health care in Canada's provinces dominated talks during the First Ministers Meeting between Prime Minister Justin Trudeau and the country's premiers.

The morning was spent on the COVID-19 vaccine rollout across the nation, but as the topic changed to health care in the afternoon, premiers tried to get Trudeau to commit to giving the provinces more money for health care.

David Akin explains what happened in the meeting.
