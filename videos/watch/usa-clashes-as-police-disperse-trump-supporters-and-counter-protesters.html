Trump supporters clashed with counter-protesters and police officers on Saturday night in Washington DC.

The counter-protesters stood outside the Capital Hilton hotel where the US president supporters were staying after a peaceful day of protests decrying the election results.

"Let it ring throughout the city of the District of Communism, USA" the crowds chanted outside the hotel.

Large numbers of police officers blocked the access to the Capital Hilton hotel and dispersed the crowds when counter-protesters tried to clash with US President supporters.

The massive Law and Order flag was displayed along Pennsylvania Avenue outside of Trump's hotel in DC for the 'Million MAGA March.'

At least 20 people were arrested and two officers with the DC Metropolitan Police were injured according to authorities.
