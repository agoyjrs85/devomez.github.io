In haemorrhoids there are swollen, inflamed veins around the anus or lower rectum. They may be either inside the anus or under the skin around the anus. One of the commonest cause behind hemorrhoids is straining to have a bowel movement. Other factors include pregnancy, ageing and chronic constipation or diarrhoea.
