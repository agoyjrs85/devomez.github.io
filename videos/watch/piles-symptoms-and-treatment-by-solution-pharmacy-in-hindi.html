In haemorrhoids there are swollen, inflamed veins around the anus or lower rectum. They may be either inside the anus or under the skin around the anus. One of the commonest cause behind hemorrhoids is straining to have a bowel movement. Other factors include pregnancy, ageing and chronic constipation or diarrhoea. Mostly hard stool in case of constipation is the prime cause. Hemorrhoids are not gender specific and are very common in both men and women. About half of all people have hemorrhoids by age 50.
