Piles is a disorder of the digestive system which precipitates the anal canal. in the case of piles, there is an increase in the length of blood vessels which stuck at the exit site of stool, the primary cause for piles or bhagandar is constipation. When we face constipation our stools become hard and it become difficult to pass. and while passing this hard stools it may cause a hard friction with the muscle of anyus an initiate burning sensation along with blood and pain. and Haemorrhoids is a digestive system disorder which appears at the end of the anus. In haemorrhoids there are swollen, inflamed veins around the anus or lower rectum. They may be either inside the anus or under the skin around the anus. One of the commonest cause behind hemorrhoids is straining to have a bowel movement. Other factors include pregnancy, ageing and chronic constipation or diarrhoea. Mostly hard stool in case of constipation is the prime cause. Hemorrhoids are not gender specific and are very common in both men and women. About half of all people have hemorrhoids by age 50. The most common symptom of hemorrhoids inside the anus is blood covering the stool. In any case these symptoms disappeared in 03-04 days, If not then it’s a matter of problem.
