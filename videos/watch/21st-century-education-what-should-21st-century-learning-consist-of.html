Paul Romani (M.Ed.) presents Pear Tree Education's view on what 21st century learning should consist:
+ integrated subjects (through theme-based & project-based learning)
+ technology
+ hands-on learning
+ collaboration (student-student, student-teacher, and teacher-teacher collaboration)
+ student-centered learning
+ creativity / innovation
+ entrepreneurship

This is an introduction to a series of videos discussing these different elements of 21st century education, as well as the problems of implementation such an education (such as public spending cutbacks).
