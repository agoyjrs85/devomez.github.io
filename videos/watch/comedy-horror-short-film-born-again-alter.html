More About "Born Again" :
Five wannabe satanists set out to to summon the devil, but things are going wrong from the start. Their pregnant sacrifice can't hold her contractions while they wait for doofus Doug, who's supposed to deliver the book of incantations they need, but he's been held up at his job at Best Buy. When he finally does show, it's one hilarious miscue after another, right up to a bloody and unforgettable holy shit twist.

They're trying to end the world, and they'll be damned if they can get it right. Born Again. Worst. Satanists. Ever.
