According to Asian Calendars today is "Daesoel" meaning "big snow".. and parts of Chungcheong-do and Gyeongsang-do Provinces saw heavier snowfall with heavy snow advisories being issued. Meanwhile, a dry weather advisory has been in place in the east for nearly a week now...so stay hydrated.

Temperature readings will be similar to slightly higher than yesterday... Seoul will top out at 6 degrees Celsius,... while those of you in Daejeon could see snow turning to rain later in the afternoon.

Temperatures will plummet again on Friday with a low of minus 7... then this weekend is forecast to be snowy one... then bone chilling temperatures are in store for early next week.

With that, let's take a look at the international weather for viewers around the world.

While temperatures will ease a bit compared to yesterday with a mix of snow and rain expected some parts in South Korea, parts of North Korea will be under plenty of sunshine together with icy cold temperatures.

As for major cities in Asia, Beijing will be under clear skies with passing clouds.

Meanwhile, those of you in Sydney and Perth will be under plenty of sunshine with highs nearing 30 degrees Celsius.

Heading to North America, major cities will be under bright skies,... while highs will range from negative territory to mid twenties depending on the region.

As for South America, those in Sao Paulo will need to keep an umbrella handy on Thursday.

Taking you to Europe,...London, Paris and Stockholm will have a rainy Thursday.

Lastly to Africa, it will be sizzling hot in Cape Town at a high of 35 degrees.
