OPPO has recently launched their 2020 midrange device - The OPPO F15, and in today’s video let’s compare it with a similar phone from the 20k price range - The Samsung A50s.

Both OPPO and Samsung have opted for a 6.4 inch AMOLED screen with Full HD+ resolution as well as a tear drop notch up top. The major difference here is that OPPO goes with Gorilla Glass 5 for the front panel while Samsung stays with Gorilla Glass 3.

When it comes to the internals we have the Mediatek Helio P70 on the OPPO F15 while the A50s comes with the Exynos 9611! These SoCs are coupled with 8/128 GB for the F15 and 4/128 and 6/128 GB variants for the A50s when it comes to RAM and internal storage respectively. We also have dedicated micro sd slots on both phones.

Coming down to batteries, we have a 4,025mAh unit on the OPPO F15, while the Samsung A50s sports a slightly smaller 4,000mAh battery. As far as charging speeds go, we have VOOC 3.0 fast charging on the OPPO F15 while the Samsung comes bundled with a 15 Watt charger in the box.

Coming to the cameras, we have a quad camera setup on the OPPO and triple camera setup on the samsung. Both phones have a 48MP main sensor that is paired with an 8MP Ultrawide sensor and a 2MP depth sensor! The F15 comes with and extra 2MP macro sensor! On the selfie side of things we have a 16MP snapper on the OPPO F15 while the A50s has a 32MP selfie shooter!

Finally on the software side of things we have Color OS 6.1 on board the OPPO F15 while the Samsung A50s is running on One UI 1.5 - both are based on Android 9 Pie.
