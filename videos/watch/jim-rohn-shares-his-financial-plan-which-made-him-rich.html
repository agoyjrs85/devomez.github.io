Jim Rohn was broke at age 25, however, at age 31 he became financially independent. He became a millionaire at age 31. What happened between age 25 and 31 was that Jim Rohn amended his philosophy about wealth and all other values of life. He developed a wealth plan which he shared in this video. What matters is not the amount of money you have, what matters is how you allocate the money you have. Wise allocation of money over a couple of years can take you from being broke to being a Millionaire - Jim Rohn
