Piano & Vocal Demo recorded by Elton John in 1969 or 1970 at Dick James Studios. The song was never recorded further than this and was left out of the collection of songs which became both the "Elton John" and "Tumbleweed Collection" albums. Eventually this demo was released as a bonus track about 40 years later!
