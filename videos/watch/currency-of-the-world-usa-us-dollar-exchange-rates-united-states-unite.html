The United States of America (USA) is a country consisting of 50 states, a federal district, five major self-governing

territories, and various possessions. Most of the country is located in North America between Canada and Mexico.

The population of the United States of America is about 328,2 million people.

The largest five cities in the United States of America are: New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Dallas–Fort Worth, Houston. The

capital city is Washington, D.C..

The official currency of the United States of America is the United States dollar (USD).

United States banknotes are: 1 dollar, 2 dollars, 5 dollars, 10 dollars, 20 dollars, 50 dollars, 100 dollars.
United States coins are: 1 cent, 5 cents, 10 cents, 25 cents, 50 cents, 1 dollar.
