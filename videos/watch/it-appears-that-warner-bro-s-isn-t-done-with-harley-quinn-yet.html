Jeremy Conrad from DCEU Mythic is reporting that his "multiple" sources are claiming that Warner Bro's isn't done with Margot Robbie's Harley Quinn yet. This makes sense seeing as Robbie's an integral part to the success of the DC Extended Universe, and people do love her portrayal of the character.
