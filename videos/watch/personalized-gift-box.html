Hi everyone!
Here's a simple video with an idea for a DIY personalized gift box (great for special occasions or birthdays). I filmed this during the holiday season and it was a Christmas gift for friends but you can do this for whatever occasion you want/need. You only need to think of the person you're giving it to, select a few items that you know they'll like, reuse a box and wrap it in a cute way. And voilá!. They'll love it and they'll know it was handpicked by you.

DIY Gifts are great! Have fun creating!
