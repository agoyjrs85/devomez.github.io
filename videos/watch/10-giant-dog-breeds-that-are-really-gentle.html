When it comes to dogs, most people will judge a book by its cover and decide what kind of temperament a dog has just by its looks alone.

Giant dog breeds can look very intimidating, but most are surprised to find out these dogs are just gentle giants wanting to snuggle.
Here are 10 dog breeds you probably thought were tough cookies but are actually surprisingly gentle. They are : Newfoundland dog, Bernese Mountain Dog, Leonberger, Irish Wolfhound, Great Pyrenees, Bullmastiff, Greater Swiss Mountain Dog, Saint Bernard, English Mastiff, Great Dane.
