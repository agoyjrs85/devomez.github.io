In this lesson I'll introduce you to 14 different English idioms using metaphors from food and drink. Idioms are words or phrases that mean something you might not expect from the words' normal meaning. For example, if something is "fishy", it means that it is suspicious. I will teach you the meaning of "which side your bread is buttered", "nice as pie", "gravy train", "not my cup of tea", "crying over spilt milk", and more.
