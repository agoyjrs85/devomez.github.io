Throughout control there are Hidden Locations that reward you with ability points when you find them. The locations that are shown on screen are the starting fast travel points, not necessarily where the hidden location is.

To access most of them, you will need the levitate ability. In a couple of cases you'll need the ground slam attack to reach them and in some cases you may also need the mold pill from Dr. Underhill, you get that during the 'Old Growth' side mission.
