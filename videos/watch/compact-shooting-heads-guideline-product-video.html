In this video Matt Hayes talks with Guideline Chief Product Designer, Leif Stävmo, about the unique features and where to use these short shooting heads.

The COMPACT lines are specialized tools for fishing small to medium sized rivers or in slow flowing waters where a longer sinking head would be hard to lift out for a new cas. All the shooting heads are prelooped for easy and fast change.
