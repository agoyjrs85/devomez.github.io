BEAUTIFUL DIY CRAFTS AND IDEAS THAT WILL TURN YOU INTO A PRO



Are you looking for new ways to decorate your home and give your house some sparkle? In this video, I show you some brilliant ideas and DIYs to try in order to have fun and enjoy yourself.

In the first video section, I show you how to take your marker and adding different colored inks to it, turn it into a rainbow marker. Or you can try the salt and glue trick. Where you draw some letters on a piece of paper using glue, then you add some salt on top, and using ink gives it some color.

Another great marker trick is to cut some slices off your marker to draw multiple lines with it.

Instead of using notebooks, you can take an old DVD case and use it as a note-taker. Simply, write on it with a marker, then wipe it off with a sponger and reuse as many times as you like.

If your markers are water-soluble, you can use them to give your drawings watercolor effects too.
