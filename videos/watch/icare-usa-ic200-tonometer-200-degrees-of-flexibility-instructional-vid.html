The Icare ic200 tonometer is designed
for professional use in the clinic as well
as the operating and emergency rooms.
The ic200 is fully portable, requires no
anesthetic and its positional freedom
allows measuring whether the patient is
sitting, supine or in an elevated position. A
light indicator at the probe base confirms
your positioning of the tonometer prior to
measurement. A green light indicates the
measurement will be reliable, a red light
indicates incorrect positioning.
