We asked four Oxford students about whether using the internet can sometimes make our lives less healthy and happy.
For more on the security risk that Chris mentions at 4.15, check out https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/technology-48262681

Shona uses the internet to get news and share information, while Chris enjoys new technologies such as dockless bike sharing and travel websites. Mick says that the Internet can help you make connections, whether you're a member of a minority group or a person living away from home, and Rebecca points out the impact of the Internet on modern medicine. And -- spoiler alert -- everyone agrees that it's great to be able to access study materials online rather than arguing over shared books or trawling through card catalogues!
