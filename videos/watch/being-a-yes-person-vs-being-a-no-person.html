Now I’m a 100 percent believer in energy over matter, in quality over quantity.
I will never commit to anything just to make someone happy whether it’s my parents relatives close friends or even business ventures just to look good, show I’m a good person, or gain something. Understand one very important thing when I say no to doing something it’s because I don’t feel the want to do it from my heart and soul and I know if I do it out of some sort of duty even though I’ll be smiling or the outside my inside energy toward that person or that thing will be so negative or low that honestly it’s that vibration that I have truly left with that person.
Everything means everything is energy and understanding the law of attraction is so important to your life and what u attract. That’s why a lot of people say, ‘Ohhh why does so many bad things happen to that good person’? You know why, it’s because on the outside they display a false sense of what is because they think it makes them look good or be good by social standards and expectations but, inside they are not happy and vibrate on that lower energy out to the universe. The universe is only interested in the vibration because you can not fake emotions but you can act out physical behaviours.
So I tell you my friends, learn to do things with truth and integrity even if it means towards loved ones and dear ones. If they get offended by you being real and honest then it’s actually their issue to deal with and it’s something for them to grow through.
So don’t live your life trying to be a good person rather be a honest person because you can’t fake your vibration. 🙏🏼🙏🏼🙏🏼
