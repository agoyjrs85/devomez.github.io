Everything means everything is energy and understanding the law of attraction is so important to your life and what u attract. That’s why a lot of people say, ‘Ohhh why does so many bad things happen to that good person’? You know why, it’s because on the outside they display a false sense of what is because they think it makes them look good or be good by social standards and expectations but, inside they are not happy and vibrate on that lower energy out to the universe. The universe is only interested in the vibration because you can not fake emotions but you can act out physical behaviours.

So don’t live your life trying to be a good person rather be a honest person because you can’t fake your vibration. 🙏🏼🙏🏼🙏🏼
