Jim Rogers is one of the world's largest investors who like to talk about the stocks market, investing tips, and ways to make money. In this interview, Jim Rogers said: "I knew nothing about Wall Street. I knew it was in New York. I knew that something bad had happened in 1929. I didn't know there was a difference in stocks and bonds. I thought they were the same thing so I knew nothing about investing. I'd never invested, never thought about it. Surely read about the crisis of 1929 and the depression we all had, but I, I had not a clue. But I did quickly fall in love because this was a place where they would pay me to do to know what was going on in the world and that's what was my passion."
