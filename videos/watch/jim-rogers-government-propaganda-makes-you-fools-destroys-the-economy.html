Jim Rogers is one of the world's largest investors who like to talk about the stocks market, investing tips, and ways to make money. Surely read about the crisis of 1929 and the depression we all had, but I, I had not a clue. But I did quickly fall in love because this was a place where they would pay me to do to know what was going on in the world and that's what was my passion."
