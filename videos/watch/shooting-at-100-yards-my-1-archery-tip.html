Hunter shoots his Athens Solace (70 lbs 26" draw) at 100 yards and shares his #1 tip for becoming a better archer.

My number one tip for improving your shooting is to push your limits at long range. Long range for one person can be totally different than long range for another person. A new archer's long range may be 30-40 yards. My long range is 100 (sight is maxed out at 100). Cameron Hanes has left us all behind and his longs range is a target on the moon I think. The point is you need to get out of your comfort zone if you want to improve. And it is crazy how easy 50 yards feels after shooting a couple groups at 100... 20 yards feels like you can touch the target!
