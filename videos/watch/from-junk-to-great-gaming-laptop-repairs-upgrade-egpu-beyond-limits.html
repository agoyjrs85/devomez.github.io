📍 The Laptop model is HP Probook 6450B, upgrades from i3 370M to i5 450M, from 2GB RAM to 8GB RAM, SSD instead HDD.

📍 The eGPU is made from: EXP GDC v8.5, express card, GTX960 4GB, 550w PCU and simple aluminum case.

📍 To setup the eGPU i need to do all manually. Searching and installing drivers, making some changes in drivers and i used DIY eGPU software ( making custom dsdt and few additional settings )

📍 Games in this video: Serious Sam 3 BFE and Metro: Exodus

📍 The performances with eGPU are good ( I mean on 1st gen ), but personally I Will NOT recommend the eGPU for 1st Gen Intel CPU's. Setup may be very complicated with some models and the FPS is not very stable.

📍✔️ Recommended 2nd gen Intel Core i3, i5 or i7 CPU's OR NEWER.
