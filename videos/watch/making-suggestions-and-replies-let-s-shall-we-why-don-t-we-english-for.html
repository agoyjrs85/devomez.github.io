Make a Suggestion, Invite or ask someone to do something - Let's ... / Shall We ... ? / Why Don't We ... ? / Would you like to ...? We will practice 8 ideas when inviting or asking people to do something and replies. All the language in this video is listed below. Each conversation is illustrated and color coded for better understanding. This is an interactive listening and speaking video. Please listen and then repeat after Mark. Have fun learning about how to ask people to do things and speak English now.

English in use:
Let's go to London. I've always wanted to go there.
What about going out to dinner? Let's stay home instead.
How about seeing a movie? Sounds good to me.
Shall we get a drink? I was thinking the same.
Would you like to watch TV? What's on?
Why don't we order a pizza? Yes, I love pizza!
What would you like to do tonight? Let's stay home.
Let's go for a ride. Yes, let's do it.
