Join me in the flight deck live in the ZIBO MOD 737 as we apply winter ops procedures.

Starting off in Murmansk (ULMM) in North Russia where it’s currently -10 we’re going to look at De-Ice/Anti-Ice procedures and how to configure the aircraft for departure in freezing conditions.

We’ll then be flying 90 minutes South to St Petersburg (ULLI) where VATRUS have full ATC coverage, we’ll again look at the implications of approaching an airport in frigid conditions!

Make sure you bring plenty of warm clothes!
