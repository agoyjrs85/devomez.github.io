Jim Pope shows us his beginnings into the sport of climbing and his progression into competition climbing. Jim's journey starts in the Lake District and catches up to his present day visit to Norway to sample some of the most iconic lines Norway has to offer. With mentors Gaz Parry and Gavin Symonds in toe, we explore his connections with the landscapes around him and the people who enabled him to be one of the UK's best climbers.
