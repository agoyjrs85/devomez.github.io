Whenever or wherever you put on flip flops/sandals/thongs, one word comes to mind - relaxation. Sun, water sand....
Unfortunately even with the best things in life, there is a downside - even the worlds favorite footwear can bite. While some suggest fabric straps as an alternative, these are not comfortable when wet and do not dry fast. We wanted to create a flip flop strap that reduced the chance of abrasions or blisters using an innovative design and a proprietary rubber blend . Our rolled inner seam reduces friction and reduces the chance the strap will bite into your skin even when wet.
We combined this passion with purpose and plan to donate a portion of every sale to help support education for girls in countries where they are particularly not given access.
We are also introducing rewards for recycling or repurposing the flip flops (with our DIY suggested projects).
