Shiva Oswal, a resident of Cupertino, CA made game show history during his appearance on Who Wants To Be a Millionaire during November 2017. He won $250,000 becoming the youngest player ever (age: 13 years), worldwide to win $250,000 on 'Millionaire.' Shiva's appearance in 2017 was during season 16 of Who Wants to Be A Millionaire, on Nov 14 and Nov 15, 2017, episodes 47 and 48, 'Whiz Kids Week'.

In 2018, Shiva was invited back to Millionaire on a special show celebrating Millionaire's $100 Million Dollar Season: https://youtu.be/9qZGiVqffTA. During this return visit, Shiva added another $50,000 to his 'Millionaire' winnings, and made history in two ways:
1. The only kid in Millionaire history to be invited twice
2. The youngest contestant worldwide to win a total of $300,000 in 'Millionaire' history!
