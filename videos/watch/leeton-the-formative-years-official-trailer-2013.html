Here it is Ladies and Gentleman, the official trailer for Leeton - The Formative Years.


A documentary which takes you back to the first decade of the township of Leeton, in the Riverina of New South Wales. From 1910 to New Years Eve 1919, we witness and experience the birth of a town, from the beginnings of the Irrigation Scheme to the affects of the Great War. Leeton - The Formative Years blends historical fact with captivating drama to bring to life the unique beginning of the town of Leeton.
