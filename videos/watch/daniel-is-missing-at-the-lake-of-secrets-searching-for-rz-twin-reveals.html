Now Matt Slays, Rebecca Zamolo and Maddie must split up and find Daniel and RZ twin. Rebecca overhears a secret meeting while Maddie finds a piece of RZ twin's mask. Matt found Daniels beanie but no sign of Daniel the camera man. They all meet back up and the ghostbuster car is missing now too.
