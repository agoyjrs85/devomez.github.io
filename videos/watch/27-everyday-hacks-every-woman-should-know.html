I show you how to make your own natural hair mask using rice, coconut flour, and coconut oil. This will make it easier for your hair to comp through and make your hair smoother.

You can use some tape to get rid of glitter on your face.

I show you a simple way to contour your lips and make them appear bigger using a dark shade concealer all around and then a lighter shade concealer on your cupids bow.

You can also rub some aloe vera on your stretch marks to remove them fast.
