Amazing beauty tricks and everyday solutions to all of your problems



I show you how to remove your contact lenses by folding some Q-tips and turning them into soft tongs. Just be careful while doing so.

Coat your lashes with Vaseline before applying mascara, and then warm up your lash curler to curl your lashes. This will make them appear longer.

I show you how to make your own natural hair mask using rice, coconut flour, and coconut oil. This will make it easier for your hair to comp through and make your hair smoother.

You can use some tape to get rid of glitter on your face.

I show you a simple way to contour your lips and make them appear bigger using a dark shade concealer all around and then a lighter shade concealer on your cupids bow.

It’s better to use natural skincare products than chemical products when you have somewhat of healthy skin, so if you suffer from dry skin, for example, you can use aloe vera to smooth it out.

You can also rub some aloe vera on your stretch marks to remove them fast.
