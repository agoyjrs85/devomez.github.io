The "construction crew" was living in a hole in the foundation of a large construction site. We worked with the project employees to get them out safely, but were not able to find mom after looking for several days (it was an active and very noisy construction site). Watch Bob, Mort and Hammy grow up to live incredible lives with their new families!

While this may sound like a unique story, did you know that outdoor/community cats produce 80% of the kittens that are born in the US each year? (Source: The Humane Society)
