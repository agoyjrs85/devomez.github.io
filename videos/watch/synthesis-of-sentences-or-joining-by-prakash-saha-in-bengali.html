Synthesis of sentences or joining by Prakash Saha in bengali
In this video I have highlighted english grammar synthesis of sentences or joining. At first I have classified joining three parts.
The parts are
1. Simple sentences + Simple sentences= Simple sentences
2. Simple sentences + Simple sentences = Complex sentences
3. Simple sentences + Simple sentences = Compound sentences
Then I have showed that we can join two or more than two simple sentences into a simple sentence with the help of ten different processes.
The processes are
1. Present Participle
2. Perfect Participle
3. Past Participle
4. Nominative Absolute
5. Noun in Apposition
6. Geround
7. Infinitive
8. Adjective
9. Adverb
10. Adverb "Too"
