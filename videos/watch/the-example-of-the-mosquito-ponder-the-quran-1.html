مثال البعوضة - تدبر القرآن الكريم، الحلقة الأولى
---------------
Ponder the Quran is a new series created with the intention of inspiring you to pick up a Quran and read it with reflection (tadabbur). Many of us read the Quran in a shallow sort of way; some in Arabic despite not understanding Arabic, and some with the translation, but without giving it much thought. How can we expect the miracle and truth of the Quran to become apparent to us without us searching for it? Pondering over the Quran unlocks a whole new dimension you never knew existed that proves to you 100% there could be no other author than Allah SWT. I hope I could show just how much we can extract from one verse in this episode!

Only in the last century or two have we uncovered so much about the mosquito. We all find them annoying, but because of the age of Science, they have proven to be incredibly impressive. Physicists constantly look to nature for inspiration on how to develop different technologies, and to this day, no human creation comes close to the precision and perfection of nature. How then in our limited intellects and abilities can we decide there is no Creator and no purpose for existing?

When this verse was revealed 1400 years ago, especially in Arabia, Science was non-existent. The majority of the Arabs were actually illiterate. If we really reflect on the wording of just this one verse, it really is an eye-opener!
