Everyone has a choice but sometimes some choose not to do certain things.

Those that choose not to are stronger and very much self aware and would rather not settle for something that may potentially take away their sanity . On the other hand it’s almost as if they’re empathetic as they put themselves in your shoes and when they wouldn’t like their own reaction, they’re actually international about considering your feelings and such really needs you to acknowledge simply by respecting them for being that humane.

People are able to:
Snap at you, Judge, Shame you, Hurt you, Give you an attitude, Be rude, Ratchet, To give you I don’t care vibes etc

But what do they do🤔they choose not to
They confront you in a nice way, Are understanding, Don’t shame you, Are compassionate, Are polite & care enough to consider your feelings.

If they can so can you because
You have so much more which means you can always do more than you are doing😉.
