Those that choose not to are stronger and very much self aware and would rather not settle for something that may potentially take away their sanity . Snap at you, Judge, Shame you, Hurt you, Give you an attitude, Be rude, Ratchet, To give you I don’t care vibes etc

But what do they do🤔they choose not to

If they can so can you because
You have so much more which means you can always do more than you are doing😉.
