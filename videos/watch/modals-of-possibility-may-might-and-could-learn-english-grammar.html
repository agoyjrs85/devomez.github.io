Modals of Possibility.

In this tutorial, you will learn 5 general rules about modal verbs and then I will look in detail at the modals of possibility: may, might and could. With example sentences and common errors analysed you can see how modals can help you sound more like a native. At the end of the video, you have the opportunity to test how much you have learned and understood.
