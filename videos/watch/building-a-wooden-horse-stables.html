What a summer it was, absolutely had no time to dedicate to youtube.
This video will introduce you to the process I am going through when delivering orders to the customer.
The process starts with the bunch of timber slowly converted into panels and assembled on the customer's site. No major ground preparation required, though, as long as it is relatively flat. Building using 3x2 framing with 16mm t&g shiplap, 4x2 roof purlins covered with 12mm OSB board and finished in Onduline roofing sheets. 4ft high kickboards are a standard option with our buildings.
If you would like to see more of my work related to horse stables and field shelters please visit www.stablebuildcompany.co.uk
