Harm & Ease present their new Single "Nothing More than Love", the bands' first official release since 2018's Black Magic Gold. The track was produced by Grammy Award winner Joe Chiccarelli (Elton John, The White Stripes, Foo Fighters) and mixed and mastered by Brian iele in Buenos Aires. The lyrics speak of the dispair of a broken heart. The fleeting nature of love itself and the embrace of chaos as a result.
