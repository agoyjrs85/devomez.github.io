The Trans Euro Trail (TeT) was kicking my ass so I went looking for an off-road router around that steep section. First I had to restock on water and in village nearby called Pe da Serra there was a fountain. There I found a group of Portuguese villages butchering a couple of lambs and they invited me for lunch. There I ate some good Portuguese food. Well fed I continue the ride down to Loule, passing through Evora where I have a quick look at the Roman temple and end up after a night ride in the Algarve.
