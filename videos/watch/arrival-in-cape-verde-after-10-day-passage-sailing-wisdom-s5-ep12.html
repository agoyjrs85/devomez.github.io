We spend a very beautiful final 2 days at sea before our approach to Cape Verde. Upon our arrival, however, we are told we must remain on the boat until the police and doctors come to us for a Covid test. The country is officially open, but after 4 days of waiting, we begin to dip too far into our food and water provisions for the crossing. We decide to go to shore with all of our boat and passport info so that we can get SIM cards and food.
