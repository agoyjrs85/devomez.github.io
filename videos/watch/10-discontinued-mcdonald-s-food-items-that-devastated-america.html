SUMMARIES:
- Mighty Wings were a McDonald's menu item from 2013 and ending in early 2014. Mighty Wings are juicy, bone-in chicken wings in a bold, spicy breading.
- McDLT was a burger with lettuce, tomato, and mayonnaise that McDonald's offered as a response to the Whopper.
- Fish McBites were a McDonald's menu item similar to the Chicken McBites.
- McDonald's released a new limited-time condiment, the Szechuan teriyaki dipping sauce. I
- The Spanish Omelet Bagel was a fairly simple recipe of ham, cheese, egg, and of course, a bagel.
- McDonald's founder, Ray Kroc had the right idea when he forbid the sale of hot dogs.
- The McJordan was a cheeseburger served on a sesame seed bun.
- The Strawberry Shortcake McFlurry would cost you 474 calories with 14 grams of fat.
- McPizza was a pizza product that McDonald's attempted starting in the late 1980's.
- McDonald's onion nuggets were clumps of diced onions that were breaded and fried à la chicken nuggets.
