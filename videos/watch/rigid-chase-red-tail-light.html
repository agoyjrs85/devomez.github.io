The RIGID Chase rear facing LED light adds safety and functionality to any UTV, buggy or 4x4 application by packing reverse, courtesy, strobe, running, and brake lights into a single compact package. A halo of powerful colored LEDs in red, amber, or blue around the perimeter of the aluminum housing can be used as a strobe (for racing and chase applications), running light, or third brake light. The center portion of the RIGID Chase functions as a 715 lumen white light suitable for use as a Reverse or area light, and there’s also a smaller downward-facing courtesy light for illuminating the cargo area.
