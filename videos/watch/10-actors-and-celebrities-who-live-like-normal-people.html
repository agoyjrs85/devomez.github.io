The word celebrity has become synonymous with fame and fortune. When we think of Hollywood stars, we assume they have a net worth of millions of dollars. And some of them do have a very impressive net worth. The lifestyle of the rich and famous is one that has long been famous for excess being your wildest dreams. We see celebs post on social media about their luxurious cars, make up, clothing, travel, and overall lifestyle.
However, there are those celebs who have an impressive net worth but impress us even more by doing a 180 and choosing to live a more frugal lifestyle. Don’t let those numbers fool you, some celebs are very careful with their cash and live just like we do. Well almost like we do. They don’t have to worry about financial stability but that doesn’t mean they can’t be smart with their money and save it. Makes sense to us.

One of the best known for being charitable is Keanu Reeves, who donated 85% of his salary from the Matrix to the special effects and costume team.

Shailene Woodley refuses to splurge and instead makes her own cosmetics. Mariska Hargitay doesn’t spend money for fear of not getting roles in the future. She grew up not having too much money and learned to save. Similarly Sarah Jessica Parker, grew up in a family of 8 and learned to be frugal as she lived on welfare as a kid.

Many of these celebs have net worths in the millions, who else do you think made our list?
