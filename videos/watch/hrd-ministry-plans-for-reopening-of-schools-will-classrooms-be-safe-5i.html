HRD Minister Ramesh Nishank Pokhriyal recently revealed that schools and colleges which have remained closed since around March 16 will reopen after August 2020. The Ministry is currently working on a plan to reduce the school syllabus as well as the school hours. The Ministry has sought suggestions from all stakeholders in this regard. Around 33 crore students have been waiting for school reopening news to dispel their doubts.
