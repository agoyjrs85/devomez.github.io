Tour the Fall 2020 Princess Margaret Home Lottery Showhome in Oakville, Ontario, designed by Brian Gluckstein. Order tickets now for your chance to win this Showhome, part of the $6 Million Grand Prize: https://tickets.princessmargaretlotto.com/

This Art Deco-inspired home is all about the details! Each room has its own unique architectural elements, as well as colored window trim to match. The grand, two-storey family room is the heart of the home, while the hardworking kitchen stuns with integrated appliances, a free-standing pantry and more. See how Brian uses black to make design details pop against the neutral palette and why he chose a different look for the staircase.

Note: At this time the showhome will not be open for tours.
