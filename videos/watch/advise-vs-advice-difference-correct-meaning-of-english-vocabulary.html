There is often confusion over the words "Advise and Advice":

----------***--------------------***---------------------------***---------------------***----------

The very quick answer is this:

* Advise is a verb.
* Advice is a noun.

----------***--------------------***---------------------------***---------------------***----------

Advise: The words advise is a verb meaning to give advice.

Example:

* The rich are advising the poor
* Attach yourself to those who advise you rather than praise you.

----------***--------------------***---------------------------***---------------------***----------

Advice: The word advice is a noun meaning a suggestion for a beneficial course of action.

Example:

* Take my advice. I don't use it anyway.
* This is my advice for you, please take care of it.
