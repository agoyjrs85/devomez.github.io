Algerians head to the polls later on Sunday for a constitutional referendum.
They will vote on a revised constitution the government has pitched as a reflection of the Hirak, the movement that has led weekly protests for more than a year.
Observers expect an extremely low turnout for the vote.

Al Jazeera’s Mohamed Vall reports on what is at stake.
