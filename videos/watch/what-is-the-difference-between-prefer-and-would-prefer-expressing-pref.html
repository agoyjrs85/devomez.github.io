What is the difference between prefer and would prefer

prefer and would prefer

Would Prefer (Prefer)
prefer  / would prefer means "to like or want someone or something more
than someone or something else" 

We choose one thing between different options. We can use "prefer" in
3 different forms in English

Prefer something to something else
- I prefer football to basketball.
- She would prefer tea to coffee

Prefer to do something rather than do something else
- They prefer to drive rather than go by bus.
