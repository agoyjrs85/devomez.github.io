Addiction is a serious problem that can affect every area of your life. While many people associate addiction with mental health issues, impulsivity, and getting involved with the wrong crowd, it can also come down to having an addictive personality. Simply put, some people are more prone to addictive tendencies than others. To explore more about addiction and certain tendencies, in this video, we’ll be taking a look at some of the signs of an addictive personality.
