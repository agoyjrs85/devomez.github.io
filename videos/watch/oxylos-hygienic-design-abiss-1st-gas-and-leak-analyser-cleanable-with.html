OXYLOS Hygienic Design is the only gas and leak analyzer in hygienic design for production. True helper for evaluation of shelf life, it measures and optimizes your packaging integrity in reproducible and controlled test conditions. Complying with DIN 55508-1 standard, this device gives direct measurement of leak with high sensitivity and speed (10μm in less than 10s), minimum stressing pressure (10 mbar) and minimum influencing variables. Easy to clean with a water jet, this automated device is safe for operators.
