In this 'Insights from the Master', Paramahamsa Vishwananda talks about the grace and opportunity to be on the spiritual path. Paramahamsa Vishwananda explains that the opportunity to fully surrender to the Divine doesn't happen in every life, and that it's up to each person to take advantage of the opportunity given in this life, as it is uncertain when it will come again.

In a time when millions of people are searching for answers to the never ending questions of life, the appeal of Paramahamsa Sri Swami Vishwananda is that he gives insights into life that go beyond religion, race, gender and social status. He stands out before us as the very embodiment of Love, ultimately inspiring us to live life with Love, faith, and trust in God.
