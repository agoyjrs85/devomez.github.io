這裡沒有封城，僅有七人死亡。儘管最近疫情捲土重來，但別忘了嚴守防疫原則，感謝辛苦的醫療人員。

2020年，新冠肺炎震驚全世界。短短幾個月，新冠肺炎病毒就蔓延到了180多個國家，一年之内就感染超過8000萬人，造成170萬人死亡。許多國家被迫鎖國。學校與企業倒閉、經濟重挫。但是，台灣仍然保持經濟成長，沒有落實任何封城計畫。台灣人維持正常的生活，能夠與家人和朋友聚會，參加馬拉松比賽，看電影、上學和工作。2020年，台灣只有7人死於新冠肺炎，為什麼台灣應付新冠肺炎能夠如此成功？

此節目由三立電視與Discovery聯合製作，跟著資深新聞人廖筱君與公衛專家連加恩踏上了一段揭秘的旅程，從邊境防疫、隔離政策、醫院管理，到大數據資通科技以及口罩供應鏈，剖析出台灣如何一步步擊敗新冠肺炎。

In the year 2020, COVID-19 terrorized the world. In just a few months’ time the new coronavirus spread to more than 180 countries, within a year it infected more than 80 million people and killed more than 1.7 million. Many countries went into lockdown. Schools and businesses closed. Economies retracted. Taiwan however was able to see economic growth and did not order any lockdowns.
People living in Taiwan were able to lead a mostly normal life, visit family and friends, participate in marathons, go to the movies, school and work. In 2020, only 7 people in Taiwan died from COVID. Why has Taiwan been so successful in beating COVID-19? Veteran news presenter Grace Liao and public health expert Dr. Allen Lien took a look at several key cases that put Taiwan’s epidemic prevention to the test, talked to experts and policy makers, and uncovered that policies of border control, quarantine, contact tracing, hospital management, national health insurance, mask supply control, and the use of information communications technology and big data played the most important roles. Grace and Allen share with the world Taiwan’s experience in how it has been beating COVID-19.
