Ochos…

If you look closely, you’ll notice that almost all tango movements start or finsih with an ocho.

They’re truly a pillar of tango dancing.

And, very often, they are one of the first movements you learn.

But despite that, ochos can often feel uncomfortable, especially in close embrace.

If it’s the case for you, it’s very common.

Maybe you feel that you’re losing your balance, or that your partner is uncomfortable.

Maybe you aren’t sure about the size of step you should take, or why your follower is pivoting too much…

If that’s the case, we’d like to invite you to change the way you look at the ochos.

See, we know that students are often focused on the technique learned in class… and that a simple change in the way they look at the ocho can work wonders !

So, if you’re looking to have ochos that feel more fluid and comfortable, make sure you watch this week’s video !

We teach you 2 simple yet powerful changes of ‘approach’, one for leaders, one for followers, so you can simplify the way you dance and feel more comfortable.
