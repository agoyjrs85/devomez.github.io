One of the evidences of a chemical reaction is formation of a precipitate. This video provides an example of this happening.

The balanced reaction for this is

CuSO4 + 2NaOH → Cu(OH)2+ Na2SO4

Copper sulfate
Sodium hydroxide
produces
Copper Hydroxide
