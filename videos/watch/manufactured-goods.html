This is an animation that I continued from my photomontage project. It is about the media and society and their effects on individuals. People are constantly trying to reach unrealistic goals that the media sets as "perfection." It tries to manufacture us into one single way of aesthetics. We all see celebrities and their fans destroying themselves to be better--- They have plastic surgery and dangerous weight goals, trying to cure their own body dysmorphic disorders. I used the destruction of beauty. I turned the media's idea of perfection into something ugly. The story ends with the character being judged. Of course, like in reality, it's not enough. They always want more. They are always waiting-- until there's nothing left.
