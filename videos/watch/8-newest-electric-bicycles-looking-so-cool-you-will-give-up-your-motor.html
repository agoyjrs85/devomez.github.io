When it comes to urban transportation, there are still many places on Earth, where two-wheel vehicles still prevail. Motorbikes, scooters and mopeds with pedals (that are used for better range) usually join cars and buses on the streets, if not make the most of traffic on the road. Compared to larger vehicles, these means of transportation are characterized for low running costs, great maneuverability and affordable starting costs.

Despite being so popular all over the world, two wheeled transport still has some improvements to do in order to provide more advantages for the rider. With this in mind, many companies are trying to apply the latest technologies to motorbikes, scooters and even bicycles. For example, with the application of all-electric motors, any bicycle can be turned into a more powerful commuting vehicle. It can work in either throttle-operated or pedal-assist mode. Either way, you get extra power that is stored in large capacity batteries to help you out.

Nowadays electric bicycles come in all shapes and sizes. B it seems that everyone seems to be excited the most about the motorcycle-styled models with large tires, retro headlights and brat seats. Here we are exploring the newest models within this family of pedelec or all-electric bikes, highlighting their major specifications and comparing the pricing ranges.
