Jared Harris ('The Terror') chats with Gold Derby's Jared Harris: Horror limited series succeeds because of 'patience and restraint in the storytelling.' Based on the book by Dan Simmons, "The Terror" is a fictionalized account of Captain John Franklin's (Ciarán Hinds) lost expedition to the Arctic from 1845-1848. Harris co-stars as Francis Crozier, captain of the HMS Terror, which sailed alongside Franklin's HMS Erebus.
