Jared Harris ('The Terror') chats with Gold Derby's Jared Harris: Horror limited series succeeds because of 'patience and restraint in the storytelling.' Harris co-stars as Francis Crozier, captain of the HMS Terror, which sailed alongside Franklin's HMS Erebus.
