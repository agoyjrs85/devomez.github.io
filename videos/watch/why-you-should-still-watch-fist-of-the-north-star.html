Fist Of The North star was a trailblazing anime and one of the largest media franchises to date. Kenshiro's fists created an entirely new genre of anime that would go on to influence generations of mangakas. If you haven't watched Fist Of The North Star, it's still important if you care about the history of how anime got to where it is now, or if you just want to crazy awesome pressure point "you're already dead" action.
