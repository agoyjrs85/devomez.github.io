How do you like your eggs? Scrambled? Poached? Boiled? Check out a top selection of ideas on how to prepare the best breakfast ever! And now you can cook pancakes from the bottle. You can even make a cake. Watch the tutorial! My favorite breakfast idea is to make an egg roll with cheese. The next breakfast idea is the coolest one! I love baked avocados as they are very tasty and look incredible. Cut avocados and arrange halves in a baking tray. Carefully separate yolks using a plastic bottle and gently put 1 egg yolk into every avocado half.
