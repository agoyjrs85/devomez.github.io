DELICIOUS IDEAS FOR FAMILY BREAKFAST

How do you like your eggs? Scrambled? Poached? Boiled? Check out a top selection of ideas on how to prepare the best breakfast ever! Brighten your morning with a super-delicious breakfast that is the best way to start a perfect day! Besides, all these ideas are incredibly easy to make. Amaze your family with your cooking skills. Start your day from delicious pancakes and mix all the ingredients in a plastic bottle. Take an empty plastic bottle and combine yogurt, eggs, sugar, and flour. And now you can cook pancakes from the bottle. You can even make a cake. Watch the tutorial! My favorite breakfast idea is to make an egg roll with cheese. Try this recipe and you will cook this dish again and again!
The next breakfast idea is the coolest one! I love baked avocados as they are very tasty and look incredible. Cut avocados and arrange halves in a baking tray. Carefully separate yolks using a plastic bottle and gently put 1 egg yolk into every avocado half. Season each filled avocado with paprika, dried bacon, ham, parsley or cheese and bake for 30 minutes. Egg balls wrapped in bacon is a delicious dish you can cook for dinner to amaze your friends. Firstly, prepare the rice and wrap an egg in rice using a plastic bag. After that take bacon slices and wrap the balls and fry. A cool way to store eggs for a long time is to freeze them.
