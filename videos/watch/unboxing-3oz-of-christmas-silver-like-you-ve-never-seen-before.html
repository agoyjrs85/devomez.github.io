In my thirty-fifth unboxing of 2020, there are 3 total pieces to show. 2 are gifts and the 3rd, is a very unique and special piece of silver.

Contact the US Mint here: usmint-support@usmcatalog.com

Copy and paste this form letter:

Hello, I hope all is well with you. I am a member of a large online community who purchase and collect precious metals and bullion products. Many of us shop directly from the US Mint, or from your largest distributors, such as APMEX, JM Bullion, etc.

We have been talking and communicating about products that we would like to see produced by the US Mint and sold however the mint determines is best. Two products in particular that we have identified and think would be incredibly popular are A.) fractional American Gold Buffalo coins (1/2 oz 1/4 oz, and 1/10 oz) similar to what you have created in the past. The other product we want to see is B.) 10 oz silver bars, similar to those produced by the royal canadian mint, but we want to buy American 10 oz bars from a sovereign mint. Serialized and with security features would be best.

We plan to start a petition so you can see the overwhelming support for these products. However, in the meantime, I hope that receiving this email from myself, and others like me, can help the process at the mint begin for planning these products.

That is it for now. I hope to hear back from you soon. Thank you.
