Working to support our service users to share their story is important to Livability. Sharing stories is a powerful way of engaging people’s views. Through stories, people can share life, develop connections and express needs and aspiration. Read more at http://www.livability.org.uk/share-story-listening-service-users/

To this end, the charity has recently started to run a number of 'Share Your Story' workshops, to provide another route for service users to share their experiences with the charity. The charity has just released a new film to show one of the workshops.
