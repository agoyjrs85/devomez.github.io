Dinosaurs is an American family sitcom television series that aired on ABC from 1991 through 1994, and reruns were shown on the Disney channel and eventually on their streaming service "Disney+." The show, about a family of anthropomorphic dinosaurs (portrayed by puppets), was produced by Michael Jacobs Productions and Jim Henson.
