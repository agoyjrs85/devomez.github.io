Morocco has recognizes Israel in exchange for US recognizing disputed Western Sahara as part of Morocco. US President Donald Trump today made this announcement about normalization of ties between Morocco and Israel. He further announced to to recognize Moroccan sovereignty over disputed Western Sahara. Western Sahara is disputed region between Moroccan government and Polisario front. United Nations does not recognize Western Sahara as part of Morocco.
