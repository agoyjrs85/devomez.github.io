Rural living offers some great advantages if you are over 50 and saving for retirement. I talk about my own experience moving to a rural area after 50 and the pros and cons. While its not for everyone, rural living offers some distinct advantages if you are willing to accept the challenges and embrace a new life!
After living more than 20 years in the city, I moved to my current location in the mountains a little over four years ago. In addition to be somewhere that inspires me, I was able to significantly lower my expenses, pay off debt and start my journey to a secure retirement. If you are thinking about moving to rural location some of these tips may help you decide.
