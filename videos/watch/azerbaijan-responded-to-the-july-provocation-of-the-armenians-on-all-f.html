On July 12, starting from noon hours, the Armenian armed forces have been beginning to roughly break the cease-fire in Tovuz region of the Azerbaijani-Armenian state borders. Azerbaijan territorial positions have been fired upon by artilleriya installations of Armenia. As a result of the aggression of Armenian Armed Forces, both sides have had losses. During ongoing battles, 12 military servants of the Azerbaijani Army- Major General Polad Hashimov, Colonel İlgar Mirzayev, Major Namig Ahmadov, Major Anar Novruzov, Yashar Babayev, Chief Lieutenant Rashad Mahmudov, Sergeant Vugar Sadıgov, Chief Soldier Elshad Mammadov, Soldier Khayyam Dashdamirov, Overtime Served Soldiers- Elchin Mustafazade, Nazim İsmayılov are the ones who have been bravely martyred. As a consequence of severe punishment measurements against the Armenian aggresions, the long-dated firing positions, inaction points, desert management station, reconnaissance unit's radiolocating station, head storage for military vehicles, tanks, armored personnel carriers, battalion headquarter, armored military equipments, military transportation vehicles, various combat equipment, X-55 tactical unmanned aerial vehicle, command posts, control post of artillery division , radioelectron combat point, battle posts, barracks, reserve parts for improved defense and considerable manpower have been destroyed by the accucate shots of Azerbaijan army. As always, Armenian Armed Forces have taken aim to shoot the civilians and civilian-located areas. Aghdam, Dondar Gushchu and Vahidli villages of Tovus are being fired at with large-caliber weapons and mortars. Consequently, residential houses have been severely ruined. Due to the current situation, thousands of patriotic sons and daughters of Azerbaijan, our compatriots living abroad, military reservists and war veterans are requesting to be called upon for military service in order to support our army. In this regard, thousands of appeals were made to the Ministry of Defense, the State Service for Mobilization and Conscription. Appeals are still ongoing. The youth is as active as state agencies in order to convey true voice of Azerbaijan. They are actively engaged in different social media sites to present accurate and unbiases information to the world. Volunteer young people has created a platform, called "Karabakh is Azerbaijan" to turn the international world's attention to Armenian aggression towards Azerbaijan, provocation of the enemy in the state borders of Azerbaijan, violation of ceasefire, tense situation in the front and its consequences. Also, hacker groups of Azerbaijan organize successful attacks to Armenian sites. Our compatriots living in different countries could not remain indifferent to what is happening in the country. Actions in support of Azerbaijan are being held in various cities of Turkey, Tabriz, Warsaw, Moscow, St. Petersburg, Washington, as well as in other cities. God Nisanov and Zarakh Iliev, heads of the Food City fruit base in Moscow, refused to buy products from Armenian entrepreneurs. Due to this, thousands of tons of Armenian products were spoiled. Although the embassy staff thanked the Armenian entrepreneurs after their appeal to the Armenian Embassy in Russia, Nisanov did not receive the Armenians and drove them out of the area. Representatives of different nations declared their supports for Azerbaijan and their readiness to struggle for Azerbaijan. Although the situation on the state border is relatively calm, the general situation remains tense. Armenians intentionally hiding their firing points near or behind their civilian targets.
