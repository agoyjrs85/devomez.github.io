Today I show how I mixed the LoFi Beat for my last video using the FL Studio Akai Fire controller. This is to give a flavour of some simple mixing techniques that can be used to give a Lofi vibe.
This was mixed on the fly while producing the beat and in this video I talk about why I did certain things and also how it could have been improved.
