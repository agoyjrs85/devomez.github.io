In 1970 on the second day after meeting Sai Baba at his Whitefield, India ashram, Sai Baba invited Howard Levin to live in his house. After that, their relationship only grew closer.

Howard is smart, reflective and has a great ability for recall. In this interview, the first of a two part series on Souljourns, Howard takes us back in time by fifty years. With a moment’s notice, alone with his encyclopedic memory, Howard is able to call up enriching stories of the many years he followed Sai Baba, worked for him and learned at Baba’s feet.

This interview will soon be followed by Part 2, a talk recorded by Gene Massey for the Hollywood, California Sai Center in the summer of 2020.
