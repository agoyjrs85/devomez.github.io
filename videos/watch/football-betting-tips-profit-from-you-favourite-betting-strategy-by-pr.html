If you want to be a football betting expert, you need to know some fundamental things about a football match. The key factor is how many goals are going to be scored. Because once you have worked that out, you can use it to make a judgment on value from a sports betting perspective.

Any football trading strategy would need to know this as it forms the basis for whether you deploy one particular strategy or another and also whether a match exists that actually fits your criteria.
