Managing Older Employees | How To Manage More Experienced Team Members

Did you hear? Apparently right now the workforce is made up of at least 5 generations! 5 generations! That is a bigger pot than what we have previously had and while that makes for some beautiful connections, it’s certainly not without its challenges. One of those challenges includes younger employees working with older employees or managing more experienced employees.

This can be a really challenging dynamic not just for you, but also for the experienced team member. So the question is how do you handle it right? How do you lead older employees, gain respect of older staff and motivate senior staff?

Well in today’s episode of Mak TV we will dive into five tips that will help you really feel more comfortable in your skin when it comes to managing older employees.
