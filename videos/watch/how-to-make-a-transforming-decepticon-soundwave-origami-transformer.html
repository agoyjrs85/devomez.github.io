This is a great educational paper craft to make for play, with your friends or with your family members. This paper craft / origami toy promotes creativity, story telling, eye hand co-ordination and best of all, FUN for everyone.

After folding the paper-craft, color it to build your own papertoy collection. You can also make it with your brothers, sisters or your kids to promote family bonding or simply to impress your friends on how easy it is to make A TOY OUT OF A PIECE OF PAPER.
