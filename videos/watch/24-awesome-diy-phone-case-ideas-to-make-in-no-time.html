HOW TO MAKE A BEAUTIFUL PHONE CASE FROM SCRATCH



To give your phone some color and make it look like something unique that matches your character, I show you how to make a DIY phone case from scratch. They are super easy to recreate, and you can give your phone a very style.

Take a clear phone case, add a piece of greaseproof paper at the bottom, and then cover it up with epoxy resin. Then, add little crafty pieces, such as gel to serve as ice and mini Starbucks coffee mugs to give your phone a coffee design.

You can also add some polymer clay in a syringe and then squirt it out on a clear phone case. It will look like little spaghetti strands. Then add little decorations that resemble herbs and spice on top of the polymer clay to make it resemble noodles.

You can also use some hoe glue to give your phone case a fried egg shape. Then you can take a balloon, add some slime inside and then stick it on the case to make it an anti-stress case.
