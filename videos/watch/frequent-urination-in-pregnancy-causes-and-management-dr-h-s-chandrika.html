One more thing in pregnancy is urinary infection. Because of the pregnancy hormone called as progesterone, there is some amount of stasis in the urinary system because of which there is more chances of urinary infection being more common in pregnant ladies. This she will come to know when she attends to her doctor and when she is tackled properly and one more thing is pregnancy associated increased frequency is due to the increase size of the uterus, which press to the bladder and increases the frequency of urination. This is usually common in the period of 6 or 9 weeks of pregnancy to 16 weeks of pregnancy. Again we come to pregnancy part itself it happens in later part of pregnancy, that is the last trimester of pregnancy that is when they come towards the delivery periods as the delivery period advances, the fetal parts tries to descend down to the abdomen, when it descends down, it tries to press on the urinary bladder.
