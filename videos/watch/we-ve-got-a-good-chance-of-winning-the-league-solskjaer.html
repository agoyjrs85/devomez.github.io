Manchester United manager Ole Gunnar Solskjaer insisted on Tuesday that playmaker Bruno Fernandes is not tired and doesn't require a rest.

Fernandes was brought off late in the 0-0 with Liverpool on Sunday and has registered just one goal involvement in January so far.

But Solskjaer played down any talk that the Portuguese was fatigued.

"He's not tired, no. No chance. He's one of the players who runs and covers the ground in every single game. He's very good at recovery, very good at recharging his batteries," said the United manager.

United travel to Fulham on Tuesday.
