Jeremy Clarkson set the challenge of naming his new Amazon Prime show to the China Exchange audience. During his 60 minutes, the conversation covered Top Gear best bits (remember the terrifying Death Road in Bolivia?), views on the London Mayoral elections and Brexit, the curious role of losing one’s temper in being awarded the Victoria Cross; and more petrol-head talk than a driverless, hydrogen powered car could fit in its boot!

This live, public talk and Q&A, hosted by Sir David Tang, was recorded at China Exchange in Chinatown, London, UK on 3 May 2016.
