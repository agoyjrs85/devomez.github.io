Go outside, and stand in front of a familiar place. Visualize for one minute, holding your ideal scenario in mind of where you see yourself, how you feel and what you sense. Then open your eyes and have conscious vision straight ahead. BUT.. if you notice something eerily different in your environment, then you are shifting lifelines right before your eyes. This feeling is like the Deja-vu effect, which simply means that you were once on the right lifeline, but somewhere lost your way. But now you are back on track.
