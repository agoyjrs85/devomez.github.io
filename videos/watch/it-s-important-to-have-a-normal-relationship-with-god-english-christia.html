"It's Important to Have a Normal Relationship With God" | English Christian Devotional Song

I
Whenever you do anything,
you must check if your motives are right.
If you act in the ways God requires,
then your relationship with God is right.
This is the least God expects.
But if motives are wrong, you must forsake,
you must act on God’s word
and become someone who is right before God,
doing all for God, nothing for yourself.
Whenever you do something,
whenever you say anything,
make sure that your heart is right,
you’re walking in righteousness.
Don’t let your emotions lead,
don’t choose to act upon your will.
These truths are the principles
believers in God follow.
