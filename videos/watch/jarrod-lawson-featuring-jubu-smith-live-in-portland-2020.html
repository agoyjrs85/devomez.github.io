Recorded at Albert Street Pub in Portland, Oregon on 9th March 2020. Joining JARROD LAWSON for Donny Hathaway's ' I love you more than you will ever know' is one of the greatest guitarists in the world: JUBU SMITH. Totally unrehearsed and all the more brilliant as a result. Band members: CORY LIMUACO on drums, and CHRISTOPHER FRIESEN on bass. An unbelievable privilege to witness Jubu close up in a small music room on a Monday night in Portland.
