God doesn’t just call us to stop sinning, but to live a life full of good works. So how do we not only stop doing wrong things, but start doing right things? Now that we have seen how unbelief is the root of sin, we need to see that belief is the root of holiness. In this final session of a four part series, I unpack how belief in the Gospel of Jesus rewires our hearts.
