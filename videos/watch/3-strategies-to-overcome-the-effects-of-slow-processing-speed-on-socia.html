You may ask yourself, “why is my brain so slow at processing information?” Jean Harville, Reading Strategy Coach, discusses what is slow processing speed and how it’s different from the executive function skills. She talks about the causes and how to recognize slow processing speed symptoms in children to better understand how it affects reading comprehension. Jean mentions slow processing speed can affect social skills when keeping up with conversation is difficult.
