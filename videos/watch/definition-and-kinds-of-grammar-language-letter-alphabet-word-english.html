Grammar - grammar is the means by which we learn to read, write and speak any language correctly.

Language - language is the means by which a person share his thoughts and feelings to another person.

Letter - the smallest sound of any language, which can not be divided another part, is called a letter.

Alphabet - A systematic table of letters, is called alphabet.

Word - A meaningful group of letters ,is called word.
