3. Have a goal and go after it -- alphas focus on a goal, objective, or mission and then get sh!t done. Alpha is a huge Joe Rogan fan, and his podcast is next level & cutting edge. 4. Look at the big picture -- alphas can handle delayed gratification and not give in to impulses. You have to be able to push down urges.

6. Uplift others -- alphas encourage others, and they help others by making them feel great about themselves. Alphas aren't threatened by others and can change the world.
