Very little is known to the common people about the reality of the legal system and jurists of our country. It has never been represented in a realistic way in the kind of films which are made concerning the jurists and law. We want this show to be a bridge of communication about the essence and the importance of the legal system and the lives of celebrated jurists such as you so that people can get a close understanding as to the value of jurists and the legal system.

As the title suggests “BEHIND THE BAR”, our objective is to engage in an informal heart to heart conversation embarking upon the journey of the life of our guest and also touching upon certain topics which can be left with the audience as food for thought.

In this episode, we have the humbled opportunity to be in conversation with Soli Sorabjee, Indian jurist & former Attorney General of India
