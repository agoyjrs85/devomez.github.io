In this video, students get a well explained, easy to understand explanation of chapter 5 from Hornbill book for class 11 students of English The Ailing Planet: The Green Movement's Role . The book has been published by NCERT and is a part of class 11 English syllabus under CBSE. The lesson The Ailing Planet: The Green Movement's Role is an article written by Nani Palkhivala (Nanabhoy Palkhivala) who was an eminent jurist and economist. The article was published in the Indian Express newspaper on 24 November 1994. Through this article, the writer highlights the declining health of planet Earth.
