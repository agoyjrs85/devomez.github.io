This is "What to Do When You Are Weak". There is strength in weakness, for when you are weak, God is strong! You must not define yourself by what's going on around you, but instead by what's going on inside of you. It's possible to feel overwhelmed, outnumbered, out-resourced, even though you are anointed and favored by God. In this brand new message, Jentezen Franklin teaches you how to push through your weakest moments to reach your greatest marvels. If you feel down right now, look up—you may be weak, but you are anointed!
