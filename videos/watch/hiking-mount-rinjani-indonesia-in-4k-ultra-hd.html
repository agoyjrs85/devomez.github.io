Mt Rinjani is the second highest volcano in Indonesia (3,726m/12,224ft), part of the Mount Rinjani National Park. It is an active volcano with last eruptions in 2010 and 2015. Enjoy the stunning views of the park during three days trekking to the top.

Day 1 - Senaru village to Crater Rim; Day 2 - Crater Rim - Segara Anak Lake - Rinjani Basecamp at Crater Rim; Day 3 - Basecamp - Mount Rinjani - Sembalun village.

Recorded April 2016 in 4K (Ultra HD) with Sony AX100.
