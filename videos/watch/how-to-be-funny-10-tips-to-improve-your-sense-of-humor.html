Watch this video for tips to improve your sense of humor, because knowing how to be funny can help you in every aspect of your life. If you’re not one of those people who can easily make others laugh, you may have to work a little to develop your sense of humor.

From making connections with your peers to getting your crush to like you, being witty is a good trait to have when socially interacting with others. However, the ability to make people laugh is not something that can be picked up overnight. It’s something that’s cultivated over time, and eventually, should come naturally. It’s not only about being able to make others laugh, but also being able to understand witty jokes yourself.

Regularly practicing these tips to improve your sense of humor will arm you with the wit, sarcasm and everything else you need to be funny, so you can make others laugh WITH you!
