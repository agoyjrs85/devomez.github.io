Recipe (6 inch vanilla cake)
250g unsalted butter
225g caster sugar
4 eggs
100g milk
225g cake flour
2 1/2 tsp baking powder
1/2 tsp salt
1 tsp vanilla extract / essence

Steps:
Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy. Add in eggs one by one, mixing well after each addition. Mix in salt and vanilla. Sift in 1/3 of dry ingredients and mix until JUST combined (no pockets of flour can be seen; please do not overmix). Add in 1/2 of the milk and mix well. Repeat this process, ending with the dry ingredients. Divide batter into three 6 inch pans (330g of batter each). Bake in a preheated oven at 155°C / 311°F for 35-40 min or until toothpick inserted comes out clean. For more detailed steps: https://youtu.be/u9h6RSQyAE8

Recipe (swiss meringue buttercream)
6 egg whites (around 185g of egg whites)
120g sugar
270g unsalted butter
Vanilla to taste

Steps:
Over a double boiler on the lowest heat, constantly stir egg whites and sugar until mixture becomes hot to touch and sugar has dissolved. Whip on high until stiff peaks form. (stir the bottom of the mixture constantly as the mixer might not reach the bottom and it may look like stiff peaks but in actual fact it is still at soft peaks) Then, gradually add in soften butter and mix until buttercream forms. Add in vanilla and mix on the lowest setting for a few min to remove the air bubbles and create a smoother buttercream. Fill each cake layer with 40g of buttercream, and crumb coat the entire cake with more buttercream (just a thin layer will do if not there may not be enough buttercream left for decorating)
If you are worried, you can increase the recipe by one egg white. (7 egg whites (215g), 140g sugar, 315g butter and vanilla to taste)
For more detailed steps: https://youtu.be/DT7bdGqRys4

Divide remaining buttercream into: 240g blue, 45-50g beige and rose red, 10-15g yellow, 60g green, 40g white and a little bit for black and blush colour (around 1 tbsp)

Please use OIL-BASED food colouring. However, if you have to use normal food colouring, when mixing in the colours, let it sit for awhile before mixing it more. The colours will darken slow but gradually so do not keep adding in food colouring.

I used mainly wilton piping tip no. 809 and no.1
