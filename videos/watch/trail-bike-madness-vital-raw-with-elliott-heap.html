Elliott Heap shreds his 14kg Nukeproof Reactor. 14kg (or 30 pounds) with full DH tires and 130mm front and rear travel, originally setup for dual slalom races
We knew the tracks would be tech and gnarly and Elliott being Elliott, loves a good challenge. Some of the tracks were heavily storm-damaged (we didn't realize until we turned up), and were pretty much unrideable. But Ell pointed the 130mm down the hill and somehow made it work. The first day we shot, Ell had a big OTB and sliced his hand open badly, forcing a hospital visit. But we got there in the end. Shot in gloomy North Wales on some of the shortest dankest days of the year.
