Travel with us to explore Tokyo in just two days! This past Christmas, we had a romantic and jam-packed trip featuring animal adventures at some of the city's cutest cafes, where we got to play with miniature pigs and hedgehogs. We also explored Meiji shrine, some of Tokyo's best shopping districts, and ate our weight in good food at Tsukiji fish market.
