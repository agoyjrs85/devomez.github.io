Travel with us to explore Tokyo in just two days! This past Christmas, we had a romantic and jam-packed trip featuring animal adventures at some of the city's cutest cafes, where we got to play with miniature pigs and hedgehogs. This was my last long break of law school, and we wanted to make the most of it! Stay tuned for another video on our snow monkeys tour in Nagano, and one on our trip to Singapore, coming soon. :)
