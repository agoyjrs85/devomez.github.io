We all need a fresh start after 2020! Thankfully, God offers to make things new for us. Today, we’re talking about a fresh start with our family. How can we start fresh when wounds and bad history run deep with those who are supposed to be closest with us? Pastor Kyle shares with us just how God offers us a way to step back from the drama.
