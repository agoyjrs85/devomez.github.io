In The City - bad points about a city and good points about a city. Each point is presented in sentence form and is illustrated for better understanding. KeyWords: traffic, parking, subway, bus, phone booth, signal, taxi, walk, job. In the second part of this video you will practice on your own. This video will help you communicate in English and talk about a city. Have fun and speak English now.

English in use:
There is too much traffic in the city.
It's easy to get lost in the city.
Parking is expensive in the city.
The subway is crowded in the city.
Buses smell bad in the city.
Phone booths don't work in the city.
I can never find my car in the city.
There are too many signals in the city.
However,
I can always get a taxi in the city.
I can easily walk in the city.
Pizza is better in the city.
I can find a good job in the city.
It's easy to get lunch in the city.
There's lots to do in the city.
