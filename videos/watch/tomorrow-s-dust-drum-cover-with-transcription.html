Here is my drum cover of Tomorrow's Dust by the band Tame Impala. The song is track 6 on the album The Slow Rush which was released on the 14th of February 2020. The song was written, recorded and produced by Kevin Parker.

I have attempted to transcribe and perform the drums exactly how they are on the record where humanly possible.

Transcription available here: https://www.roddybailey.com/downloads/tomorrows-dust-drum-chart/
