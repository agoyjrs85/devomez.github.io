Perth again? This is my third improvement of this concert. It is a beautiful concert, with an excellent audience recording, but it can be improved.
So I made changes, more and more effective. I recently developed a new (revolutionary for me) method of EQ/processing and it gave me good results. The problem with single-track files is always the same: if you optimize the bass, you go to modify the treble too - if you modify the guitar, you also go to modify the voices too and so on ...
With this simil-multi-track method, I try to singly modify solo instruments, bass, treble. In this way I try to find the best spatial and tonal balance for any single strument.
This is an experiment, I tried to make this concert even better. I am satisfied, keep in mind that this is not a professional recording, but it is a good amateur one.. To you!
In the song "How Could I Be Such A Fool" you can hear Napoleon having big problems with the microphone.
