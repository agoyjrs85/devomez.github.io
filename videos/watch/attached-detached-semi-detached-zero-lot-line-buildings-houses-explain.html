Attached, Detached, Semi-Detached, and Zero Lot Line Buildings and Houses explained.
The applicability of building and zoning codes vary based on if a buildings designation as a Attached Buildings or Attached House, Detached Building or Detached House, Semi-Detached, or Zero Lot Line. These terms identify the relationship between buildings and property lines. Their definition and meaning is explained by NY architect Jorge Fontan.
