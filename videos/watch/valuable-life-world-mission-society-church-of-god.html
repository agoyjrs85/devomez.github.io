The stones that had value before civilization
have no value in modern societies.
Likewise, there is a great difference in value
between earthly things and heavenly things.

Being God’s Witnesses Is the Most Valuable Life
The value of the Kingdom of Heaven cannot
be measured by the standard of value on this earth.
God wants His children to receive heavenly blessings
by living the most valuable life instead of living
for empty, worthless things on the earth.
So God has told us to be His witnesses
in Samaria and to the ends of the earth.

Therefore, God’s people should testify
about Jerusalem Mother, who gives salvation,
by preaching the Gospel of the Kingdom as Her witnesses.

Daniel 12:3
“Those who are wise will shine like the brightness
of the heavens, and those who lead many
to righteousness, like the stars for ever and ever.”
