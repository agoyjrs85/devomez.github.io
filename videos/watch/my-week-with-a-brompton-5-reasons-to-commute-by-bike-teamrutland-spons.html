Thanks to Rutland Cycling (being part of Team Rutland has it's perks!) they put me in touch with Brompton who loaned me one of their ultra light bikes for a week.

I used the bike to commute for the week and thought I would share with you some of the reasons I cycle to work and the benefits of commuting by bike in the hope that it might inspire you to give it a try too.
