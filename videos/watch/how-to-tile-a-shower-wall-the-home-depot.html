Shower tile installation begins with choosing the right materials for your shower. When tiling a shower wall, you'll need to use materials that do well in the consistently wet environment of showers. Ceramic tile is a great choice because it's tough, easy to clean and stands up well to wetness and moisture. While porcelain has many similar benefits, it's more delicate and fragile.

Add a personalized touch to your home with bathroom tile. It comes in a variety of colors, designs and patterns, allowing you to find a tile that complements new or existing decor.
