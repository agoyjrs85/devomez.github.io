San Francisco 49ers trade rumors are heating up around the QB spot as rumors grow around Jimmy Garoppolo’s future and the 49ers trading for a new starting QB. Matthew Stafford is off the market, but there are several other options like Kirk Cousins, Deshaun Watson and Matt Ryan. Host Thomas Mott ranks his top six QB trade options for San Francisco on today’s 49ers Report.

Deshaun Watson is the ideal trade option for the 49ers this offseason, but could come at too costly a price. Quarterbacks like Sam Darnold and Carson Wentz underwhelmed but still show promise, while Teddy Bridgewater would come at a great price. Matt Ryan and Kirk Cousins, meanwhile, both have ties to Kyle Shanahan and are intriguing 49ers trade targets.
