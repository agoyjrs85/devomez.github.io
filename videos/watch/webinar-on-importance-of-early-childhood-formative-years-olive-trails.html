Hello Parents,

We brought together a panel of expert early childhood education practitioners of Olive Trails preschool for this webinar which covered the following topics -

1. Importance of formative years
2. Brain development in the early years
3. Helping your children manage their emotions & mindfulness techniques
4. Fun things to do during the self-quarantine
5. How to engage better with your children

This webinar conducted on Saturday, 2 May 2020 provided opportunities for parents to join in discussions and deepen their understanding of the unique needs of children's early years.
