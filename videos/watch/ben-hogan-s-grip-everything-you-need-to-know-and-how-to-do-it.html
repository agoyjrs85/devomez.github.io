Ben Hogan's Grip - Everything you need to know about Ben Hogan's grip and how to grip the golf club like Ben Hogan. Ben Hogan had a very unique golf grip that he designed to prevent himself from hooking the golf ball - his nemesis golf shot. He designed a golf grip that would allow the clubface the stay open longer and also help him to hit the golf ball with the wrath of God every single time he swung.
