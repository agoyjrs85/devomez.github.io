WHAT IS THE NEVADA CRIME OF AIDING AND ABETTING?

Aiding and abetting a crime subjects you to the same penalty you would face if you were the principal committing the crime in Nevada. An aider or abettor is treated the same as the principal in Nevada. Mere knowledge of a crime without assistance is not aiding and abetting in Nevada.

Aiding and abetting in Nevada is helping, counseling, or encouraging somebody to commit a crime. For example, a getaway driver is treated the same as the person who committed the crime. Additionally, you can be found accountable for any crime in Nevada that could be a natural and probable consequence of the crime you were aiding an abetting. An example would be an unintended shooting that happens in the commission of robbing a bank.
