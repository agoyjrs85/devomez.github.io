The young and gifted sarod player Abhishek Borkar from Pune was the featured opening artiste on Day 2 of the FEA-curated PAST/PRESENT Festival in Kolkata.

Debjit Patitundi gave Abhishek a superbly subtle, sensitive and attentive accompaniment on the tabla. Both artistes perfectly complimented each other and presented the attending audience with a very mature and fulfilling performance.
