In this video, we’re going to talk about the causes of hair loss. 

When dealing with hair loss, it’s really best to treat the causes of hair loss rather than the symptom, which is the hair loss. 

What causes hair loss?:

1. Hair thinning—The thyroid gland can cause thinning of the hair 
Causes of a thyroid problem:
• Liver problem
• Lack of bile 
• Estrogen spike

2. Receding hairline—An androgen problem 
Causes of an androgen problem
• High levels of insulin
• High levels of cortisol 
• IGF

3. Patches (alopecia)—An autoimmune problem
Cause of this autoimmune problem:
• Overactive adrenal from a stress event
