Grid-Scale Energy Storage.
Countries are actively pursuing reductions in their reliance on #carbon intensive #powergeneration. More than 25% of LAC’s primary #energy is derived from #renewableenergy (RE). But to incorporate variable #renewablesources, grids require system flexibility – a service energy storage can provide. This event facilitated the exchange of these lessons learned from around the world, focusing on how they can support regulatory environments in #LAC.
