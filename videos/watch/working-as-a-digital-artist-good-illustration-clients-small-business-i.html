This week's video is all about small business illustration work. You guys always ask me how to talk to illustration clients, how to manage artwork revisions. So this week I'm taking you guys along with me behind the scenes of a conversation I had with a client, a cacao brand, about final illustration feedback and artwork review.

I will be sharing with you guys why it's so important for me to work with small local brands and tips on how's to what's in part like working as a digital artist.

Conversations like this are really common when working as an illustrator, in this one they share feedback about my artwork, we talk about their cacao, known to increase creativity. Let me know below in the comments what you think and how those reviews are for you.
