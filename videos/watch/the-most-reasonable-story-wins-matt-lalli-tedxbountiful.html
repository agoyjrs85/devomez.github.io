Matt Lalli is a trial lawyer by profession with more than 30 years of experience in the courtroom trying mostly business disputes. By disposition, he is a philosopher, reader, writer, and teacher with a knack for finding profound lessons in the obscure, mundane, and confusing. Though largely an introvert who claims to live most of his life within the walls of his own head, Matt developed a love for public speaking through speech classes in high school and it continues with him today, both professionally and personally. His wife Andra and three children bear the burdens – and occasionally the benefits – of that passion.
