With Isshiki Otsutsuki defeated and Kawaki having the karma seal removed from his body, a lot of the major problems facing hokage Naruto appear to have been solved, with the exception of BoruShiki going on a rampage and destroying Sasuke's rinnegan in the process. While Naruto is in his near death state, a result from his new transformation, and Sasuke is significantly weakened without his rinnegan, a new threat still remains in the shadows, one that some fans have been pointing to for almost a year now. The most dangerous member of Kara, the only fighter we haven't seen in battle, could potentially be Code. With Isshiki now destroyed by Naruto and Kawaki, Code should be easy enough for Naruto and Sasuke to defeat, however, the man who Isshiki/Jigen entrusted with guarding the Ten Tails, which is crucial to the Otsutsuki clan's plans for Earth being harvested, has to be stronger than we're imagining. Could the ultimate trump card be that Code knows a jutsu like potentially the six paths coffin sealing jutsu? Could he become a jinchuuriki for the 10 tails?
