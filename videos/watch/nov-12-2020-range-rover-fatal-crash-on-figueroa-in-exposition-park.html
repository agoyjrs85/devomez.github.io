According to witnesses at the scene, a silver Range Rover Sport was street racing with a 4 door sedan BMW. They were both traveling at high speed well over 100 mph Southbound on Figueroa when the Range Rover lost control and rolled into a tree. The driver died instantly and the passenger was transported in critical condition. The witness said the BMW pulled up at the crash scene and then sped off southbound.
