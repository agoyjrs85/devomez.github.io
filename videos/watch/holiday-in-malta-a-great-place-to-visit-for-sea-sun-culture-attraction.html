WHAT TO DO ON A MALTA HOLIDAY?

FACTS ABOUT MALTA BEFORE YOUR MALTA HOLIDAY
“Malta, officially known as the Republic of Malta, is a Southern European island country consisting of an archipelago in the Mediterranean Sea. Malta lies 80 km south of Italy, 284 km east of Tunisia and 333 km north of Libya.
Malta covers just over 316 km2, with a population of just under 450,000, which makes Malta one of the world's smallest and most densely populated countries. The capital of Malta is Valletta, which at 0.8 km2, is the smallest national capital in the European Union.
Malta has two official languages: Maltese and English.” – WIKIPEDIA
