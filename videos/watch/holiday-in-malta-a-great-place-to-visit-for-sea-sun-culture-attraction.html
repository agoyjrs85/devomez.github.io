WHAT TO DO ON A MALTA HOLIDAY?
Have you a booked a holiday in Malta? And are you wondering about what to do on your upcoming Malta holiday?
If you are going to spend your holiday in Malta you have so many options, but one of the must-see attractions when visiting Malta is the island’s capital of Valletta. Valletta is a historic port town, and if you feel like, you can take a boat trip to Sicily in Italy from Valletta.
If you like old and historic cities like Valletta, you should also visit the city Mdina, which is the islands old capital. If you visit Mdina on your Malta holiday you will visit a historic city with many charming and narrow streets surrounded by old churches and architecture.

FACTS ABOUT MALTA BEFORE YOUR MALTA HOLIDAY
“Malta, officially known as the Republic of Malta, is a Southern European island country consisting of an archipelago in the Mediterranean Sea. Malta lies 80 km south of Italy, 284 km east of Tunisia and 333 km north of Libya.
Malta covers just over 316 km2, with a population of just under 450,000, which makes Malta one of the world's smallest and most densely populated countries. The capital of Malta is Valletta, which at 0.8 km2, is the smallest national capital in the European Union.
Malta has two official languages: Maltese and English.” – WIKIPEDIA
