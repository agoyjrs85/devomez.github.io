Around the millennium you could not listen to the radio or walk into a club without hearing "Do you think you’re - Better off Alone?” The smash hit ‘Better off Alone’ was released on april 3rd 1999. The eurodance / eurotrance anthem has 2 official music videos. The first one that was released is called the UK edit.

Released on the notorious Positiva label in the UK, where it hit #1 in the UK Dance charts and #2 in the UK top 40 instantly. It was the original music video directed by the talented Dutch director Olaf van Gerwen.
The music video ‘Better of Alone’ known worldwide is the version that was shot in Miami. In this video we see the sexy Alice DJ team dancing and singing throughout the video clip.
Check out the video here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lgs9QUtWc3M

In 2013 the evergreen was given a new impuls. Shakira called and shared she was working on her new album. She wanted to record a new version Better off Alone.
No less than one month later; David Guetta on the phone. He just finished recording 'Play Hard' Ne-Yo and Akon and requested permission to include the 'killer hook' from 'Better Off Alone'. Resulting in another worldwide smash for David and renewed attention for Alice DJ.

Alice DJ used to be Alice DeeJay. But with a new face and team, the name was abbreviated and officially became AliceDJ.
