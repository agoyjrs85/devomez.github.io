This video is about a very useful sentences structure which are given below:
* Although
* Though
* Even though
............................................................................................
These words are used to speak the following Hindi situation in to English:

Ex: Hala ki wah moti hai lekin tumse tez daudti hai.
Mana ki wah bahut paise kamata hai lekin wah kanjoos hai.

Rule: [Although/Though + sentence, yet + other sentence]

Ex: Though she is fat, yet she runs faster than you.
Though he earns a lot of money, yet he is miser.
...............................................................................................................
Although and Though both are same and can be used interchangeably .
.....................................................................................................................
Even though
If you want to emphasize on the first part then you can use 'Even though'

Ex:
Even though she is fat, yet she runs faster than you.
................................................................................................................
instead of 'yet' you can also use comma
Ex: Even though she is fat, she runs faster than you.
