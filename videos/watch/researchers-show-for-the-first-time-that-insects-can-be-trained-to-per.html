Researchers at Londons Queen Mary University have shown that bees can be trained to perform complex tasks.

A real bee is shown a task to perform by a fake bee, which it then mimics in order to get a reward. The bee is able to teach other bees to do the same, displaying learning abilities that have never been seen before in insects.
