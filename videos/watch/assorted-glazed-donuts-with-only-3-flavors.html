How to make assorted flavors Glazed Donuts
I made Sugar, Strawberry & Choco Glaze with assorted designs.

*Ingredients
210 ml warm milk
1 egg
2 tbsp sugar
2 tsp yeast
3.5 cups bread flour (350 grams)
1 tsp salt
50 grams butter

*Sugar Glaze
1 cup powdered sugar
3 tbsp milk
1/4 tsp vanilla

*Strawberry Glaze
1 cup powdered sugar
3 tbsp milk
3 tsp strawberry flavoring

*Choco Glaze
30 grams butter
3/4 cup powdered sugar csifted)
1/2 cup cocoa powder (sifted)
6 tbsp milk(it’s 5 tbsp in the video but should be 6, sorry for the typo error)☺️

*Toppings
Sprinkles (can use also nuts, choco flakes/chips, etc.

Prep time: 2.5 hours
Cooking time: 10-15mins.

Yield: 18 pcs donuts (30 grams each, size 6cm diameter)

I shaped the donuts manually, you may used donut clutter if you have.
