Kerala Blasters and FC Goa played out a hard-fought draw in tonight's contest in Bambolim. After both sides created opportunities early on, the Gaurs struck first through Jorge Ortiz's stunning free-kick. However, Kibu Vicuna's side came out all guns blazing in the second half and drew level through Rahul KP's header. Juan Ferrando's side were reduced to 10 after Ivan Gonzalez's sending off but they managed to hold on to grab a point.
