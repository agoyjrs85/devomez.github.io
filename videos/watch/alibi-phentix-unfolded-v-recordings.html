'Redemption' is the brand new EP from Alibi. Since signing with V Recordings in 2015 they've left a trail of beautifully crafted sonics, and this project is no exception.

Both the title track 'Redemption' and 'Unfolded' (with Phentix) hold the unmistakable, tech-driven characteristic you'd normally associate with the São Paulo duo. 'Uneasy' rounds off the release by showing their atmospheric side, previously shown on their 2018 album 'Said & Done'.

This EP continues the trend of Alibi getting better and better with each release.
