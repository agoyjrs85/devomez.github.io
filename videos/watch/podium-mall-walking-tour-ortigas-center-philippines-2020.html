Podium Mall Walking Tour Ortigas Center Philippines 2020

In this video I’ll be visiting the Podium and will give you a walking tour of the mall. Located at the corner of ADB ave and jula vargas street it is nestled in the middle of the Ortigas Center .Philippines.

Operated by the SM Supermalls. It was a joint venture of the Singaporean company Keppel Land and BDO Unibank started back in Dec 2001. The BDO corporate Tower and the Podium West towers are the two buildings directly above it.


During the time of my visit Metro Manila is in the middle of the Pandemic and the city was under General Community Quarantine. You will see all people inside wearing masks and following social distancing protocols. I’ll identify and show you some of them as we go along.
