WHO AM I?YOUTH OF 2020, is a two part documentary that revolves around a self introspection by the youth of 2020. In the documentary, they try explain who they see themselves as and the state of the youth in a constantly evolving yet structured South Africa. They discuss trending patterns and habits amongst their peers and give their personal opinions about why they think what is happening amongst their peers is happening both positive and negative. They also give solutions on how they think all the negatives that the current youth is dealing with can be solved and how all the positives can be improved.

The documentary is also filled with amazing performances by talented South African youth.
-Part two drops in 2021
