Steven and his sister Grace left the Sharer Family house, loaded up their spy supplies onto their boat and set sail onto the sea. On the hunt for treasure, Steven and Grace kept an eye out for this ghost ship. A few miles down the ocean water Steven soon spotted an abandoned ghost shipwreck. This ship is huge and looks super haunted. This is not pretend play pirate ship this is real life. As Stephen and Grace got closer they noticed some very haunted noises and mystery shipwreck pieces.
