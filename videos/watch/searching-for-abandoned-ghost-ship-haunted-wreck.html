WHY IS THE MYSTERY SPY AFTER THE HIDDEN ABANDONED SAFE TREASURE MAP!? Well, Stephen Sharer and the Sharer Family don’t exactly know why but what they do know is the treasure map and the iPhone found while “EXPLORING ABANDONED ISLAND FOR LOST TREASURE” has led Stephen and Grace Sharer to search for an abandoned ghost ship. Steven and his sister Grace left the Sharer Family house, loaded up their spy supplies onto their boat and set sail onto the sea. On the hunt for treasure, Steven and Grace kept an eye out for this ghost ship. A few miles down the ocean water Steven soon spotted an abandoned ghost shipwreck. Standing tall in the shallow waters, is an old rusted metal ship. Was this a pirate ship with hidden treasure or what was this ship used for and why did it wreck and become abandoned? This ship is huge and looks super haunted. This is not pretend play pirate ship this is real life. Stephen drove his boat closer to the shipwreck and as the Sharers got closer, they notice the ship becoming more haunted, super spooky and they wanted to explore more of this abandoned ghost ship! The treasure map they found in the abandoned safe led the Sharers to this location so Stephen and Grace put on their life vests, gathered their spy gear and headed off to explore for any underwater treasure above and below this pirate ghost ship. As Stephen and Grace got closer they noticed some very haunted noises and mystery shipwreck pieces. They were careful when exploring and then all the sudden they saw someone spying on them from a jet ski! It looked like the Mystery Spy!! The same spy that left a mystery note at their front door at the Sharer Family House!! Comment #Haunted if you think this boat is an old pirate ship full of treasure!!
