Greetings extended family! I hope you are all well

I wanted to tell you all about my work experience as a nurse in The Gambia back in 2017.
That was the summer I just finished my nursing education here in Sweden and decided to see what the Gambian health care looks like - and I had an experience I'll never forget!

There were a lot of times during my work experience I just wanted to cry because of the conditions of my people, and seeing that they sometimes didn't have the means to do anything about it due to lack of resources. And to just know what kind of materials were needed but at the same time knowing they were not available broke my heart.

The Gambian health care needs to improve!
