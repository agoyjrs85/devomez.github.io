Such A Time As This written by Clive Jackson

"Such a Time as This was written as a reminder: I am free to be who He made me to be. He is our greatest encourager, believing in us more than we believe in ourselves. Especially in this time of darkness, we cannot be afraid to shine our light. We were made for such a time as this. YOU were made for such a time as this. He said..." - Clive Jackson
