Such A Time As This written by Clive Jackson

"Such a Time as This was written as a reminder: I am free to be who He made me to be. He is our greatest encourager, believing in us more than we believe in ourselves. He sees the true gold that was placed within us, and He chose this moment in time for us to shine. We don't have time to doubt the glory that's been placed within us, but we do have the permission to let it out. The world waits for us to show the piece of Jesus that each individual holds. Especially in this time of darkness, we cannot be afraid to shine our light. We were made for such a time as this. YOU were made for such a time as this. He said..." - Clive Jackson
