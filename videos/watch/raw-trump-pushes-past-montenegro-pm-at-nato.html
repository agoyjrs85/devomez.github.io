STORYLINE:

US President Donald Trump on Thursday appeared to push himself past the prime minister of Montenegro during the NATO summit in Brussels.

The incident happened as the alliance leaders, and Dusko Markovic of soon-to-join Montenegro, were given a tour of NATO's new headquarters.
