Here's what we talked about in the video:

What you'll need to get started virtually wholesaling:

House hunters - CL labor gigs/video/docs
Cold Callers - Indeed
Field Guy - CL labor gigs
Money Phone - Vumber, call rail
CRM - Asana, todoist, podio
Establish relationships with:
Cash buyers
Realtors
Title company
Attorney
Contractor

Ways to find cash buyers:

Propstream http://trial.propstreampro.com/thewholesalecoach/
Motivated seller leads https://www.motivatedsellerleads.org/
Website OnCarrot Investor Websites: http://bit.ly/getoncarrot
Social media
Craigslist
REIA Meetings

What to do next:

Get your leads skip traced
Hire your cold callers
Hire your house hunters
Know your buyer's criteria
Prepare all your marketing material
Have systems in place to convert a lead into a customer

Ways to find motivated sellers:

Text messaging
Cold calling
House hunters
RVM
Have field guy put out some offline marketing (bandit signs, flyers, brochures, biz cards)
JV Deals

How To Negotiate the deal and get to the table

Get pictures of the property
Use the pictures to estimate the repairs
Negotiate the price to wholesale prices
Start soft shopping the property
Get the deal for less than what the buyer wants to pay
Get earnest money
Get assignment contract signed
Send dos to title company
Coordinate deal till closed

Congratulations!
