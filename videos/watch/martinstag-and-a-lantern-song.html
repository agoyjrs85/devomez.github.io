Hi guys, here is some info on how St. Martin's Day (November 11th) is celebrated in Germany, including a little example of a lantern song...
Mini Deutsch dictionary is below!

- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

der Martinstag - St. Martin's day
die Laterne - lantern
der Laternenumzug - lantern procession
das Martinsfeuer - St. Martin's bonfire
der Glühwein - mulled wine
der Weckmann - bread man
die Martinsgans - goose eaten on or around St. Martin's day
die Klöße - dumplings
der Rotkohl - red cabbage
der Laternenstab - lantern rod
der Fasching - carnival season (Mardi Gras)
der Karneval - carnival

- Carnival season is traditionally seen as a chance for people to go wild and let their hair down before Lent starts on Ash Wednesday.

♫ Ich gehe mit meiner Laterne,
und meine Laterne mit mir.
Dort oben leuchten die Sterne,
hier unten leuchten wir.
Laterne, Laterne,
rabimmel, rabammel, rabumm.
Laterne, Laterne,
rabimmel, rabammel, rabumm!

♫ I'm going with my lantern,
and my lantern with me.
Up above shine the stars,
we shine down here.
Lantern, lantern,
rabimmel, rabammel, rabumm!
Lantern, lantern,
rabimmel, rabammel, rabumm!
