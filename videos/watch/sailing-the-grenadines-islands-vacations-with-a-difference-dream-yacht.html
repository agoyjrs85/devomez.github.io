The Grenadines is a special area of the Caribbean, which is best seen from the ocean. We offer by the cabin vacations for Grenadine islands sailing where you do not need any experience.

Book a comfortable double cabin with private bathroom and spacious communal areas. Dine with your fellow guests and enjoy the atmosphere onboard as you adventure together.
