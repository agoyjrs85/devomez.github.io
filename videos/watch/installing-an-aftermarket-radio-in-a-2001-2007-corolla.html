So... car radios get old, and when they do, you need a new one. The logical route would be to buy a new one and put in in yourself, of course!! That's exactly what I'm doing today: Putting an aftermarket Kenwood DPX502BT in a 2006 Toyota Corolla. While there were certainly a lot of hurdles to overcome, we got it done and it is AWESOME!! I hope you guys learn something about how you can do this on your own car and enjoy!!!
