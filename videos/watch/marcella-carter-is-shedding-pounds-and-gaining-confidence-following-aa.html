Marcella Carter was overweight and had a laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy which is one of the three surgical procedures offered at the AAMC Weight Loss and Metabolic Surgery Program. Distinguished bariatric surgeon Dr. Alex Gandsas heads the program team that includes bariatric a nurse coordinator to help patients through the process of scheduling surgery and any appointments required, and a registered dietitian to monitor progress before and after surgery. Marcella thinks so much of Dr. Gandsas and his team that she regularly travels from her home in New Jersey to Anne Arundel Medical Center for her follow-up appoints after having surgery at the hospital.
