As an alternative to the standard AGILITY CONTROL suspension, the DYNAMIC BODY CONTROL steel suspension with variably regulated damping adjustment is optionally available. The shock absorbers are individually controlled at each wheel depending on the driving situation, speed and the condition of the road surface. Three characteristic curves can be selected using the DYNAMIC SELECT switch - see next section. The AIR BODY CONTROL air suspension can be chosen for even more comfort and variability.
