As an alternative to the standard AGILITY CONTROL suspension, the DYNAMIC BODY CONTROL steel suspension with variably regulated damping adjustment is optionally available. Three characteristic curves can be selected using the DYNAMIC SELECT switch - see next section. The AIR BODY CONTROL air suspension can be chosen for even more comfort and variability.
