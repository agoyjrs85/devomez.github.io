The Arctic waters around Svalbard, Norway are under threat. A big part of the seafood industry recently promised to stay out of these pristine waters, but sadly other companies are still sending massive bottom trawlers to plunder its sea life. To halt the destruction of this unique part of the world, Norway must decide to protect it once and for all – before it is too late.
