Lebanon has seen a surge in COVID-19 cases after rules were relaxed, putting the country's health services under immense pressure.

The head of Lebanon's main coronavirus hospital has warned that the healthcare system is close to collapse - with not enough beds, drugs, oxygen, ventilators or staff available.
