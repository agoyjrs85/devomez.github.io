AMAZING DENIM HACKS AND DECORATIONS TO UPGRADE YOUR OLD CLOTHES



All of us have at least three pairs of jeans in our closet, and most of the time we end up wearing just one of them. In this video, I show you some repurposing and recycling hacks that will bring your old jeans back to fashion.

You can take old dark-shaded jeans and place them on a flat surface. Then, take a paintbrush, soak it in bleach and add bleach splatters on the jeans to make it interesting. Then add some fabric dye to it and make it gradient – and voila.

You can also give your jeans a fun pattern using bleach again. Simply dip some cotton pads in bleach and then tap them on different areas of the jeans to make them look like dots.

I also show you how to repurpose old jeans and turn them into an amazing dress.

For the summer, I give you some sewing ideas to recycle old clothes into something more fashionable.
