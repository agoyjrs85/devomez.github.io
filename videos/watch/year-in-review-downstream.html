At the turn of the decade nearly 12 months ago, some said that we were on the verge of entering a new Roaring '20s. How prescient they turned out to be.

Did Boris Johnson's landslide breathe a new lease of life into the Conservative Party? Has coronavirus changed politics for a decade? Downstream hosts Ash Sarkar and Aaron Bastani are joined by James Butler to discuss the year in review, and what comes next.
