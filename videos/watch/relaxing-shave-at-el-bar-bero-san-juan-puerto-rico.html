A full face shave at El Bar Bero in San Juan, Puerto Rico, with barber Jesus Rodriguez.

Jesus is 27-years-old and was born and raised in Puerto Rico 🇵🇷 As a barber he feels it’s important to give his customers a unique experience. He wants people to feel at home as well as “wow them with a dashing traditional or modern haircut.”

Jesus was a professional hip-hop and salsa dancer. Later, his interest to work in the fashion industry led him to barbering. In 2019, he won third place in Puerto Rico's Professional Beauty Show 🙌🏼

This barbershop was opened 5 years ago as an addition to the successful barbershop themed bar. El Bar Bero Owner, Carlos Rivera, explained how the barbershop theme of the bar was in homage to the salon/barbershop that had been operating in the building for over 30 years.
