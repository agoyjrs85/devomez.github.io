I show four recent cross stitch finishes, all my current cross stitch WIPs (Works in Progess), and some old Christmas finishes. I also review the Velky Potoky Mini Stand, talk about pakke kalenders, and project cards. Whew!

@Handworkmaniac on Instagram (Brenda)
adnerbyakj@gmail.com
LNS: The Craft Center of Fine Stitchery in Salt Lake City
My Lapstand is the "Elan" from ArtisanDesign.com and I also use the Velky Potoky Mini from Vpotoky.com.

Haul--Project Cards from SWardDesigns on Etsy.

Finishes:
--LinenandThreads.com, 2020 SAL. I am using 40 ct Alabaster Zweigart Linen and Belle Soie "Blue Lagoon" silk in two different dye lots, and Silk n Colors "Alpine Grasses". 1over 2. And some Dinky Dyes Midnight, and some Glorianna In The Navy.
--ModernFolkEmbroidery.com, 2020 SAL--I am using "Missus Sedas" silk hank (she has an Etsy Store) called "SuperNova". And Belle Soie silk "granny smith" for the center tree section.
--"Gingerbread House" by Mill Hill. A beaded ornament kit.
--"The Gift of Peace" by FriendStitch (FriendStitch retreat collaboration from Bent Creek and Heart in Hand)

WIPs:
--"The New Normal" by Long Dog Samplers. 1 over 2 on 40ct white linen. Sulky 12 wt thread: 1173, 1332, 1188, 1115, 1232, 1047, 0169, 1190, 1229, 1270.
--Pandemic by Long Dog Samplers. 1 strand of Sulky 12 wt cotton on 36 ct off white linen. I am using 3 (or 4) "king spools" of "Cactus" blendable (4086) and 2 petite spools of "Turquoise" solid color (1095). Using one strand of DMC 520 and 3810 for the backstitching. And using Sulky 1271 (dark green) for the border. I ordered my floss from Sulky.com.
--Kringles by Little House Needleworks. 2 over 2 on 32 Ct "Stormy Night", with the called for Overdyed Cotton floss.
--"Huckleberry Farm" by The Blue Flower. On 40 ct Silkweaver "Elegance" that I ran through the washer a few times with Clorox 2 and "Oxiclean White Revive". With overdyed cottons from my stash.
--Lost No More by Dimensions. Kit fabric and threads. Artwork by Greg Olsen.
--"Sky Blue Street" by Sodastitch. On 36 ct white linen, called for DMC. Originally started as 1 over 2. Going to switch to 2 over 2, and go back and stitch with 1 strand over the parts already done with 1 strand.
--"Beachcomber" by Carolyn Manning Designs. On 25 ct off white evenweave, 1 over 1 with the called for DMC floss.
--"Churchyard Christmas" by Praiseworthy Stitches. 2 over 2 on 32 ct "Mirage" linen by Picture this Plus. Using the called for Overdyed cotton flosses, some are GAST and some WDW.
--"Bump N. D'Night Farm" by Praiseworthy Stitches. 1 over 2 on 40 ct Creekstone linen by Silkweaver. Called for overdyed cotton floss.
--"R-Kade" by Long Dog Samplers. 1 over 2 on 40 ct linen Conifer by Picture This Plus. Silks from Silks4U. Fall colors is PR114

Current WIPs I did not show:
--"A Stitcher's Shelf" by Heaven and Earth Designs. Artwork by Aimee Stewart. 1 over 1 on 25 ct Evenweave. I bought the bag printed with the pattern (and the other stitcher's shelf bags, zipper bags, and notebook) at Society6.com.
--Mini Four Seasons by Heaven and Earth Designs. Called for DMC on 25 ct white evenweave, 1 over 1.
--"Peaceable Kingdom" by Catherine Theron. Class piece.
--Disneyland by Neni Designs (etsy), 1 over 2 on 40 ct Dwarf Linen. DMC threads. For the Pattern Keeper compatible version, see note at the top of this page.
