This is a new Ocarina of Time romhack by DezZival. The basic premise is like the Ganon's Castle trials but more complex. You can find the trailer and download links here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zDNQP-ZsQDY
The download from there is a .ppf file that must be patched onto the OoT master quest dubug rom with PPF-O-Matic.

I thought this hack was pretty cool, some parts were a bit annoying but overall it had some really interesting and unique puzzles.
