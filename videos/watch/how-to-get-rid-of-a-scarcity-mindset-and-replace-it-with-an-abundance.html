If you have a scarcity mindset, where you always look for the downside of a situation instead of the upside, you'll never be able to reap disproportionate rewards in life. If you're always trying to protect what you have, you'll miss out on the benefits of investing your time, money, and resources into ventures and situations that can change your life. Most people are stuck in a constant state of scarcity, contraction, and fear, which is why they look out at the world and have such limiting beliefs about upward mobility. Instead, move into abundance and know that you can always gain more in life and that you don't have to hold onto every little attachment you have.
