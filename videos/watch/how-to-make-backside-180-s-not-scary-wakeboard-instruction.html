What's my favorite trick? Hard to really pin that one down but if I had to...I'd go with "Any Backside 180". This trick scares most people to the point that they don't even try it so I've put together some steps that take you through a process of little progressions starting with learning how to Powerslide all the way up to doing a Wake to Wake Backside 180. So there's really a few different how to's within this video that are all fun to learn. So, take your time and learn each step on and don't move onto the next until you feel you've mastered that trick. Enjoy Your Ride!
