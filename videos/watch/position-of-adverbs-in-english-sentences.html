LESSON SUMMARY:

#1 – Do Not Place An Adverb Between A Verb And Its Object.

#2 – There Are Three Normal Positions For Adverbs: front, middle, and end

#3 – The Position Of Adverbs Depends On Their Type. Some Adverbs Can Go In Various Positions.
- adverbs of manner
- adverbs of time and frequency
- adverbs of place
- connecting and commenting adverbs
- adverbs of certainty
- emphasizing adverbs
