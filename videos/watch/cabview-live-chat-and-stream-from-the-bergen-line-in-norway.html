The Bergen Line from Oslo to Bergen consist of:
The Drammen Line (Oslo - Drammen)
The South Line (Drammen - Hokksund)
The Randsfjord Line (Hokksund - Hønefoss)
The Bergen Line (Hønefoss - Voss)
The Voss Line (Voss - Bergen)
