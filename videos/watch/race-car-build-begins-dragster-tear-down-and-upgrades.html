At last! We've began tearing down the dragster for the many upgrades and maintenance items on the list for the 2021 season! This year, the race car will house a 582ci Head Hunter engine with numerous improvements throughout the rest of the car. A new carburetor from Get'M Performance, a Pro-Stage kit from K&R Performance, possibly some new batteries, new axles, adding an alternator, and some wiring projects to accommodate the freshly re-built front half of the chassis! We have A LOT of work to do... 😳
