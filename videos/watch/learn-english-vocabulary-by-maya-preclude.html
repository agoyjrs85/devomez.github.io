Learn English Vocabulary by Maya - Preclude



In this video we will learn about "Preclude".



Pronounce as : [pri-klood]



Meaning :

1&#41; Make impossible, especially beforehand.

2&#41; Keep from happening or arising; make impossible.



Example :

1&#41; Your role in the projects precludes your involvement in the

competitive project.



Synonym :

Close out, Rule out, Forbid, Foreclose, Forestall, Prevent



Antonym :

Facilitate, Promote
