Geosciences and Environment at Lausanne, Switzerland: Nature and Society.

The Faculty of Geosciences and Environment at the University of Lausanne in Switzerland is known for its multi-disciplinary approach to the social and earth sciences. The faculty covers a wide scope of topics including palaeontology, glacier studies, political ecology and climate science and prides itself in active collaborations between researchers within the department as well as with other universities in Switzerland.

The department offers access to cutting edge labs with world-class instruments, generous support of research and a wide range of opportunities for students, including field trips within Switzerland and abroad while studying in a stunning alpine location.
