Alex denies that free will exists. He also denies that homosexuality has anything to do with morality. In order to make that latter claim, however, he has to define the criteria which turn something into an issue related to morality. And this is where things get interesting. @CosmicSkeptic tells us that in order for an act to fall within the moral sphere it has to be consciously chosen. But contrast that with Alex’s case against free will which culminates in the affirmation that “you just can’t choose what it is that you want.” In other words, @CosmicSkeptic's worldview is riddled with an outright self-contradiction. On the one hand, Alex tells us that choice is off the table, given that free will doesn’t exist. On the other hand, Alex resurrects choice in order to establish his notion of the moral sphere. Now, there is more that could be said as @CosmicSkeptic relates free will and choice to an illusion that is beneficial to our evolution as a species. These issues make Alex’s case even more problematic. To find out why, watch this video and let me know what you think.
