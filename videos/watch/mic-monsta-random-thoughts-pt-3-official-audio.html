Random thoughts narrate Mic Monsta's musical journey as he gives himself credits for his efforts in the music industry so far.
After winning Best Hip Hop Artist at the 2020 Muzikol Awards, Mic Monsta releases Random Thoughts PT.3 to announce his 4th studio project "VIBES CLINIC".
The album which already has hit singles like Johnny and Positive Vibes is set to be out before the end of the year.
