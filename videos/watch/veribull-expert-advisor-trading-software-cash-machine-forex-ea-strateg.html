As far as pairs and lot sizes I look for pairs with strong trends and more room to continue trend for Veribull to trade. You want the pairs to be as uncorrelated as possible. The risk management I personally use with this strategy is:

For Conservative Risk
1 pair at .01 per $700 in trading account.
Trade no more than 8 trending pairs if account size allows (an account at $5600 would support 8 pairs) and after that then start increasing lot size at .01 increments one pair at a time per $700 above the $5600 forex account


For Moderate Risk
1 pair at .01 per $500 in trading account.
