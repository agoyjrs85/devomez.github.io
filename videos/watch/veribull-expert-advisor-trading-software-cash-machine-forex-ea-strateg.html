As far as pairs and lot sizes I look for pairs with strong trends and more room to continue trend for Veribull to trade. You want the pairs to be as uncorrelated as possible. Only trade multiple GBP pairs if you are doing the moderate risk or aggressive risk approach. I still wouldn't trade more than 3 GBP Pairs.

The risk management I personally use with this strategy is:

For Conservative Risk
1 pair at .01 per $700 in trading account.
Trade no more than 8 trending pairs if account size allows (an account at $5600 would support 8 pairs) and after that then start increasing lot size at .01 increments one pair at a time per $700 above the $5600 forex account


For Moderate Risk
1 pair at .01 per $500 in trading account.
Trade no more than 10 trending pairs if account size allows (an account at $5000 would support 10 pairs) and after that then start increasing lot size at .01 increments one pair at a time per $500 above the $5000 forex account

For Aggressive Risk
1 pair at .01 per $300 in trading account.
Trade no more than 12 trending pairs if account size allows (an account at $3600 would support 12 pairs) and after that then start increasing the lot size at .01 increments one pair at a time per $300 above the $3600 forex account
