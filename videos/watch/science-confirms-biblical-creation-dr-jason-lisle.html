In this video, astrophysicist and author, Dr. Jason Lisle, presents the facts on three fields of science: genetics, information theory, and geology, which includes radiometric dating & carbon dating. It's entitled "Science Confirms Biblical Creation".

In his presentation he also explains the assumptions made by those who subscribe to the theory of evolution. And exposes a number of faults with the evolutionary argument.

Dr. Lisle is a speaker and author of the following books:

1. The Ultimate Proof of Creation: Resolving the Origins Debate
2. Discerning Truth: Exposing Errors in Evolutionary Arguments
3. The Stargazer’s Guide to the Night Sky
4. Taking Back Astronomy: The Heavens Declare Creation
5. Why Genesis Matters

To learn more about Dr. Jason Lisle: jasonlisle.com
