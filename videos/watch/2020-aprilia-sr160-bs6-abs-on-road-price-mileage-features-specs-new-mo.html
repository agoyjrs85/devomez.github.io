2020 Aprilia SR160 ABS BS6 variant is here. New Aprilia SR150 BS6 ABS is now replaced by the Aprilia SR160 ABS. Aprilia SR160 BS6 gets all new engine but everything else remains identical. Halogen headlamp and tail lamp, big alloy wheels, dual tone seat, is all same.
