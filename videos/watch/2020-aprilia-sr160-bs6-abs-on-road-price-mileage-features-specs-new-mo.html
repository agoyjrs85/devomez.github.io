2020 Aprilia SR160 ABS BS6 variant is here. New Aprilia SR150 BS6 ABS is now replaced by the Aprilia SR160 ABS. This is not just rebadging, Aprilia actually increased the displacement of the engine to cop up the possible losses of BS6 conversion. So, the New Aprilia SR150 is Aprilia SR160! Aprilia SR160 BS6 gets all new engine but everything else remains identical. Halogen headlamp and tail lamp, big alloy wheels, dual tone seat, is all same. Instrument cluster gets updated with analogue-digital unit with red backlit which looks sporty. Aprilia SR160 2020 model is still for college going students who need only fun out of the scooter. Practicality is not there. Underseat storage is also not that great.
