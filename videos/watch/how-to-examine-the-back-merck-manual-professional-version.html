How to examine the back, including the following:
• Inspection (eg, for kyphosis and scoliosis)
• Palpation (eg, of the pelvic brim, vertebral column, sacrum, ileum, sacroiliac joints, ileolumbar ligament)
• Selected maneuvers (eg, straight leg raising, sciatic nerve stretching, concealed straight leg raising)
