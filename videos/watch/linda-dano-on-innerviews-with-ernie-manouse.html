Linda Dano is one of the biggest stars of the soap opera world -- at one time appearing on four different soaps as the same character. She is also the much talked about host of the lifetime TV show Attitudes, which inspired a well recognized spoof on Saturday Night Live. But these days she spends a great deal of her time educating people about clinical depression -- which she was diagnosed with after the loss of both her husband and her mother in less than two weeks time. On this episode of "InnerVIEWS with Ernie Manouse," our conversation with soap opera star and public speaker, Linda Dano.
