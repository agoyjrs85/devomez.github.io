On this 1980 episode of Play the Percentages with Geoff Edwards, the Smets are back with $300 in their pockets, and time had been called during their bonus round in which they had 42 points out of a possible 100. When they finish, they meet the Burns in a new game. If either side's intuition pays off correctly, they could win $36,000. Can the Smets continue that championship reign, or will the Burns pull a win with a good percentage?
