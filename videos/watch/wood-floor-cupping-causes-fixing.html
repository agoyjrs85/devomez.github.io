Wood floor cupping can be avoided or fixed by following a few simple steps. If your hardwood floors have a wavy appearance, they are likely cupping due to wet mopping or an underlying moisture problem. The key to fixing a cupped floor is to first quickly identify and rectify the moisture source. If cupping persists, you may have an unusual moisture source. A flooring inspector certified by the NWFA should be able to find hard to identify moisture sources.
