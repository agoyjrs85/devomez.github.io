The 3-bedroom, 3 ½ bath townhome at 3053 N Lakewood isn't your typical townhome.

For starters, it's owned in fee simple, unlike townhomes with condo associations.

The home is unusually wide and boasts south, east and west exposures for maximum light throughout the day. The home has multiple outdoor spaces, including a deck, a private bluestone patio and a roof deck adjacent to a top-floor office.

Among the home's many recent upgrades are a sleek kitchen with high-end appliances and a spectacular master bath. The master suite has volume ceilings, great closet space and walls of west- and south-facing windows.

The lower level of the home has a family room, a guest bedroom and bath, and a large laundry / storage room.

The home is priced in the mid-$600s.
