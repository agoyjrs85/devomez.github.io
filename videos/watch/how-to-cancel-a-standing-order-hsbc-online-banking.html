This easy-to-follow tutorial will show you how to cancel a Standing Order via HSBC UK Online Banking, in 7 steps.

Step 1: Log on to your HSBC Online Banking
Step 2: Select ‘Manage’
Step 3: Select ‘Standing Orders and future payments’
Step 4: To cancel select ‘Show details’
Step 5: Select ‘edit details’
Step 6: Select ‘delete payment’
Step 7: Confirm cancellation

Learn more at https://www.hsbc.co.uk/ways-to-bank/mobile/
