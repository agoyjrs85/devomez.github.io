What is reproductive system ?????

Reproductive system/genital system,  is a system  of sex organ, within an organism which work together, for the purpose of sexual reproduction of organisms.


female reproductive system, is made up of the internal, & external sex organs that function in reproduction of new offsprings.


Female reproductive system;

Female’s internal reproductive system are vagina, & uterus, & fallopian tubes, & cervix, & ovaries.

External structures of female reproductive system included mons pubis, & labia majora, & minora, vulva, & clitoris.

Mammary glands are not the female external genitalia, at the time of birth both Male and female have mammary glands but in females it develops during the period of puberty due to the hormonal changes.

The female reproductive system contains two main parts: the uterus, which hosts the developing fetus, produces vaginal and uterine secretions, and passes the anatomically male sperm through to the fallopian tubes; and the ovaries, which produce the anatomically female egg cells.


Labia majora;

It is also known as the outer lips and, it is the folds of skin on either side of the opening of the vagina, & it is covered with hair, in most adult women.

Labia minora;

It is the small folds of skin and lacking fatty tissue, it extend backward on each side of the opening of vagina, it present inside the labia majora.

Clitoris;
It is the point here labia majora meet, certain ways, clitoris  can be compared with the male penis, it is extremely sensitive to being touch for the sexual stimuls.
