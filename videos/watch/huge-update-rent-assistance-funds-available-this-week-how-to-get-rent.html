Rent assistance is available for those people who need help paying rent. The cancel rent movement may not result in anything, but it doesn't matter if you want free rent. The rent assistance stimulus program allows you to get free rent money for past due rent or utilities.

It is estimated that 14 million people are behind on rent to the tune of $70B in rent arrears owed. But there is $25B for the new rent relief rent assistance program.

WHEN will the rent assistance money be available? JANUARY 26 is when STATES get the money from the Treasury for rent relief. But it may be a few more weeks for your city to get rent assistance updated into their rent assistance application.

You can use the rent assistance to use for your past due rent, rent currently owed, utilities owed, or utilities past due.

The rent assistance is for up to 12 months past.

WHO IS ELIGIBLE TO RECEIVE THE RENT ASSISTANCE?
Renter assistance recipients must meet the following criteria:
(1) Qualified for unemployment benefits OR experienced a reduction in household income, incurred significant costs, or experiences other financial hardship due (directly or indirectly) to the pandemic (must ATTEST in writing) AND
(2) to get rent assistance must demonstrate risk of EXPERIENCING HOMELESSNESS OR HOUSING INSTABILITY, which includes:
- Getting an eviction notice
- Rent due notice
- Past due utility notice
- Unsafe or unhealthy living conditions
- Other evidence
(3) must have a household income of 80% or less of AMI based on EITHER total income for 2020, OR monthly income NOW at the time of application
Agencies must also determine if the funding is duplicative of other government funding, i.e. other sources
Cities will rectify you every three months by checking but the rent assistance does not count as income.

WARNING: they will prioritize those with incomes below 50% of AMI OR those UNEMPLOYED FOR 90 DAYS.

Where to apply for rent assistance? Google "rent assistance" in your city. You will see a list of the city resources for rent assistance. The websites may take a while to update in the coming weeks with new rent assistance.

The rent assistance will pay DIRECTLY to your landlord or utility provider, unless they do not want to deal, then they will pay to you.
