This is an incredibly important podcast. Because nothing brings out our wounding like relationships. Sometimes from our childhood, sometimes from previous or even current partners. In order to become free from the cycle of repeating these traumatic experiences, we must first become aware of what is happening in the subconscious. Where is the trauma coming from, and what is the pattern that it creates? Once we have this awareness we can then start to apply the tools and techniques necessary to begin the healing process. I’ve never seen anyone do it better than master coaches Christine Hassler and Stefanos Sifandos, who put Vylana Marcus and I through some of their programming with the Fit For Service tribe in Sedona. We were absolutely blown away, and had to get them on the show to talk about it. We also talk about the principles of Sacred Union, and as a bonus Christine and Stef included a free gift on the topic for all the listeners here | https://christinehassler.com/sacredunion/
