Randy Santel "Atlas" vs Banh Mi Boys' Massive "Big Boy" Vietnamese Banh Mi Sandwich Challenge w/ Pho Wings and loaded Beef Bulgogi Fries at Banh Bi Boys in Metairie, Louisiana!! After attempting the "meaty" SmokeHouse BBQ Pitmaster Challenge at Pyre Provisions in Covington, Louisiana, I traveled south down to Metairie, Louisiana which is just outside of New Orleans, LA. I was the first person to attempt and help kick off the new "Big Boy" Vietnamese Banh Mi Sandwich Challenge at Banh Mi Boys which also included some delicious dry-rubbed Pho Wings and loaded Beef Bulgogi Fries. I had 1 hour to finish four over-stuffed Vietnamese banh mi sandwiches, each filled with 1.5x the normal sized portion of flavorful meat - grilled lemongrass pork, beef brisket, grilled steak & egg, and then grilled shrimp which had an extra flavorful sauce on it. The challenge included 10 dry-rubbed Pho Chicken Wings plus a mega-sized order of loaded Beef Bulgogi Fries. I was trying to win my $100 Vietnamese food meal free and the first spot on Banh Mi Boys' Wall of Fame. Thanks to Peter and everyone with Banh Mi Boys for the delicious food, drinks, and hospitality!! Thanks also to everybody who came to watch and meet me that October evening!! I appreciate everyone in the Metairie, Louisiana area and throughout the state of Louisiana who supports our efforts!!

Banh Mi Boys' "Big Boy" Massive Banh Mi Sandwich Challenge in Metairie, Louisiana ($100):

- Banh Mi Boys has been serving up delicious Vietnamese banh mi sandwiches and other foods in the Metairie, Louisiana for many years now. They offer delicious Vietnamese po-boy sandwiches as well!!
- You must finish 4 of their banh mi sandwiches, and each has 1.5x the meat of a normal sandwich.
- The four sandwiches are grilled lemongrass pork, beef brisket, steak & eggs, and grilled shrimp.
- For an appetizer, the challenge includes 10 dry-rubbed pho wings which are a delicious must try!!
- There is also a massive portion of loaded Beef Bulgogi Fries which are totally amazing as well!!
- There is a 1 hour time limit to finish everything. Winners get the $100 meal free and Wall of Fame!!
