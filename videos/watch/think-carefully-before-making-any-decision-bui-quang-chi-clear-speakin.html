T7 years ago, I had a unforgettable memory.
I challenged my limits with a bold decision.
I walked from Vinh city to Ho Chi Minh city in 45 days.
The journey stretched across the central provinces of Vietnam.
The length of road is 1424 km.
That time was summer. It were very hot and dry. In some sections, the asphalt melted because of hot weather
Walking for many days caused my feet to swell due to blisters
When it broke, it made my feet very hurt.
Severe weather and my foot paint made me extremely despondent.
I wanted to give up.
At that moment, I asked myself.
"Why did I start this trip ?"
"Is this a wrong decision?"
The answer was no.
I started because I wanted to overcome my limit.
I wanted to train myself to be consistent with the goals set.
I wanted to prove to my parents that I can take care of myself.
After that, everything got better.
After 45 days, I went to HCM city safe and sound.
I lost 15 kg.
My skin got black because of sunburn.


Ater a rest period, my health has gradually improved . I felt good and happy.
At the end of the trip, I learnt 3 lessons
First, think carefully before making decision, after making decision we shouldn't regret.
Second, keep going, you will arrive.
Third, our limit is no limitation. If we really want, we can do it.
My name Quang Chí, I'm an environmental engineering.
Thanks for your listening.
