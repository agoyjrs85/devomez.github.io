This is my review of Little Book 2 "Noe Simple Task from Freddie Noe, son of Jim Beam Master Distiller Fred Noe. This second release is a unique blend of an 8 year Kentucky straight rye, 13 year Canadian rye, and 40 year Canadian 100% corn whisky. The blend is bottled uncut and unfiltered at 118.8 proof, and is available nationwide at an MSRP of $100. Hope you enjoy the review and thanks for watching the Mash and Drum whiskey review channel!
